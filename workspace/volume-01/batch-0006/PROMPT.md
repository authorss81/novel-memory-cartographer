## Hard constraints — these are not suggestions

1. **Chapters 51–60 are *The Bell and the Blank*, and `outline/volume-01.md` fixes its content.** **Orrin's floodgate test date is announced together with an altered Line 7 timetable that removes the remaining informal crossings, and the pressure redirected after the Chapter 39 seamquake finally concentrates at the river-core interchange. It does not become a second seamquake. It becomes a pinned seam: a seam held open instead of sealed, because the order keeps feeding pressure into it, and it will not close while the order stands, which is worse for the people who live beside it than a collapse would be.** All of that happens. The order is not suspended until Chapters 58–60.

2. **The pinned-seam condition is named here and only here.** The phrase does not appear in Chapters 1–50 and **must not be retro-fitted into them**. It is a **condition, not a new remnant type, grammar, or failure state**: a seam held open, not sealed; its pressure travels toward whatever the timetables, gates, and records point it at; **it comes loose only when the order is suspended and the routes around it are re-cut**, which is exactly what Chapters 58–60 do. **A pinned seam is quiet, not healed, and a pinned seam may produce a full seamquake first and then stop producing new ones, which is what makes it harder to live beside than a collapse.**

3. **The four-day window closes, the test runs, and the regrade does not finish on time.** The soft lens **stops on the first of next month, which is Monday the first**, and it has not stopped yet when this batch opens. The last reach can only be laid, levelled, proved and certified between **the second and the fifth**. **The floodgate test is the sixth.** Danner is back on full rate for eleven days, his review is reset, and his certificate on the second-stage variation says in his own hand that **the certification is made in the fourteenth day of a standard deviation in the readings at the trench and that he will not be able to say in a room that he stood behind it.** He certifies it anyway, because the level has to be certified by a man and the only man is him. **The level is not back in Milo's file and may not be put back.** The joint finding of the twentieth of October is in the file and its second paragraph is about Danner, not about Orrin's order.

4. **The test drives the western culvert back out to full flow, and it is the same structure Chapter 7 wound down to a maintenance slit.** It is **not a first opening**, it is **not sealed** at any point in Volume 1, and **the culvert ends the volume gated, silted, and re-aligned — materially changed, not restored.** The widening of that gate is what lets the pinned seam widen.

5. **The altered Line 7 timetable removes the remaining informal crossings, and it is announced with the test date, not before.** The diversion is already permanent; the Ferry Road turn has been a signal since the first of the month with *no staff turn* in the box; **now the eleven seconds go the other way and the count has nowhere to be handed at all.** Barrow is the man who can minute it and who will minute what it is, and he will minute that a temporary arrangement is not a reason to vary an order, in advance, in a file, and it will be very difficult to unwrite.

6. **The residents split over whether to cross at all, and the split is real and nobody is argued out of it.** Some residents cross to Cedar Court, some return to a supervised section of Bellwether, and **one chooses a memorial route rather than a home.** The three answers from Batch 0005 — **a neighbourhood, a memorial route, a safer connection between existing homes** — are on the page in three mouths and **none of them was chosen**, and the resolution must not choose for them either. Lena Marr organises a supervised return and **is not the centre of it by the end**.

7. **Owen Fitch refuses any route that would give him an inherited part, and that refusal is a real refusal and is not a wound to be healed.** His name is on **five copies** of the turn sheet in a space left empty on purpose, with *third floor* and nothing beside it, and **to take a name off a document somebody has to say the document was never theirs.** The batch may not heal him, may not thank him, and may not have anybody explain his position to him.

8. **Pell's mark holds for hours, not for days, and this is the batch in which that limit is tested.** The Streetkeepers and the Streetkeeping practice **can hold a return mark for a working shift and cannot hold one overnight, and they may not be asked to.** Pell's stamina, his local knowledge, and his eye for a watched route are what make the witness work possible, and **he is not Milo's magical assistant and has no power of any kind.** Pell has still not seen Tern, and the list is not a finding and Ostrow is not a source who confirms anything.
