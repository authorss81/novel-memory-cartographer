## Hard constraints — these are not suggestions

1. **Chapters 41–50 are *The Provisional Map*, and `outline/volume-01.md` fixes its content.** Milo, Mara, Pell, Iona and the residents expand the one-shift inspection route into **a durable provisional return route with several witnesses and a deliberate blank on paper.** It is a larger and more dangerous project than the limited route of Chapters 1–10: it must survive a timetable cycle, protect a neighbourhood-scale set of choices, and **remain revisable by people who were not present at the first crossing.** The method succeeds **only** when people are allowed to revise the route and choose where they go. **A false witness and a copied map create a second dangerous route**, and that is where the cost of trying to restore Bellwether too quickly is exposed. The team learns that **Orrin's project is both an engineering necessity and a political instrument.** The residents decide **whether they want a neighbourhood, a memorial route, or a safer connection between existing homes.** All of that happens. **None of it is resolved by Milo Rook, and the choice is not made for them.**

2. **The batch is a working project with failures in it, not another argument about forms.** `state/current.md` records that standing instruction after Batch 0004, and it is the governing note for pressure. Lead with people doing a thing on a date and let the procedure arrive late, as Batch 0004 did. **At least two of the ten chapters must turn on something going wrong that nobody foresaw**, and the failures must be practical: a person who cannot get there, a sheet that is wrong in an ordinary way, a door that is shut, a person who says no and is right.

3. **The four-Thursday instrument runs four times in this batch and expires.** The minute's terms are fixed: **one named person — Lena Marr — once a week, in daylight, on the last Thursday of each of the next four weeks: the twenty-third of this month, the thirtieth, the sixth of next month and the thirteenth. She may stop at any point and the stopping needs no reason. Nothing is recorded as a household route. The works' consent is given by the site manager. It expires at noon on Friday the fourteenth of next month and cannot be extended by anybody including the chair, and there is no form for extending it.** **Write all four uses, on those four dates, with the twenty-third of this month the day after the Bench and the fourth on the thirteenth.** **It may not be extended, renewed, minuted again, converted, or turned into the new route by paperwork, and it restores nothing and protects nobody.** The district has nothing for the fifteenth, and that is the batch's clock.

4. **Milo cannot build the thing and may not be left alone with a document about a person.** Iona Sen's fifth finding is live, unwritten, and was broken again on the tenth of the month. **The provisional map is therefore built by other people, on paper somebody else holds, and Milo's part is arithmetic, labour, versions and refusal.** He may refuse to be its author. He may not become its custodian, its clerk, or the person who holds the only pen. **Do not cure him and do not let him off.**

5. **The deliberate blank is on the page and is a design decision, not an accident.** There is a space left in it on purpose. **Somebody who was not there at the first crossing — the fourth of Sazur — must be able to fill it or change it in this batch, and must do so at least once.** Nobody may fill it for them and nobody may describe the blank as a loss, a mystery, or a thing to be completed. **The written line home in Milo's own Field Book is intact throughout this batch.** Its loss is Chapters 51–53 and **may not be booked, hinted at, or found by a character.**

6. **The three answers must all be on the page and none may be chosen for them.** A neighbourhood, a memorial route, a safer connection between existing homes. They are not synonyms and the people who hold them are not the same people. **Owen Fitch may refuse a role that would make him an inherited part**, and that refusal is a real refusal and is not a wound to be healed. **Lena Marr is organising a thing and is not the centre of it by the end.**

7. **Ada Fenn's withdrawal is load-bearing and is not a problem to be solved.** She does not appear in this batch either. **She is not to be named, thanked, replaced, argued back, or put on anything, and no neighbour quietly becomes the door-check.** The volume guardrail requires the district to build a **second and third source of witnessing** — the rota was the first — so this batch builds a **written schedule with a blank** and a **door-check somebody else can perform as a written turn rather than as a habit**, and the difference between a habit and a written turn is the whole argument. **Nobody goes up three flights of stairs to ask her.** Ada may be referred to as a woman of sixty-eight, and by nobody in particular, and not as a solution.

8. **The false witness and the copied map are the batch's cost, and they are local.** Somebody in this district puts a name to a piece of paper for a household, and a copy of something the district made gets carried somewhere it should not, **and a second route appears that nobody authorised and that people walk because it is on paper.** It must cost somebody something specific and it must not be a magical artefact. **Keep it small, ordinary and administrative: a return, a schedule, a drawing copied out of an older book, a sheet of typing paper.** It is the same *kind* of thing as the copy register's Crown Heights consultation reference, and **it is the next volume's question planted once and not answered.** It does not touch the Quieting, the First Atlas, or the One Survey.

9. **Orrin Sable's project is both an engineering necessity and a political instrument, and the batch must show both without resolving it.** The soft lens, the regrade, the sixth of November, a lead lateral from 1904, three pumps on a street he is not responsible for, and a man on half rate who certified a level he cannot stand behind. **He is not made wrong, not softened, not suspended, and not a final antagonist — that is Chapters 58–60.** He is a pressured local official with a real safety basis and a Prefecture's standing instruction behind him. **If he moves, let it move by costing somebody something.**

10. **The order stands. The plate stays. The name is not restored.** A four-inch steel plate is on the only door in the flood-control wall and it is lawful and it is there to stay. **Do not remove it, unseal it, reopen the door, or call it anything else.** Nobody may obtain a current address for Cedar Court in this batch: the name must stay on the compensation schedule and off the street index, and **the address/money trap is still live, still unsolved, and still payable only in Chapters 58–60 by costing somebody money. No clerk may solve it.** The **nine school places** and the spring intake are still on the page, **two of the nine are already lost because Lena Marr put her name on a Registry instrument**, and no third may be quietly regained.

11. **The count of people and the count of rooms is a civic bill, not a scene.** Two hundred and thirty-one on the eighth of Sazur, **two hundred and forty-nine in fifty-four rooms in eleven streets**, eighteen short, thirty-one rooms on no list at all, two and four a head a week, five hundred and fifty-four pounds and forty, forty-three pounds and two. **A rate is built on a property and a person in a room is not a property. The two numbers will never be the same number and a rate cannot be built on a door.** **No batch closes this administratively.** The Civic Recovery officer is a person who could be dismissed over that number and may be put under more pressure, not less.

12. **Atlas residue stays rare.** At most one appearance in ten chapters and one is not required. Batch 0004 used one, in Chapter 34, in six words. The Field Book was otherwise silent four times in that batch — the fourth, the sixth, the thirtieth of Sazur and the nineteenth — and Milo wrote the silences down as two dated pairs. **He may add to that record. He may not read anything into it, and the next batch may not collapse the pairs.**

13. **The orientation blank is eight named remembered routes and may grow.** Later batches **may add to the list and may not remove from it.** All eight are recoverable when marked and all are marked by other people. **Every new loss must be a real place somebody else can mark, and the return, if it comes, comes from somebody who is not Milo.**

14. **Crossings, if there is one.** Stage 1 held; no advance and no new cost. Six crossings stand: the fourth, sixth, eighteenth, twenty-first and twenty-sixth of Sazur and the tenth of the month at 14.06. Chapters 1–10's two are not inflated and may not be. **This batch may add at most one crossing**, and if it does it must be named, dated, placed, recorded in his own hand, and the count read aloud in a yard with Iona Sen's tally present, and all four of the signed conditions satisfied, and **it must cost a route and buy nothing.** A batch about a working project does not need a crossing, and the seam does not need to be visited for the project to be hard.

15. **The triad accounting is prose and is not re-explained.** Two supports withdrawn — the public name and the ordinary routes — and the witness support broken by scattering, with **one routine left standing and overloaded**. Chapter 32 used it once, in Orrin's mouth, about the rota, and he was right. **A later batch may not restate it as if it were news, may not write it as three withdrawn, and may not write it as a thin remnant that later grew.** The thin remnant at the western culvert is a separate, smaller thing.

16. **The answer to the bells is fixed and is not re-litigated.** The first two calls moved people toward the old service court; the third is a check, a person saying a space is clear before the next turn. **It may be quoted, used, put in a document, or doubted by a competent official. It may not be reopened, hedged, or re-decided.** Nobody in this batch is to decide it again.

17. **The clue chain is carried, not resolved, and is not Soren.** Two documents carry a pair of marks, not three. **Three references exist and are references and not pairs**: the Crown Heights repository consultation reference against *p.9 withdrawn*, the one on the copy register, and the one on the Filewell index. 18. **Attachment 9, the copied route, and Pell's card are carried, not resolved.** Attachment 9 is a dated, unplaceable real name. Pell's card is *Ostrow asked me who keeps a road that is not on the map. I said the people who live on it. He said that is what the list says.* **Pell comes back in this batch and he has still not seen Tern.** The list is not a finding, Ostrow is not a source who confirms anything, and nobody resolves either.

19. **The withdrawn page, the ledger number, the roll, and the burial are three or four separate threads and stay separate.** The death record is still closed. **Lena Marr has still not been back, still does not know what question to ask, and the woman of fifty-four who mends other people's clothes is alive, unnamed on the page, and cannot be told where he is buried. Reopening the death record is Chapters 58–60. Do not reopen it here, do not find the grave, and do not let a clerk be made to be the villain who hands it over.**

20. **Tomas Rusk's withdrawal is closed and paid.** He did not take the account back; the reason is a wage, four years of a pension and a knee, and nobody argues him out of it. **The October position is three sworn accounts and one unsworn.** His mechanic's sentence may be asked for in this batch and may be given **true and unsigned**. **The condition he attaches to the account itself is Chapters 51–53 and is not this batch's to write.**

21. **Tarin Kest is gone and does not come back as an ally.** He went to a desk in Crown Heights on the twenty-second with a form in his coat pocket carrying *no person available* in the person-familiar box, in his own name, in his own hand. **A letter, a refusal, a query from another division, or silence are all permitted. A return to the district, a change of heart in a scene, and a friendship are not.** The **Filewell precedent** stands as a precedent: a variation requires a person familiar with the route and there is not one. **The *no person available* phrase has now been on three instruments and may not become a solution.**

22. **Jo Lask's suspension runs to the twentieth of this month.** He is on the shed at Alder Quay and is not suspended from the platform. **He refused a turn in Chapter 34 and was right, and the method change worked once.** He may be asked again and may refuse again for the same reason. **The interval guarantee from the tram authority, in exchange for keeping the extra stop off the public timetable, is Chapters 51–53 and is not to be extracted in this batch.**

23. **Pell's mark holds for hours and not for days, and that limit is not yet tested.** The Streetkeepers can hold a return mark for a working shift. **They cannot hold one overnight and may not be asked to.** Pell's stamina, his local knowledge, and his eye for a watched route are what make witness work possible, and **he is not Milo's magical assistant and has no power of any kind.**

24. **No new grammar, faction, antagonist, or final enemy.** **Sel Iri and Halden Vey are not for this batch.** Halden may be named in a document or in a superior's standing instruction, which is already on the page, and neither appears in person. **Milo does not stabilise Bellwether alone; every workable passage is held by named people doing named labour.** **The culvert is not sealed in Volume 1 and the twenty-second of Sazur is not a first opening.** Old Mabb is not woken, not written down, and **his name is not said out loud**; no Registry clerk is to be the villain who notices him.

25. **The pinned-seam condition is not named and is not due before Chapter 51.** The floodgate test date is on the record and **the announcement of it, together with an altered Line 7 timetable that removes the remaining informal crossings, is Chapters 51–53.** This batch may bring the works' own deadline of **the sixth of November** nearer and may let people say the word *November* in a yard. **It may not run the test, alter the timetable, or stage the climax.** The floodgate climax is Chapters 54–57.

26. **Milo must not have a clean victory and must not be cured.** His flaw — compulsive authorship under fear — is intact and worse, because the new project is exactly the thing he wants to own. **At least one chapter must show him refusing to be the author of something he could have built, in public, and being right, and at least one must show him failing at something he was good at.** No kiss, no declaration, no jealousy scene, no rescue. Work, disclosure, disagreement in front of danger, and repair are the vocabulary.

27. **Atlas residue, the Field Book, the Lark token, and the written line.** The token is cold for the sixth time and **may stay cold**. It may not open anything, summon anybody, or replace a witness, and **Milo does not use it as a private key.** The Field Book is fallible paper and not an oracle.

## Beat map for Chapters 41–50

Use this as a spine and vary the pressure. **Batch 0004 led with action and the working site. This one is a project, so it should lead with work and people and let the paperwork arrive late and wrong.**

- **41 — the first Thursday.** The day after the Bench. Lena Marr stands at a door once, in daylight, with the works' consent on the record, and the thing asks her for clear and she says it, or she does not say it, and it goes the way it goes. **The project is named out loud in a yard and nobody agrees what it is.** Practical and character.
- **42 — the timetable and the turn.** The provisional route has to survive a timetable cycle, which means the tram authority, a diversion, and a count handed to a person. **Jo Lask is asked for something and the asking is the chapter.** Political and practical.
- **43 — the count that does not close.** The money, the rooms, the officer under the return. Somebody wants the number to be a rate and it is not a rate. Consequence and civic pressure.
- **44 — the copied sheet.** Somebody copies something the district made and carries it where it should not go, and a second route appears on paper that nobody authorised. Investigation, and it must be a document, not a feeling.
- **45 — the false witness.** Somebody in this district puts a name to a piece of paper for a household that has not agreed to it, and the second route is walked by somebody who believed it. **This is the batch's cost and it lands on a person who is not Milo.** Cost.
- **46 — the three answers.** Neighbourhood, memorial route, safer connection. A yard, a school, a kitchen table, a washing line. Nobody is persuaded and nobody is promised. Political and character, and the batch's heart.
- **47 — the low streets and the works.** Orrin's project as engineering necessity and as political instrument, in the same scene, both true. The second gang, the soft lens, a trench, a form. Action and political pressure.
- **48 — the blank, and who fills it.** The written schedule goes up with a space left in it on purpose, and a person who was not there on the fourth of Sazur fills it or refuses to and does something else instead. Investigation and relationship, in that order.
- **49 — Mara's review and the method change under a project.** Her own-conduct review **sits on the third of next month.** The disclosure discipline now has other people inside it: she cannot file for him, and the project is full of people who did not sign anything. Relationship, in public, without warmth.
- **50 — the fourth Thursday, the expiry, and the new problem.** The thirteenth, the fourteenth at noon, the fifteenth with nothing on it, and the thing that arrives instead. **End on a decision or a threat, not a resolution** — and the new problem must be the one Chapters 51–60 is built on.
