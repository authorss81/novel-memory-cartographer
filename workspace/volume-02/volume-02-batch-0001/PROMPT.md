## Hard constraints — these are not suggestions

1. **The batch opens on the disappearance and it is a civic fact before it is a mystery.** A commuter tram fails to appear on the morning run; **every live map in the city is already correct without it**, and the maps were updated by somebody, and that somebody's authority is the question. **Nobody may find the passengers' names in a map.** The names are held by a manifest, a conductor, a depot clerk and thirty-one people who each know their own, and **the map is the thing that is wrong.**

2. **Volume 1's rule is the volume's method, and it is stated once and used: an Atlas route is maintained by people and documents, so a copied maintenance map can move or erase people as effectively as a continuity order.** That sentence is the bridge. **It must arrive as somebody's practical conclusion from work, not as a theme, and not as Milo's.**

3. **The disappearance is not a monster and not a room.** `outline/series.md` fixes the reversal and it is the batch's hinge: **the tram is not trapped in a static space; it is travelling through a route that needs a witness to remain distinct from the present.** Therefore **the batch cannot solve it by finding a room and closing a door, and no one may hold it still by force.** The thing that keeps a route distinct is a person saying a space is clear before the next turn, and **Volume 1 already spent that instrument; it is not a new power here, it is the same one, in a worse place, with thirty-one people in it.**

4. **Milo's first persistent orientation blank is established in this batch, and it is established by a crossing, and it costs a route.** `bible/power-system.md` is the authority on the order of costs and it is not negotiable: **orientation blank at Stage 1, failing sensory detail at Stage 2, the first personal blank at Stage 3, the seam-scar at Stage 4, the record blank at Stage 5, the authorial blank at Stage 6.** **Nothing belonging to Stage 3 or later may appear in these ten chapters, not even as an unexplained symptom.** In Volume 1 the written line home went blank on the twenty-fourth of November and was **still recoverable when somebody marked it**; **in this batch it stops being recoverable inside the volume, and that change is the batch's cost.** The remembered-route list stands at **ten, the original eight intact, none removed**, and later batches **may add and may not remove**.

5. **Mara Quill writes his return route. That is milestone 2 and it is a professional act, not a romantic one.** She does it because she is the person who has to be there and because nobody else can, and **she is not going to do it kindly, and it is not going to be tender, and he does not ask and she does not offer and then she does it anyway because a person was going to walk into a moving route.** **Nothing romantic happens beyond that and nothing is going to.** The milestone's second half is binding in the same breath: **trust begins, and so does her knowledge of his private motive**, and the Lark Street grievance is not resolved, softened, or explained away, and **she now knows something about him that she cannot un-know and does not use.**

6. **Mara's first unauthorised copy of a Registry record happens in this batch and it costs her.** She lost preferred access on the fourth of December, she carries a two-year note, her appeal in one line is not stayed, and **the copy she takes is of a document she has no current right to see.** She does it knowingly and on purpose, in the same register as the carbon: *a document in a file is a thing somebody can produce.* **The hearing on the Bellwether order has still not been set and this batch may not set it**, and the investigation's reporting officer must still be a person other than any person named in the Prefect's letter or in the order.
