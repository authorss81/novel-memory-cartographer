# Open Threads

## Central mystery

Why do forcibly erased streets persist as physical remnant streets, and why does the Common Atlas suppress their pressure while making Greywake more likely? The planned answer is the Quieting, the First Atlas’s unreleased Singular Mode, the Registry’s use of continuity orders, and Soren Rook’s One Survey. Partial erasures are deliberately *not* the answer: cutting one or two of the triad — name, route, witness — leaves a ruin, a false address, or a thin unstable seam.

### MOVED

## Status after Chapters 51–60 — what closed, what moved, what is now more dangerous

Batch 0006 is *The Bell and the Blank*, and **it closes Volume 01.** A date was announced twenty-one days early, four people set four conditions, a page went out of a surveyor's own book, a culvert came out to full flow, a seam would not close, four hundred people answered a check, a hundred and forty feet of frontage was given a name it had lost six years ago, an order was suspended, a dead man got a cause and a wife and a plot number, and a woman of about thirty-four signed her own name to a levy in a hall in front of thirty people. The detail is in `state/batch-summaries.md` and `state/continuity.md`; this is the short list.

### CLOSED OR ANSWERED IN THIS BATCH

- **The floodgate test has run, and the western culvert is at full flow.** The sixth of December, the gate out at 06.20 and back to twenty-four inches at 14.06, the wall held, the clay held, and **the reach went out of true eleven inches over forty feet at the third call while about four hundred people answered a check meant for one man at a door.** **The culvert was never sealed and is not sealed now; it is gated with a stop-screw and a padlock, silted to a third of its diameter, and re-aligned, and it is not the culvert that was there before the order.**
- **The condition is named and it is a condition.** *Pinned* — a seam held open instead of sealed, fed by a live order, not closing while the order stands, quiet rather than healed. **It comes loose only when the order is suspended and the routes around it are re-cut, which is what the twelfth of December did, and nothing in the volume says that out loud before it happens.**
- **The order is suspended, opened for investigation, and its author is stood down** — and the letter says in terms that the suspension is not a finding, and that the classification of the eleventh was made correctly on the day it was made, and that the re-cutting of every timetable, gate, notice, classification, drain and record is what is required. **Orrin Sable got nine sentences out and stopped in the middle of the tenth, and one of the nine matters, and nobody asked him.**
- **The worker's death record is open.** Cause, next of kin, and a plot number, filled from a card index in about four seconds by a clerk who noticed nothing. **The widow of fifty-four has been told where her husband is buried, by Lena Marr, in about forty minutes, and was not asked a question and was not given an apology.** She has waited six years and has never had a bill.
- **The address/money trap is paid, and it is paid by costing somebody money.** **Lena Marr is the named rateable head of a temporary accommodation household, at two and four a head a week, in advance, for as long as that building is a building, and the compensation is untouched and the name is off the street index, and no clerk solved it and no clerk noticed the man on the third floor.**
- **The count bill is closed by money, not by administration.** Two hundred and fifty-two certified, the twenty-one short recovered, **and no room in this district is on a rate book today because of anything that happened today.**
- **Seven of the nine school places come back**, on a Continuity referral, in Advocate Quill's hand, naming the same woman who is now paying the levy. **The two lost in October are still two and she said so twice, unasked.**
- **Tomas Rusk's condition is paid and it is not his oath.** It is a name — **Bridge Reach** — and it is now in the district's drainage book with a date, on the works maintenance schedule with a gang and a fortnightly inspection, and on a tram diversion panel, and his own name is in the reopened order file with no oath on it, and it cannot be taken back.
- **Jo Lask's interval guarantee is extracted and the count is a count again**, in a printed rule book, in exchange for keeping the name off the public timetable — and it is his name in the book, and it has ended his own inquiry, and he will not be on that reach again.
- **The four conditions of the crossing, all four, both times**, and both counts read aloud in a yard with Dr Sen's tally, which now has eight lines on it.
- **The three answers are not chosen and are not going to be.** A street, a way to walk, and a road with flowers on it are all still held by three different people, and **what happened instead is that twelve of twenty-six people used the road and eleven did not, and one woman walked it and would not go in, and the district has no idea what it wants and is not the worse for it.**

### MOVED

- **The second stage of the orientation blank has arrived and is on the page: the written line home, gone clean out of the book, with the paper and the ink on either side intact, and it is not fading.** It is recoverable when marked, and it is marked by a nurse of twenty-three, by a woman of sixty who would not go on a list to do it, and by a boy of seventeen with chalk at four miles an hour. **The list of remembered routes is ten and none of the eight has gone.** He has asked two people to write the route for him and been refused twice by people with no reason to refuse him, **and the line is still blank, and he cannot tell whether it is a cost, a bad memory, or a page that needs another person's mark, and has written all three down in that order and decided none of them.**

- **Ada Fenn is on nothing, was asked for once, and was not asked for.** A man in a yard said *go and ask the old woman on the third floor*, and Rue said no first and Lena Marr gave the reason: **she is not ours.** Nobody went up three flights of stairs. **She is referred to once in ten chapters, as a woman of sixty-eight, by somebody who is not Milo Rook, in a sentence that says she was not there.**
