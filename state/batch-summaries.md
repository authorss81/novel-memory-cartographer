### What the batch is, and what it is not

### The ten beats the volume outline fixed for this block, and where they landed

1. **The central pressure: a commuter tram disappears from every live map during the morning run, taking thirty-one passengers with it.** Chapters 61, 68, 69. **It is not late; every live map is correct; the maps were amended on Monday the thirteenth of October and the amendment is printed, dated and attributed to a standing instruction with a reference number, and nobody had to want anything.**
2. **Major movement: the search leads through old maintenance routes, Thread Hall, and a Keepsake remnant that preserves the habits of a vanished market; Milo's first stable field map and Mara's first unauthorised copy of a Registry record become necessary.** Chapters 62 (the maintenance route and the copy of the gang's paper), 63 (Thread Hall, and a refusal that is correct), 66 (Saltmarket Row, Keepsake, and the first stable field map, which is a map of who does what and on which day), 65 (the unauthorised custody-register page).
3. **Midpoint reversal: the tram is not trapped in a static room; it is travelling through a route that needs a witness to remain distinct from the present.** Chapter 67, in Jo Lask's mouth and then in Nessa Bray's, from both ends, and the reversal is a **route in motion, not a place** — *nothing is holding it out of here, it is being out of here, and the thing keeping it out is the same thing keeping the coffee smell on that Row.*
4. **Climax: Milo and Pell move the passengers through a moving Cadence route while Mara keeps a legal return path open; the team recovers the tram and loses an Atlas record that cannot be recreated.** Chapters 68, 69, 70. **Pell holds the count with a counter and no words for thirty-five minutes and has no power of any kind; Mara's return path is a card with three hands on it, and the lost record is a line on a sheet about a drain in a rack, because a route in the Atlas is a name and a name needs a rateable place.**
5. **Concrete resolution: the passengers return; the city learns that the Atlas depends on clerks, witnesses and maintenance, not only on the Index; Milo's first persistent orientation blank is established; it is not yet the deeper personal blank of a witness-led restoration.** Chapters 69, 70. **The blank is now the thirty-fifth day and three marks on it in three hands have not brought it back, and he has written one sentence in the imperative in the whole book.** The Atlas-depends-on-clerks finding is not a speech: it is **a gang foreman who will not write a word about a person on a form, a works clerk who was asked for a schedule *for index* on a Wednesday and sent it, a man of sixty-three in a basement who was right twice, a woman of fifty-four on a stair with a list of what is on a shelf, a conductor who says the next one out loud four hundred times a day, and a second tin on a bottom shelf in a plan room.**
6. **Relationship milestone 2 — a crossing leaves Milo unable to navigate home and Mara becomes the person who writes his return route; trust begins, and so does her knowledge of his private motive.** Chapter 67 for the route, Chapter 65 for the knowledge, and the two are **not** connected: she writes the card on the Sunday night and she has had the custody page since the Friday, and he never finds out in this batch, and she never uses it. **No kiss, no declaration, no jealousy scene, no rescue. Nothing romantic happens and nothing is going to.**
7. **No new grammar, faction, antagonist or final enemy.** The grammars in this batch are **Cadence** (a sequence with a conductor in it) and **Keepsake** (a market that is not there), both canonical, and **Thread Hall appears as a reading room and a keeper, not as a faction or a prize.** Sel Iri does not appear. **Halden Vey does not appear and is not named — his name is on a letter of the eleventh of December in Volume 01 and Volume 02 does not repeat it.** The villain is **a box and a filing number**, and the authority is **a standing instruction with a reference number**, and the tram's disappearance is attributable to a method and not to a new face.
8. **Volume 1's rule is the volume's method, and it is arrived at from underneath by somebody doing a job.** Chapter 62: *A road is not a line. A road is eleven men and a hundred yards of drain and a sheet of paper a foreman carries out of a hut…* and the two halves of the same sentence, nine hundred yards apart, from **a works clerk of forty-six at a desk on a quay** and **a man of sixty-three at an index desk in Crown Heights**, neither of them Milo, neither of them able to say they are talking about the same thing. **Milo writes the join down once in his own book and refuses to make it in a yard.**
9. **The clue chain is carried, not joined.** The copied tick in a fourth column, the copied turn sheet with a contractor's head on it, and the carbon relocation schedule **appear nowhere in Chapters 61–70 as a found document and are joined to nothing.** A new copy is found — the gang's own paper, a routing slip with *for index* on it — **and Milo refuses to put it in a column with anything, twice, and the reason is the same bad reason it has been since November: a column is a case, and he has made one twice in eleven weeks and both times it made itself into a case.** **Two documents still carry a pair of marks and not three; the new carbon carries one and is not a third pair; Kest has not matched the strings; Soren Rook is not named in Chapters 1–70.**
10. **The cost curve.** Stage 1 held on the ledger; **Stage 2 begins and does not complete.** Orientation blank: **the ninth crossing did not cost a route and he does not know why, and the list stands at ten, the original eight intact, none removed, all ten marked by somebody who is not him.** Failing sensory detail: **it begins, permitted from here and not before, and it is two things — the pitch of the third gong of a Line 3 morning car, which he needed, and the taste of the tea he made at five in the morning on the twenty-third, which he did not — and no face, no place, no person, no name.** **The first personal blank does not appear, and neither does a seam-scar, a record blank or an authorial blank.** The Lark token **stays cold**, on eight of thirteen days, and is not used.

### What was not touched

**Ada Fenn does not appear in Chapters 61–70 and her name is not written once in them.** She is referred to three times, all of them in the established Volume 1 idiom and never by name: a works clerk and Milo in Chapter 62, Mara Quill's district inventory and Milo's Field Book in Chapters 65 and 66, all of them **a woman of sixty-eight**, and all of them inside the standing prohibition. **Owen Fitch does not appear in Chapters 61–70 and his name is not written once in them; his name is still on five copies of a sheet of paper with a contractor's head on it and has not been taken off any of them, and that is stated in the district's inventory in Chapter 65 and nowhere else.** **Old Mabb is not named, not written down, and not woken.** **Orrin Sable, Whelan, Rennick, Danner, Petts, Tarin Kest, Nell Crale, Verrick Assal, Ola Petrossian and Barrow appear once each or not at all**, and none of them is asked to do anything that reopens a Volume 1 question. **The order is suspended and not discharged, the name is restored only as a contested community and memorial name, the culvert is gated and silted and re-aligned and is not the culvert that was there before the order, and the seam is quiet and not healed — and nothing in Chapters 61–70 touches any of it except to say, once, in a flat inventory, that none of it has moved.**

### What was won, and what it cost

**Won, and real:** thirty-one passengers, a conductor of thirty-eight and a driver of fifty-one alive and on a shed road; **the ninth crossing, walked by a gang because a foreman read a manifest and understood a blank line at the bottom of it**; **the *Alighted* column filled in by the only person who was there**, which is the one column in the city that could be; a card with three hands on it that gets a man up his own stairs; a second tin on a bottom shelf in a plan room, in a building with a man in it who has not signed a deletion in about two months; and the knowledge, arrived at by three working people in three rooms, that **an Atlas route is maintained by people and documents, and that a copy of a maintenance map can move or erase people as effectively as a continuity order, and that nobody has to want anything.**

**Cost, and not curable:** **the passage cannot be recorded and therefore cannot be proved, and in about four months a department may say that no passage occurred, and the district will have no instrument;** a brass token in a custody column with a surname and no address and nothing signed for it; a foreman who will be asked in about four months by a man in an office whether he witnessed thirty-one persons, and will have to say yes; a second-stage cost that is now **a sound and a taste and possibly nothing else**, and he does not know; a nine-line blank that is the thirty-fifth day and has three marks on it; **and a woman of thirty-one with a two-year note, an appeal not stayed, no preferred access, a custody register page in a drawer in a flat with a coat in it, and a piece of paper on a man in a railway coat that she is not going to use.**
