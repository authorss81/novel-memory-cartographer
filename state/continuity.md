## World and timeline

- Alderquay is a river-delta city built on reclaimed marsh and governed through the Continuance Charter.
- The Long Rain struck the Alder Fault forty-one years before the opening. During the emergency response, crews built the First Atlas, the original design of the Common Atlas.
- The First Atlas was **plural by design**: several named routes, custody distributed among district crews, and deliberate gaps left wherever two accounts of a place could not be reconciled. Its gaps were meant to stay open.
- The crews added an emergency setting, the **Singular Mode**, which forced one conflicting account to become the Index’s version and suspended the others. It was logged as temporary.
- The Quieting occurred because the Singular Mode was still engaged after the flooding began to recede and its forced output was then treated as the record. Nine districts ceased to be reachable, and tens of thousands were displaced or lost.
- The Registry of Continuance was formed afterward, with a founding rule of no single authority over a route and no gap closed without a hearing. It reversed both clauses, rebuilt the Singular Mode as a standard instrument, and began using emergency closures for land, identity, and political control.
- Lark Street was erased eighteen years before the opening. Soren Rook approved a necessary route severing to stop an immediate seamquake, then used the emergency to erase claims and suppress inquiry. Neris Rook died during the evacuation. Her route trace remains distributed among survivors and is not a recoverable person.

## Magic rules

- A **full remnant** forms when a place is forcibly cut off from all three supports of the **triad: name, route, witness**. These three words are canonical; do not substitute body, address, or witness community when describing an erasure.
- **Partial erasure:** one support withdrawn leaves a ruin, a disputed address, or a rumor. Two supports withdrawn leave a **thin remnant** — unstable, offering at most one service, unable to maintain its own witness, and the usual site of a small seamquake. All three withdrawn leaves a full remnant with a cue, a service, a demand, and a failure. A half-completed erasure, with one support left carrying pressure it was never built for, can produce a full remnant more coherent and more dangerous than a cleanly erased one.
- Unless a passage says “thin remnant,” *remnant* means the full kind.
- Remnants occupy seams and follow a dominant memory grammar: Cadence, Keepsake, Kinship, Mercy, Ledger, Weather, or Threshold. There are no other categories.
- The seven grammars are a vocabulary, not a solution. Every street combines a dominant grammar, a leaking secondary grammar, its own disguised cue, its own demand, and its own failure condition, so two streets of the same grammar can require opposite handling.
- Habit memory is repeated human use, not a perfect recording of the past. A rumor without a lived route or shared practice is not magical.
- A viable map uses a threshold, a meaningful turn, a witness, and a return route. Durable restoration also needs a blank for current life.
- A map can trace, guide, stabilize, restore, or hold a route. It cannot read minds, resurrect the dead, force consent, or prove one history is the only truth.
- Fixed restoration without a blank creates a claimed place and binds the custodian to inherited roles.
- Atlas residue may offer one short Field Book prompt. It is incomplete and never solves the moral or political choice.

## Ending constraints

- Soren initiates the One Survey, which is the Singular Mode run deliberately and citywide. It briefly suppresses seamquakes by forcing singular agreement, then draws excluded pressure through the Common Index and causes the terminal Greywake.
- Milo refuses both sole authorship and a fixed, claimed restoration of Lark Street.
- The coalition maintains a distributed Atlas. Selected places return as living, revisable districts; unstable places remain marked hazards or memorials.
- Soren releases the Index and dies. Neris is not resurrected. Milo loses a fixed autobiographical coordinate, reliable official location, and the remembered sound of Neris’s voice.
- The larger extent of the Margin remains intentionally open, but no new cosmic antagonist may be introduced.
- The final image leaves a walkable blank, not a perfect map.

24. **Mara Quill's return route, which is milestone 2, and it is a working instrument.** Sunday the twenty-first at about twenty past eleven, in a porch, in the rain, in about forty minutes, **on a sheet of accountancy paper in biro with a heading, a date, an *out* and a *back*.** He does not ask and she does not offer. 27. **The loss is a document and not a person, and the batch says it twice, in two mouths, in that order.** **Mara Quill, first: *Nobody died.*** Thirty-one passengers, a conductor of thirty-eight and a driver of fifty-one, and every one of them alive, and the conductor went to bed in her own bed at ten past three. **And Hettie Brann, second: *a document is the only thing in this city that bites*** — *I have watched this city eat four people in six years over a document, and it was a piece of paper in a drawer… there is not one now… in about four years that rack will be cleared, because racks are cleared, and when it is cleared the thing will not have happened. **And it will be done again.
