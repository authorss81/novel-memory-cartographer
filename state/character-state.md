## Milo Rook

- **Where he is:** Alderquay, out of the Hall of Wards at ten past five on the twenty-first of the month, twenty-three days from the expiry of a four-Thursday instrument that protects nobody.
- **Stage:** 1 — trace-sight. Unchanged and not advanced. He is not going to write a grammar, and the batch's finding is that the thing the district needs next is not a map and is the one thing he is under a standing finding not to be left alone with.
- **Crossings: six, all in Volume 1, and he reads all six out loud in a yard on the tenth of the month with Iona Sen's tally present.** The 4th and 6th of Sazur, the 18th, the 21st, the 26th, and **the 10th of this month, in and out at 14.06 from the ground-floor door of Cedar Court at the coal hatch side, on a count of nine called from the path head by Rue and walked in and out by Verrick Assal, who did not have to stop it.** Chapters 1–10's two are not inflated.
- **Cost paid so far: the orientation blank, first stage only, and it is now eight named remembered routes.** The eighth, added on the tenth, is **the two hundred metres from the works gate to the end of the disused platform.** It came back within a day, in chalk, on a gate post, put there by the gate-man on his own initiative after Rue told him what the surveyor was going to be like, and the gate-man said *it is not for you, it is for the next man.* **Later batches may add to the list and may not remove from it.** The Field Book is intact, the written line home is complete, correct and legible to anybody asked to walk it, and the written-line loss remains a Chapters 51–53 event.
- **The Iona Sen finding, which is not a condition and cannot be signed: he is not to be left alone with a piece of paper about a person.** He has still never written it down anywhere. He broke it again on the tenth of the month by going into a court with the accountancy-paper rota's state in his head, and Iona Sen told him so in a yard of nineteen and refused to say it in front of them and refused to let it go.
- **What he gave away and what it cost.** On the twenty-first of the month the **schedule level he certified under paragraph four of the amendment comes out of his file** and goes into the works' file under a foreman's signature, at Mara Quill's insistence, without his consent. He asked for it. It is the only unambiguous thing he had in the file and he gave it away because a file that one man can discredit is a witness, not a case. **Danner said yes on the Thursday in six words — *Plate fixed. Six twenty-five. My plate.* — and the price is that Danner will be asked to stand behind a level over a trench with four feet in it at a Bench in October, and has said he will not be able to, and said it while standing, and did not ask for it to be written down.**
- **What he was told in writing and could not answer: the contamination question.** Tarin Kest asked him, on a ramp, in front of nine people, on the fourth of the month, whether his figures were contaminated by a private grievance. The answer is the Lark Street disclosure, which is in no file in this city and is going to stay that way. He could not answer aloud because the promise of the twenty-ninth of Sazur means nothing bearing on the file goes to Mara Quill in writing whether she can use it or not; he could not answer in writing for the same reason. **Tarin's judgement of that answer is on the page and is not wrong about the risk of it.**
- **His position in the October file, as it now stands:** the level is in it and is a document; his interpretation of the level is excluded; the joint finding of the twentieth is in it and names him as the certifying officer of a level and nothing else; the four accounts are in it, three sworn and one unsworn; and the Registry's own classification of the site is in it, correct, and unexcluded.
- **The seamquake is partly his.** The joint finding's second paragraph says a surface was made level across a place which had been two surfaces for six years. **Orrin Sable is in that finding once, in a list of persons who were present.** Milo wrote it down as a fact and not as a grievance and has told Mara Quill he is not going to be in a room with her on the third of October and that he has known it since the fourteenth of Sazur.
- **The bill is now in front of him as a job he cannot do.** A durable provisional return route with several witnesses and a deliberate blank, revisable by people who were not there at the first crossing, must exist by the fifteenth of next month to replace four Thursdays. He cannot build it, and the reason is the fifth finding and not a legal one, and the first person anybody in the district will think of is on the fifth floor of a converted pump works and has not come down since the third.
- **The Lark token is cold for the sixth time** and produced nothing. It was in his pocket in the dark on a step in a side street a hundred and fifty yards from a bridge on the night of the nineteenth and he did not take it out.

## Mara Quill

- **Where she is:** the river core, under a review opened on her own hand that **sits on the third of next month**, and the advocate of record on a file with a hearing in October.
- **What she won on the twenty-first, and its exact size.** One instrument out of four asked for: **one named person, once a week, in daylight, on the last Thursday of each of the next four weeks — the 23rd of this month, the 30th, the 6th and the 13th of next month — expiring at noon on Friday the fourteenth of next month, extendable by nobody including the chair of the Bench, with no form for extension.** The order is not suspended, the public name is not restored, no variation of the order is made, and the minute says in terms that it protects nobody and restores nothing. **The named person is Lena Marr, who has not been named by Mara because she has not been asked her.**
- **What she refused and why, in her own words, on the fourteenth.** She drafted an application to strike the surveyor's record from the file on Milo's own application, and told him she had drafted it. Not because she thinks he is contaminated: because in seven days she had to put something in front of a Bench and she was looking for a way not to have to be the one who defends him in a room. **She then refused to do what he asked** — to take the measurement out and leave the accounts — because a file that one man can discredit is a witness and not a case, and because a Bench can do nothing with an account nobody can check.
- **The other way, and what it cost.** She did not file the Lark disclosure and did not ask for it and is not going to. She took Milo's name off his own level and put it under a foreman's signature, and told him first and did not ask him, and when he caught her designing a rescue for him she went away and asked Danner herself, on the Thursday, and Danner said yes in six words.
- **The second set of initials in the intake routing slip's annex box is still unresolved and still hers alone.** She has read it four times and has not read it aloud to anybody.
- **Her rates authority case, undecided**, on the per-head additional schedule of two and four a head a week calculated on two hundred and thirty-one people, which the district's own count of the sixteenth and seventeenth puts at two hundred and forty-nine and which thirty-one rooms on no list at all sit outside of entirely.

## Tarin Kest

- **Where he is:** the district file store, the works office, a corridor of the Hall of Wards, and a yard at Cedar Court he was not invited to. **He goes back to a desk in Crown Heights on the twenty-second** and is not coming back to the district.
- **What he is:** twenty-eight, Survey Enforcement field inspector, nine days in the division when he arrived on the fourth of the month, auditing **the maintenance of the continuity order and not the order**, because he can recommend suspending an order and cannot yet do it honestly. He wants to prove the Registry can correct itself before somebody decides to dismantle it.
- **What he is right about, and it is on the page:** the long sheet filed as false means the city holds the only description of that route's use on a confession; the classification's fourth finding will decide October without anybody choosing to; a *believed* rota is not a witness and neither is a *made* route; and the diversion takes eleven seconds out of the count.
- **What he is wrong about, and the batch shows it without correcting him:** that the surveyor's record is the thing that can be excluded and that four accounts are a case. He is told, in a yard, by a district officer, that two divisions of his own department have said two different things about the same street in the same week and he has to write both in the same paragraph.
- **His paragraph, written on the fourteenth and not changed afterwards:** the surveyor's record is excluded from October **as to interpretation, construction and cause**; measurements of level, fall, settlement and aperture are admitted as measurements, unverified, on the surveyor's own signature; and the account of a coordinated evacuation interrupted by the order is not in issue on the file, he has not tested it, and he does not accept that it establishes a hazard. **He says in Chapter 37 that he is not Milo's adversary and that he told him on a Saturday before doing it because he thought he would want to know, and that the kind of officer that is worse for Milo than the other kind.**
- **What he did at the end, and what it cost him:** he lodged a variation application **in his own name, in his own hand, in a corridor in front of nine people**, on the Filewell precedent, and the clerk completed it with *no person available* in the person-familiar box, and he said thank you, and put it in the inside pocket of his coat instead of a folder. **He knows what it is to put your name on a document about somebody else's district and he has spent nine days watching Milo Rook do it.**
- **His flaw, on the page:** he believes a well-placed signature solves a political problem. He is nine days in and he has a form with his name at the top of it and an entry in a box that says nobody exists, and he is entirely correct that this is a document and a document is what a department can be made to do something with, and he has no idea what to do next.
- **What he is not:** not a friend, not an ally, not a mage, not an administrator. He has not met Mara Quill's second set of initials and does not know about them. He has not noticed the man asleep on the third floor and did not pursue it when Milo's file gave him the phrase. He has said he is going to spend four months on a desk in Crown Heights thinking about a plate of accountancy paper with a promise at the foot of it and has asked Milo never to mention it.

## Nell Crale

- **Who she is:** sixty-three, Bridge Street, low end, four rooms over a shop with a cellar under it, twenty-two years keeping a cold store and a delivery of milk and ice in two baskets on a hand barrow. **She is the only cold store within four streets, because the low streets' own machines went in the closure and were replaced by nothing, including a sentence.**
- **What she lost on the twelfth of the month:** the cellar, which took four inches of standing water by two o'clock on a Sunday in a month with nine-tenths of an inch of rain, with nine hundredweight of ice in it and four families' worth of milk on a Tuesday and a Friday. **The works' answer is November and the district's answer is a lead lateral from 1904.**
- **What she said to Milo Rook:** *you'll write it and nothing will come of it* — and she was right, and she told him to come back and tell her nothing came of it, because her mother's boy died of nothing coming of it and she would rather be told by the same man twice than by nobody. She is not a device, she is not the face of anything, and nobody has put her in a yard.

## Danner

- **Where he is:** off the alignment, the trench and the plate for the duration of a review, on half rate, since ten past six on the morning of the sixteenth of the month, after nine weeks in which he read the drainage truth into the only box on a district form that would take it and then applied for the plate that guarantees the box is a lie.
- **What he did this batch and each of it cost something:** filled in the *alternative considered* box by hand so that nobody could put a word in the space; read the soft lens's behaviour to Tarin Kest and predicted two further feet of settlement across the reach; told Orrin Sable to his face that his plate date and his variation are not the same document; said the low streets flood in November again and said the district has a man in a plate and a river behind it; **stood down from a return for writing one true word on a form.** His price is still unexcused: the regrade is stopped two days out of eleven weeks, a soft lens is taking a bite out of every pass, and the district flood line is at nine inches on a street that was at six on the sixteenth.

## Lena Marr

- **What she put her name to on the twenty-first of the month, in her own words, read out in a room by another woman:** a door, once a week, for four weeks, on a Registry instrument, with the price stated by her first. **Two of the nine children are not coming back to a school in the spring because the mother of two of them is a named person on a paper in a building in Crown Heights.** She put that in the minute herself and she has not been thanked and will refuse to be.

She said so in a yard on the twenty-first of the month, in front of twenty people, and said that she has not run out of road but has discovered she does not know the question any more, and that every clerk she has found in four months has been entirely correct. **Rue told her to come back on the Sunday, not for an answer, for the walking, and Lena Marr said it was the first sensible thing anybody had said to her about it in four months.**

## Tomas Rusk

- **Where he is:** his front room, sixty-one, a bad knee, four years of a district works pension, and a second-shift roll he handed over in September.
- **What he refused this batch, in public, on the eighth of the month:** to swear the account. **He did not take the account back.** It stays in Mara Quill's hand, dated, with his name at the foot of it, and it is still evidence, and it is worse evidence, which is what Mara predicted in front of forty people on the second of the month.
- **His reason, in his own words, and it is not curable:** a sworn account is a man saying out loud that he will stand behind it for the rest of his life; his account says a road was not being kept on a reach of the district works on the night a second-stage main failed; a road cannot be put right; and he is going to draw that pension for four more years. **It is a wage. Milo Rook looked for a wrong reason for a week and there is not one and he wrote that down.**
- **What he said once and will not say again:** that whoever wrote the replacement page on the ninth of that month six years ago had a mechanic's private book in front of them, and the number on that page is the number on the first page of his own book, which has never been in the district's possession.
- **The October position:** three sworn accounts and one unsworn. **Verrick Assal's written request — asked three times, said nothing three times, not a witness — is the precedent for what a documented non-witness looks like, and Tomas Rusk's refusal is now a second instance of the same discipline and not an act of cowardice.**

## Verrick Assal

- **Where he is:** the works compound, mending chain, alive, on a wage, fifty-eight, with a wife who is one of two people in the world who knows what he did on the tenth of the month.
- **What he did:** he said yes. **He walked Milo Rook in and out of a court at 14.06 on the tenth of the month and did not have to stop the work.** His reason, given twice: he has been asked three times about a bell he was not in a room for, he asked in writing to be recorded as not a witness, and it is in the record and it is a strange thing to have wanted; and a man who can say stop is a different thing from a man who can say clear, and that is his whole qualification and it has never been on paper before.
- **His condition, which is on the page and which Milo has not broken:** if his name is put next to a bell he will say so in whatever room it was put in. He said it out loud, in a yard of nineteen, before Milo could say it for him. **He remains not a witness and the record still says so.**

## Jo Lask

- **What he refused this batch, and why it was right:** a turn, in writing, in advance. *A turn is a place where a route changes its mind. I do not have a time. I am suspended. There is no box. And a man who is free at four is a man anybody can use.*

- **What he said that the chapter is built on:** the diversion is a street that is not a street and you give a man room, and that is why they gave the box a minute and a half at the turn, and a signal is not a person, and a count goes to a person.

## The works, the Recovery officer, and the number

- **The Recovery officer** has been a civil servant for nineteen years, has a relief schedule of two and four a head a week on two hundred and thirty-one people on the trestle table since the middle of last month, and **for the first time in this case turned the schedule the right way up** — face up — to say the number out loud.
