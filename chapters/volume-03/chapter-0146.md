# Chapter 146: Side Four, And A Row With A Sentence In It That Is Not Anybody's

**The flatbed from a yard at Cobb Lock comes in from the city on a Friday morning at about a quarter to seven and goes back to the city on the Friday afternoon and it does not wait either way.** He got on it at Cobb Lock at about ten to four and it was in the yard in the city at about half past four, and he walked from there.

**The bundle was on the counter of the quay section office when he came in, because the bundle comes on a Friday, and the weekly sheet goes into the rack on a Friday, and the third side of the schedule was in it and the fourth side was under it and the fourth side was not the same paper as the third side.**

**THE ELEVENTH STREET DISTRICT, one line, typed.**

> Stability of district:
> Works of record:
> Units benefited:
> **Ground of discontinuance: (a) no maintained seam of record**
> **NOT TO BE MAINTAINED AFTER 31 AUGUST**

A reference in the top corner in the same series as the number on the face-up card, and a date under it, and the date was **the sixteenth**, and the sixteenth was a Thursday.

---

"The office completed it on Thursday," said Cyril Sale. He had a works clerk's jacket on and a pen behind his ear and he had not sat down. "**They complete them on the Thursday. It is a Thursday thing and it has been a Thursday thing for about eleven years and it is not a punishment and it is not a meeting.**"

"It says no maintained seam of record."

"**It says there is no maintained seam of record, and there is a check to be made on that every year, and the check is done by reading the district's own file, and the district's file has got nothing in it, so the answer is nil, so they write the key.**"

"Then it is a true sentence."

"**It is a true sentence and it is the only true one available to them, and I want to say that before anything else, because I have known a works office that put a sentence in a column that was not true, and this is not that office.**"

---

**And Milo Rook stood in a corridor four rooms above a fitter's shop at about twenty to five on Friday the seventeenth of July and worked out the whole of it in about four seconds, standing up, to himself, and he has written down that it is the second time in eight months he has named something in a corridor and both times it was in a corridor and neither time he was in a room with a chair in it.**

**The check is done by reading the district's own file.**

The district's file has got a level in it, under a foreman's signature, and a line with a date on it, and a joint finding of the fourth of December.

**And the entry that would have gone in the file on the sixteenth of December was made on a classification, and the classification of the eleventh was withdrawn in the spring, and a classification that is withdrawn is not to be relied on by any party, and a record that rests on a withdrawn classification is not a record that can be relied on by any party, and so the thing that happened in the eleventh street in December is not in the file, and so the check is done and the answer is nil, and the key says that where there is no maintained seam of record the works are not reimbursable at the present rating, and there is a printed column on a printed side in a building about a mile away, and a man with a pen completed a row in about a minute and a half.**

**Nobody lied. There is no villain in this and I have looked for one for two days and there is not one, and a department that cannot record a loss it has no form for is doing the correct thing with the forms it has, and it is the correct thing.**

---

**And the refusal, and it was in a corridor, and it took about four seconds, and it was correct, and it changed nothing.**

Cyril Sale put the sheet down and took the pen out from behind his ear and put it on the counter and did not pick it up again.

"I am not signing column six."

"You are the person responsible for maintenance in the district. I have not got anybody else."

"**I am not signing column six, and you cannot make me, and I am going to tell you why in one sentence and then I am going to stop.**"

"Go on."

"**Because if I sign that, I have put a sentence about the sixteenth of December into a file, and there is not one sentence about the sixteenth of December that is both true and mine.**" He did not raise his voice. "**There is a true one and it is not mine. There is one that is mine and it is a form about a length of road and it is not about the sixteenth of December. And I am not going to write either of them on this.**"

"You are a works clerk of forty-six."

"I am a works clerk of forty-six with three sentences on the backs of three forms in the correspondence tray and not in a drawer, and I have stopped putting them there, and this is the same thing a different size.**"

---

**And the row was completed on the sixteenth by a person about a mile away and does not need a signature in this building, and that is the whole of what a form built for one purpose does when it is handed to another one, and it happened in about a minute and a half, and it is correct.**

**Side four has been round twice.** It went out with the schedule on the third of July, and the office has kept it, and it has come back out on the seventeenth with a row completed on it, and nobody in this district was told that it had been kept.

The man of about forty-one on no list at all, from the doorway, at about twenty to six, and Milo Rook had asked him one thing and got nine words and this is the eleventh time he has said nine words at that door since the twenty-eighth of December, and there is still no column anywhere for the number.

"**Standing in a room is standing in a file.**"

---

**And then the cost, and it is not curable, and it is not paid by Lena Marr, and it is not paid by the woman of sixty on the second landing, and it is paid on a Monday morning at half past seven by eleven men and a foreman who are this city's contractor and not this city's employees.**

The contract runs to the thirty-first of August.

**The gang is on the back length on Mondays and Thursdays at half past seven. The weekly sheet covers Monday to Sunday. The sheet goes into the rack on the Friday. The duplicate goes into a tin. And the duplicate goes into a second tin, on the bottom shelf of the plan room of the Civic Atlas Office, under a repair schedule.**

After the thirty-first of August there is no gang on the back length, and no sheet to cover Monday to Sunday, and no rack on a Friday with anything in it, and no duplicate, and no carbon in a tin, and no carbon in the second tin on the bottom shelf in another building. **The condition column has been empty for a hundred and fifty-five days and the reason column for about twenty weeks, and on the twenty-seventh of August there is a half past seven and it is the last Thursday, and on the twenty-eighth there is a Friday and a rack and a sheet goes into it for the last time, and on Monday the thirty-first of August there is a half past seven and it is the last one there will ever be on that length, and on Tuesday the first of September there is a half past seven and there is nobody on it, and there is no form in this city for a gang that is not there, because a gang that is not there has not failed at anything.**

**And the arithmetic of the date, which took him about eleven minutes standing at a counter on the Friday afternoon and which nobody in this building had done.** The form says *not to be maintained after 31 August*, and the thirty-first of August is a **Monday**, and the gang is on a Monday and a Thursday, and nobody in the office that printed the form looked at a calendar, because a printed date does not know what day of the week it is and a printed date does not need to. **The last morning of that gang is the last day of the contract. Monday the thirty-first of August at half past seven, and it is a working morning, and it is the last one. The last Thursday is the twenty-seventh. A rack day is a Friday, and the last Friday inside the contract is Friday the twenty-eighth, and the sheet that goes into the rack on the twenty-eighth is the sheet for the week of Monday the twenty-fourth, and it covers Monday to Sunday, and it is the last sheet that will ever be printed for this gang. And there is no sheet at all for the week of Monday the thirty-first, because the schedule of works ends and there is nothing after it to print one. So the last walk of the season is on a Monday morning and it is not on a sheet, and it is not in the rack, and it is not in either tin, and there is no carbon of it anywhere, and there is no form in this city for a walk that is not on a sheet. And the rack is a piece of furniture in a corridor and nobody empties a rack, and on Friday the fourth of September there is a rack with nothing in it in it, and on Friday the eleventh, and there is nothing in this city that says a rack is finished.**

---

**And at about half past six that evening there were about nine people in a yard and there was no coffee, and it has been established that there is no coffee.**

He said it out loud in about thirty seconds and then stood there and did not improve it.

"**Side four came into this office in the bundle this afternoon. The row for this district has a sentence in column five. The sentence is (a) no maintained seam of record. Underneath it, not to be maintained after the thirty-first of August. It was completed in a room about a mile away on Thursday afternoon in about a minute and a half and nobody in it lied to anybody, and I have been two days trying to find the person who did it wrong and there is not one, and that is the worst thing that has happened in this district in eight months.**"

"Then the works go."

"**The works go.**"

---

Roy Pryce, fifty-four, thirty-one years, did not say it for about a minute and a half.

"**I had something ready for Friday.**"

Milo Rook did not look at anybody.

"**I had it ready on the Thursday night and I brought it in on the Friday and there is no post comes to eleven streets, because the post goes to a rateable unit and we have not got one, and the satchel is the only thing that comes in here, so it went out in the satchel on the Friday morning and it came back in the satchel on the Friday afternoon and it has been in the satchel every day since.**" He put his hand flat on his hip. "**That is the whole of it. It has not gone anywhere and it is not going anywhere, and nobody is to be blamed for it, and I would like it noted that I did not stand there on the Friday morning with it in my hand and ask anybody, because I did not think there was anywhere to ask.**"

Nobody said anything for about eleven seconds.

"And I want to say one more thing and then I am going to go and wash," he said. "**That condition column has been empty for a hundred and fifty-five days, and a man in a building a mile away has written a sentence about it, and it is the first sentence anybody has ever written about that column, and it is not mine, and I am not going to pretend it is.**"

---

**And Milo Rook stood in a yard at about ten to seven and did not say one word, and could not have said one word, and did not have one.**

*He thinks I did not fill in the column because of him.*

*That is the ninth time I have done that to somebody and the eighth time it cost somebody else something and this time it is the only time that the somebody is standing in front of me.*

---

Field Book, Friday the seventeenth of July, 23.20, a front room, one day off the sixteenth.

*One. **Side four. Stability: blank. Works of record: blank. Units benefited: blank. Ground of discontinuance: (a) no maintained seam of record. NOT TO BE MAINTAINED AFTER 31 AUGUST. Reference in the series that the face-up card belongs to. Completed the sixteenth, a Thursday, in about a minute and a half, by this office, and the district did not return it, and a district that has not returned within the period stated will be completed by this office, and I did not return it because I was in Mireward on the day and because the reason is in a drawer and a facing page.**

***And the two things in this entry that are the same district and are not the same thing, written down on the Friday night because a reader of this book is going to join them inside a fortnight and joining them would be me deciding they are the same. There is a card that came off a machine on the seventeenth of June with a hand on it and six words in the reason column, and it has a reference number on it, and this side four has a reference number on it out of the same series, and both of the numbers are about a district with no rateable unit. A district is printed on both of them and the district is the whole of the connection and the district is the connection between every two documents in this book. The sentence on the card and the sentence on side four have nothing to do with each other and I do not know whose hand the first one is in and I am not going to find out, and I have not said the six words out loud since the twenty-seventh of June and they are not in this book.***

*Two. **Why (a) is true, and I have got to write this down in full because I am not going to be able to write it again in this form, and because in about four years a man in a room is going to ask why, and the answer will not fit in a sentence.** The check is done by reading the district's own file. The file has a level and a date and a joint finding of the fourth of December in it. The entry that would put the sixteenth of December in that file rests on a classification, and the classification of the eleventh is withdrawn and is not to be relied on by any party, and a record that rests on a withdrawn classification is not to be relied on by any party, and so the sixteenth of December is not in the file, and so the check finds nil, and nil is the key. **Nobody lied. A department that cannot record a loss it has no form for is doing the correct thing with the forms it has.** A bridge is a structure on one half of it and a route on the other and it is neither by itself. A seam event is a thing a district is entered as having or not having, and eleven streets has not got one of record, and it has got one of fact, and the two of those have never been the same thing in this city and today they cost a gang of eleven men.***

*Three. **Cyril Sale refused column six in four seconds and he was right and it changed nothing**, and he gave one sentence, and the sentence is that there is not one sentence about the sixteenth of December that is both true and his. I have written that down and I have not improved it and I have not put it in a yard, and I am not going to, and there are nine people in this district who would understand it and all nine of them would be wrong about which of us it belongs to.*

*Four. **The cost and the machinery, and every part of it stops on the same day.** Eleven men and a foreman on a contract to the thirty-first of August, and the contract is not the city's and the men are not the city's and the foreman is not the city's. The sheet, the rack, the tins, the condition column, the reason column, the duplicate in a plan room in another building under a repair schedule, **and a repair schedule is a schedule and a schedule is on a printed side somewhere and nobody has ever asked when it ends. And the thirty-first of August is a Monday, and the gang is on a Monday and a Thursday, and a printed date does not know what day of the week it is, and the last half past seven on that length is Monday the thirty-first of August, which is the last day of the contract, and the last rack day is Friday the twenty-eighth, and the last sheet covers Monday the twenty-fourth to Sunday the thirtieth, and there is no sheet for the week of the thirty-first and there never will be, so the last walk of the season is the only walk of the season that is not on a piece of paper anywhere in the world. The second tin goes to thirty-seven on Friday the twenty-eighth and then it does not go anywhere, and it does not know that and neither does the repair schedule.** The rack is furniture and nobody empties a rack, and on Friday the fourth of September there is a rack in a corridor with nothing in it in it, and there is no form for a rack that is finished. **And I cannot do one single thing about it, and there is no form for that, and the reason there is no form for it is that nothing in this district is a party to anything, and the order is suspended and not discharged and not varied and not withdrawn, and the matter stands over and is not adjourned and has no date and is on no list, and this instrument is a different instrument about different work and it does not mention the order, the district, the classification, the level or me, and there is nothing in it anybody in this district can use.** I am not going to the timetable division and I am not sending anybody there and the man of forty-one has been sent nowhere.***

*Five. **He thinks I did not fill in column two because of him. He does not know I could have, in ninety seconds, in pencil, on the back of their own schedule, on the Saturday, and he does not know I put it there and did not send it, and he has put a thing in a satchel and told nine people that he did not think there was anywhere to ask, and that is the truest sentence anybody has said in this yard since the fifteenth of April and it is about a post and not about a form and it cost him a piece of paper that nobody will ever read.**

*Six. **The page home the two hundred and thirty-sixth day, blank, four marks in four hands, and I am not writing that it is there. The token cold, the two hundred and twenty-second day, in the inside pocket of this coat, and it has now been cold for two hundred and twenty-two days. Eleven remembered routes and ten crossings, the tally is ten lines, Hettie Brann's paper and Dr Sen's pocket, and I have not looked at it since the thirteenth of January and I am not going to in August. The condition column is a hundred and fifty-five days empty and on the thirty-first of August it will not be empty, it will not be there, and those are two different sentences and this city has no word for the second one. The plate is a hundred and two days in the grass and nobody has been told. The second tin went to thirty-one this afternoon.** The reason column is empty and it has been since about the fourth of April and there is no column in this city that wants to know why a column is empty.***

*And the standing position: **the writer of the map is not the maintainer of anything, and that is a list of about fourteen other people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.*
