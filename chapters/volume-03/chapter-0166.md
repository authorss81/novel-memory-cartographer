# Chapter 166: A Return About Who Was Responsible For Keeping Something In Repair, Asked About A District

**It was completed on Thursday the seventeenth of September, which was a Thursday, and they complete them on the Thursdays, and it has been a Thursday thing for about eleven years and it is not a punishment and it is not a meeting. It came back into a building in Crown Heights on Wednesday the twenty-third and Cass Ardery had it on the table in a room with about nine people in it by about half past ten in the morning.**

The page home is the three hundred and fourth day. The token is cold on the two hundred and ninetieth day. The plate in the grass at the low end of Bridge Street is a hundred and seventy days old. The condition column is two hundred and twenty-three days and is not there. **A foreman of fifty-four is twenty-two weeks exactly past four months, and nobody is going to ask him, and the date of that silence is in my own handwriting on a page I cannot get it out of.**

---

## The second thing that came of a printed side

There have been two printed sides about this district since the fourteenth of April. The first was a district schedule with six columns and a key of four grounds, and in the sixth column of the fourth side an office put a name, in about a minute and a half, on a Thursday, in a box headed *signature of the person responsible*.

**Nobody lied. A district with no correspondence address cannot be sent anything and cannot be asked anything and has been exactly that since 1911, and the office went to the document it could reach, and a contract names the supervisor, because a contract has to be able to say who is in charge of a gang on a Tuesday.**

The second one is a different instrument. It is a single side. It is headed:

> **MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITY — SEASON 3 RETURN**
> *Districts in which surface or drainage works have been discontinued during the season. To be completed by the office. State the name of the person responsible for maintenance up to the date of discontinuance, and the office at which returns for the district are held.*

And at the foot of it, in the same small type as everything else on a printed side in this city:

> *A return not received within the period stated will be completed by this office.*

**And in the box, in a typeface, on a Thursday, in about a minute and a half: R. PRYCE. And underneath, in the same hand of the machine, the office at which returns for the district are held.**

---

## The room, and the nine seconds

Cass Ardery is about forty-four and is second-in-command on the investigation of a thing he classified, and he is not the reporting officer and is not going to be, and he wrote the classification of the eleventh of the current year and withdrew it in his own hand on the seventh of December and has said in writing that there is not one rule in his department that says a man who made a mistake can report on it and not one that says he cannot, and that the silence is the reason.

He read the side in about nine seconds.

"Nobody has done anything wrong."

"**No.**"

"They could not ask the district."

"**They could not ask the district and they have not tried to and they are not going to, and the district has no correspondence address and has not had one since 1911, and the record the office holds is a contract, and a contract names the supervisor, and there is no second document in this city that says who was responsible for that road on a Tuesday in June.**" He put it down. "**That is the whole of the mechanism and it took about a minute and a half and it will happen again next season for a different district.**"

"And the name is a foreman of fifty-four and he did not sign the first one either."

"**He did not sign the first one and he will not sign this one and he is not going to be asked to, and I have read the contract and he is on it in his own hand three times and the last time was years ago and in a document that has nothing to do with either of these.**" Cass Ardery looked at the room. "**Is there anybody in this room who thinks he should be told.**"

Nobody said anything, because he was right, and because a man of fifty-four with thirty-one years in charge of a gang is not frightened of a form. **He has been in forms his whole working life. He wrote four words and a figure in a condition column in July on purpose, in pencil, in his own hand, and he did it because a length is a length and a hole is a hole. He is the least frightened man in this story about a piece of paper and he has been given two of them in five weeks by an office that cannot write to a place, and he does not know it, and nobody is going to tell him.**

---

## And then the sentence, and it is the sentence of this book, and it is in a department's mouth

"There is one thing about that side I want to say out loud," said Cass Ardery, "and I want it minuted, and I am not going to say it twice."

Nobody said anything.

"**There is no form in this city in which a district can say that something happened to it.**" He said it at the same speed he read forms out loud. "**Not this one. Not the schedule. Not a return, not a card, not an inspection, not a condition column. There is a form for a death and a form for a rate and a form for a length of road and there is no form for a loss, and where a district has a loss then the office asks it for a figure, and the district has no figure, and the office completes the row, and the row says the district had nothing to report.**"

"That is the design."

"**That is the design. It is not a complaint and it is not a fault and nobody in this room did it and nobody in this room can undo it.**" He squared the side on the table. "**And a district with nothing to report is entered as stable. And a district entered as stable is a district that cannot be frightened. And a district that cannot be frightened is a district where there is nothing to insure against.**"

And Milo Rook, in a room with about nine people in it, could not say one word.

**Because a man of sixty-one on a top track four miles out had said a version of it in about four hundred words on the twenty-fourth of July, and a man of forty-four on a bank had said a version of it on the fifteenth of September in four seconds, and a woman of thirty-four on a schoolroom step had said a version of it in July and refused to give a date, and now it was in the mouth of the second-in-command of a department in a building in Crown Heights, as a design, in nine seconds, and it was correct, and he had no answer to it, and there is no answer to it.**

**This is the second time in eight months that the best sentence said in a room has been said by somebody else and he has had no answer to it.**

---

## The two wrong-shaped things, and neither of them was noticed by anybody but him

**One.** A return about who is responsible for keeping apparatus in repair has been asked to say who was responsible for keeping a district in repair, and it has answered with a contractor's supervisor, when the true answer is that nobody was responsible for noticing, and the district is not a party, and a party is a person, and there is no person.

**Two**, and this is the one that is going to cost somebody and is not going to cost him.

The side says, in the box under the name: **the office at which returns for the district are held.** That is a correct statement. The office is the records office about a mile from where the schedule was drafted, and there are four machines in it and a man of about fifty in it, and the card comes off one of those machines on a Wednesday at about nine.

**And a description of a place is not a person, and a person is not on the side, and in about four years a man can ask that side for the office, and the office is a description, and then he can ask the office for the desk, and then for the machine, and then for the man.**

He stood there and let it happen and he did not say one word about it.

The man of about fifty in the room with four machines does not know that a piece of paper exists, has never been told, and is not going to be told. He was told by his own face in August, in half a second, by a lie Milo Rook put past him, that there was nothing in his room anybody could want, and **in the same month he has been given a district, and in about four years he is going to be findable, and Milo Rook is the man who did both of those things and he is not going to be thanked for either of them by anybody, including himself, because a person who has thanked somebody is a person who has asked.**

---

## And the day after, and the day after that

**The card came off the machine on Wednesday the twenty-third at about nine and went into the Thursday bundle at about eleven and it is in a contractor's yard on the north side of the city and it is the first thing anybody has sent to that yard about the eleventh street district since about 1911.**

**Nobody has told the district that a card is on its way, and there is nowhere to tell it, and a boy of seventeen said no in a yard on Monday the twenty-first, and Roy Pryce has two boxes with his name in them on two printed sides and does not know it and is not going to be told, and a woman of thirty-one shut a register on a shelf on the twenty-seventh of August and entered nothing and has not been told what I did on Monday morning and is not going to be told by me.**

And a man of about fifty in a room with four machines pressed a button on a district for the first time in nineteen years because a man in a good coat gave him something to do, and he did not know what it was for, and nobody is going to tell him, and there is no column anywhere for that.

*Field Book, Wednesday the twenty-third of September, 23.20, a front room in eleven streets, one day off the twenty-second.*

*One. **A second printed side in September, and the mechanism is the one from August, and it happened on the same Thursday, in the same office, in about the same minute and a half, and nobody lied.** A return about maintenance responsibility, asked about a district, answered from a contract, and a foreman of fifty-four's name in a box for the second time in five weeks, and he did not sign the first one and will not sign this one and nobody is going to ask him. **A file is a party; a party is a person; a person is a name in a box; and the only way a department has of writing to a place with no address is to go to a contract.** I have that in this book four times and it has now been in a printed side twice and both times it was correct and both times it was me who had to go and look at it afterwards.*

*Two. **THE SENTENCE, IN A DEPARTMENT'S MOUTH, IN NINE SECONDS, AND IT IS CORRECT, AND I HAVE NO ANSWER TO IT: there is no form in this city in which a district can say that something happened to it.** A man of sixty-one on a top track, a man of forty-four on a bank, a woman of thirty-four on a step, and now the second-in-command of Survey Enforcement in a room in Crown Heights, and all four of them are right and all four of them have been right for months and I am the only one of the four who has been writing it down. **A district that cannot report a loss is a district with nothing to report, and a district with nothing to report is entered as stable, and a district entered as stable is a district where there is nothing to insure against, and there are about nine hundred rows on that schedule and eight hundred of them have a word or a hand-transferred zero in it and a zero in a column about a district is a department saying a thing about a place and not about a person.** I am not going to improve that paragraph and I am not going to put a seventh column anywhere near it.*

*Three. **THE COST OF TODAY IS A DESCRIPTION OF A PLACE.** The second side carries, under a man's name, the office at which returns for the district are held, and that is true, and there are four machines in that office and a man of about fifty in it, and in about four years a man can ask the side for the office and then the office for the desk and then for the machine and then for the man. **A description of a place is how a person gets found, and it is correct, and I am the only person in the room who can see it, and I did not say one word, and I told that man in August there was nothing in his room anybody could want and I put that there in half a second.** Nobody asked me to pay this. There is no column for it and I am not going to make one.*

*Four. **The standing temptation and where it went.** It is not a column. It is a use. On Monday I gave a man in a room with four machines a district to hold, and on Tuesday I found out that a woman of thirty-four has no card in a hand index in this city for a funeral and that she checked twice on her own time in four years, and on Monday I asked a boy of seventeen to carry a card and he said no in four seconds and he was right. **I have now got three things I have done in three days and not one of them is a map and not one of them is a column and every one of them is a person, and the pairs are twenty-nine and have not moved in sixty-nine days, and an interval of one day is not a pair, and a pair is a fact about where I was and not about what happened.** The page home the three hundred and fourth day, blank, four marks in four hands, none of them mine. The token cold, the two hundred and ninetieth day. Eleven remembered routes, ten crossings, the tally ten lines, not looked at since the thirteenth of January. The condition column is two hundred and twenty-three days and is not there. The plate is a hundred and seventy days and has been told to nobody. The second tin is at thirty-seven and there is no thirty-eight.

*And the standing position: **the writer of the map is not the maintainer of anything, and that is a list of about fourteen other people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.**
