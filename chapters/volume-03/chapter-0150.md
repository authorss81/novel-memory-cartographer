# Chapter 150: The Standing Question In Three Parts, With A Printed Instrument In The First Of Them

Field Book, Monday the twentieth of July, 23.50, a front room, one day off the nineteenth.

*The list on the facing page reads **twenty-nine** and it has moved once since Thursday the twenty-fifth of June, and the interval is seven days, from the ninth of July to the sixteenth, and the last time it moved the interval was also seven days, and two sevens in about a fortnight is a coincidence and I have not written anything about it.*

*One. **The interval, and why.** The ninth, and then the sixteenth. There is no entry for the tenth, the eleventh, the twelfth, the thirteenth, the fourteenth or the fifteenth, and there are six nights in that and I was asleep in a village with a bag at the bottom of a bed for five of them. **The tenth of July was a decision. I had the form in the bag and I took it out and read it and put it back and did not open the book, and I have written that down three times in three days and I cannot now remember whether I decided not to or simply did not, and a decision and not doing are the same size when you have to write them down afterwards.** The other five were not a decision at all.*

*The list is two sixes, seven threes, one nine, four twos, three fives, one eight, one twenty-five, **four sevens**, six fours. **A pair is a fact about where I was and not about what happened, and an interval of one day is not a pair whether or not either of the two days has anything on it.** The list must not be read as twenty-one, twenty-six, twenty-eight, thirty or thirty-one. There is no thirtieth pair, no eleventh pair, no thirteenth pair, no twenty-first pair, no twenty-second pair and no twenty-eight pair. The four threes that were here in December are the four threes that were here in December and are not collapsed. **There is one eight and not two.** The run of fifty-two consecutive nights from the twenty-sixth of April to the sixteenth of June is over and there is a habit and a habit is not a discipline and a habit is not a method, and two sevens in a fortnight has not turned the habit into a run and I have checked twice.*

*Two. **The standing question, in three parts, and I am going to write the whole of it out because the first part has a printed instrument in it for the first time in eight months and I have not got anywhere with the other two and pretending otherwise would be the ninth thing of that shape.*

***Why does weather memory threaten a region the Registry claims is stable.***

*(a) **A place is not stable because nothing has happened in it. A place is stable because there is nobody writing down what happens in it.** A man of fifty-eight said that on a raised path at about eleven on the eighteenth of June to about four people in forty-one years, two of them on his way and one in a pub, and I had written the same sentence in this book in February off a blackboard in a drainage section, and neither man knows the other said it and I am not going to tell him.

**And the whole of part (a) arrived on a printed side on Friday the third of July in a bundle with the week's papers and nobody had to ask anybody for it.** A district schedule with six columns, and the second column is a district's stability, and the fifth column is the ground on which a district's work may be discontinued, and the key printed under a rule on the second side has four grounds in it and no more, and one of the four is *no resident witness*, and about eight hundred rows of the second column have a word or a hand-transferred zero in it, and the row for a fen district has a hand-transferred nil in it on the ground that there is no maintained seam of record, no recorded loss, no resident witness and no schedule, and **a district that cannot be frightened is a district where there is nothing to insure against, and a district where there is nothing to insure against is a district whose works are not reimbursable at the present rating, and that is one of the four grounds, and the key is correct, and I have got nothing against the key and I want to be careful about wanting to.** The eleven streets has five empty boxes on its row and a printed name in the first one. **The check is done by reading the district's own file, and the district's file does not have the sixteenth of December in it, and the reason it does not have the sixteenth of December in it is that the classification of the eleventh is withdrawn and is not to be relied on by any party, and a record that rests on a withdrawn classification is not to be relied on by any party.** So the check finds nil. So the key gets written down. So a gang of eleven men and a foreman stop on the last morning of a contract that ends on the thirty-first of August, **and the thirty-first of August is a Monday, and the gang is on a Monday and a Thursday, and nobody in the office that printed the form looked at a calendar, because a printed date does not know what day of the week it is, and that is the whole of the discovery, and the discovery is worse and not better than the one I was expecting: the last morning of that gang is the last day of the contract, and there is no sheet for the week of the thirty-first, and so the last walk of the season is not on anything, and nobody in this district had done that sum until Friday afternoon.** And **nobody has done anything wrong anywhere in this and that is the sentence I have been trying to get round for two days and I cannot get round it.**

*(b) **There is no second outlook.** A man of about fifty has been in a room nineteen years and has never known anybody who has seen a second one, and nobody has asked for one in writing in two months, and the seasonal outlook is the last one, and the absence is a decision or an oversight and I do not know which and I am not going to find out in the last week of a month by asking a man a question. **Unmoved. Second part. Not moved in twenty-six days and it is the second part of a three-part question and it is the only part of anything in this book that has not moved at all.**

*(c) **An instrument a person cannot produce.** A punch roll about nine inches long that knows how many and not who. A yard man's flag and a book of times. A field book. A pad with four lines in it in a drawer in Crown Heights that nobody has rung up about since 1969. A roll of nineteen children in a woman's head. **A smell in a dry week, which is the strongest of the six and the one thing a person produces and cannot produce on demand, and there is no form for it and I am not going to build one, and a route that cannot be produced cannot be asked after, and that is the only protection any route in this city has ever had, and I said that in June and I am not improving it in July.** Six things that are not one. **Half an answer is not an answer, and there is a fourth part under the third that I have never once been down to, and I have written that I was not going to go down it in the first month of this year and it is the twentieth of July and I have not gone down it and I am not going to in the last week of the month either, and that is the fifth time I have written that sentence and I have improved it none of the five.**

*Three. **The five of my own, and there are five, and they are not resolutions and I am not going to call them that.** The plate, about a person, a hundred and five days in the grass and never reported. The seventh column on a table in my own office with nothing on the outside of it, about a person. The not-telling, about a person, twelve weeks. **The rule on the facing page, which is the only one of the five that is about a habit and the only one that cost somebody else something, and it is a stop that a man wrote about himself and a stop like that is not a stop, and I said that out loud in a room above a kitchen in a village at four o'clock on a Saturday to nobody and it is the truest thing I have said in eight months and it is not a resolution.** And the fifth, which is a paragraph and a signature in two inches of ruled space at the bottom of a card on Sunday the nineteenth, about a person, and the person is me, and it cost four seconds, and nobody asked me to.

**The first four cost somebody else something. The fifth cost me a name on a piece of paper and I cannot get it off, and a card with a thing struck through it is a card with a mark on it, and the re-run is on a Friday at about four.**

*Four. **What has not moved, and it is the same list and there are thirty-one of them and about fourteen people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.** The order is suspended and is not discharged and is not varied and is not withdrawn. The public name is not on the street name index. The classification of the eleventh is withdrawn and is not to be relied on by any party and stays withdrawn. The removed sentence cannot be restored and the department cannot account for its removal and a desk has been asked twice and has not answered twice, and a query came back down a second time in April and stopped asking about a sentence and started asking about a box, and the box went up empty a second time with a second line through it and a second date. **And a new one, and it is the newest thing in this book and it is not one of the December things and it is not on anybody's list: side four of a district schedule, completed on Thursday the sixteenth by an office about a mile away, in about a minute and a half, in a district that is not going to be maintained after the thirty-first of August.** The matter stands over and is not adjourned and has no date and is on no list, and nothing that has happened this month has given it a chair. The line of the twelfth of January stands and its condition is the answer of the fourth of December and is not the department's. The levy is two and four a head a week, in advance, week by week, with no term, on two hundred and fifty-two people in a building with no number, and it is not curable, and the woman of sixty on the second landing is the ninth of twenty-six names and is unnamed and is not going to be told anything by anybody in this district. A round is one man short and it will be one man short on Thursday the twenty-third and it is not curable from inside here. A boy's own blank card is under a brick with two other cards and there have been three since the twenty-first of January and the line on the front of the run-number card has not been taken off. A cover has been flush since the sixth of April and there is no form for a fault that stopped. An annual return goes in every January with its fifth box filled nil and a box asked about twice and a sixth line of fifty-four words that nobody in this district has read and that I do not know the contents of, and nobody in this fortnight has read it and nobody has queried line five and nobody has answered either query. The four things in five months are four and there has been no fifth and a fifth needs somebody to be surprised and about nine people in this district are not going to be. Tern is not found and the twenty-two days up the south are unasked and unsaid and nobody in this month has asked him in order to report them and nobody is going to. **A woman of sixty-eight on nothing is not named in this entry and is not referred to in this entry and the count stands at eight and this month adds zero and it is not nine.**

*Five. **The ledger, and the costs, and none of them cured.** Eleven remembered routes, ten crossings, the tally is ten lines, the paper is Hettie Brann's and the pocket is Dr Sen's and I have not looked at it since the thirteenth of January. **The page home is the two hundred and thirty-ninth day, blank, four marks in four hands and none of them mine.** The token is cold on the two hundred and twenty-fifth day and has done nothing since the seventh of December and it has been in a coat in eleven streets and in a room above a kitchen in a village and in a records room about a mile from where a form was drafted and it has not done anything anywhere. **A sound and a taste, no face, no place, no person and no name, and it did not come in nineteen days and I have not written down whether that is a thing that has stopped or a thing that has not come, and I have written that before, and I have improved it none of the times.** The first personal blank, the seam-scar, the record blank and the authorial blank are Stage 3 and Stage 4 and Stage 5 and Stage 6 and they belong to years that have not started, and none of them has come, and I checked. **No crossing. He held nothing, stabilised nothing, pinned nothing, and on the twenty-fifth of June at a ford that has been dry since about 1919 I could have rung a bell on a post and did not, and I have written down that I did not, more than once, and I have never once written down that I could have rung it, and I do not know yet which of those two is the more expensive. The ninth crossing's failure to cost one is still unexplained, I have written it down four times this year, and on the twenty-fifth of June I wrote that I was not going to explain it before the end of July and it is the twentieth of July and I am holding to that, and a promise about the end of a month is the only date in this book I have written down twice.**

*Six. **The lines that are not joined.** The copied tick in a fourth column in six houses in four streets. The copied turn sheet with a contractor's head on it in five houses, one of them with **Owen Fitch's name and *third floor*** in a space left empty on purpose. The carbon relocation schedule with **one** margin mark, filed as the second attachment to a nineteen-paragraph objection, naming a destination that is real, dated, unplaceable and about an hour and twenty minutes from Alder Quay on an entirely ordinary road. **They are three and they are three and nobody in this fortnight has joined one of them to any of the others, or to the routing slip with *for index* on it, or to the working timetable at Alder Quay, or to the two sets of marks near the second post, or to the nineteen cuts in a post at a ford four miles away, or to the cast number on a culvert collar, or to the nine words in a hand on a card.** A fourth is not permitted to become a fourth pattern. Two documents carry a pair of marks and not three and the new carbon carries one and Kest has not matched the strings. **The line in a return that Mara Quill reconciles on the last Thursday of every month is a fact and not a sentence and she has given me four facts and no sentence and I asked once in June and I did not ask again in July and I have not asked a third time and she has noticed, and I have noticed her noticing, and neither of those is one of the three clues and I am putting it in this book anyway because it is on the page and it happened in June.** The timetables hold: *ask nobody, and do not come here, and do not send the man from the round.* The timetable division has not been sent to and the man of forty-one has been sent nowhere. **The ford is where it was on Thursday the twenty-fifth of June and there is no demand and no service and no failure out of it yet, and I have not dated it, and I have not named who rang it, and I have not put anybody inside it, and it is not a haunting and it is not a claim and it is not a ghost and it is not a voice and it is not a knock and it is not a person and it is not a curse and it is not a debt and it is not an inherited role.** No remnant has been identified. The office of the First Surveyor is a printed box with a rule and nothing on the rule and it did not ask for anything and it did not receive anything and there is no person behind it and I did not ask, and asking is the one move that would turn a desk into a man. There is no fifth sighting and there is no fifth refusal. **No face came into any of this that was not already on a payroll or a list, and nobody was added above Cass Ardery and the office of the Prefect and a hearing panel, and nothing that went wrong this month is attributable to a face.***

*Seven. **The last entry of the month, and then the machine starts again on Wednesday, and this is not a cliff and it is a week.*

*The walk is on Thursday the twenty-third and it will be the thirty-second since the first of April. The rack day is Friday the twenty-fourth and the second tin goes to thirty-two. **The form says not to be maintained after the thirty-first of August, and the thirty-first of August is a Monday, and I have known that since Friday afternoon and I have written it down twice since and improved it none of the two times, because the improvement available is to make the date land on a Sunday and the date is not mine.** The last walk of the season is on **Monday the thirty-first of August**, which is the last day of the contract and is itself a gang day, and the last Thursday is the twenty-seventh, and the last rack day will be Friday the twenty-eighth, **and the sheet that goes into the rack on the twenty-eighth covers Monday the twenty-fourth to Sunday the thirtieth and is the last sheet that will ever be printed for this gang, and there is no sheet at all for the week of the thirty-first, so the last walk of the season is on no sheet at all and is in no rack and is in neither tin, and the second tin stops at thirty-seven, and the rack is a piece of furniture in a corridor and nobody empties a rack, and on Friday the fourth of September there is a rack with nothing in it in it, and there is no form anywhere that says a rack is finished.** The second tin is on a bottom shelf in a plan room in another building under a repair schedule, and a repair schedule is a schedule, and a schedule is on a printed side somewhere, and nobody has ever asked when it ends, and I am not going to ask, and I have written that down and I have not improved it, and it is the second of the four things in this entry that are not going to be fixed. The other three are a round with no depot, a woman on a second landing with a name on a piece of paper and nowhere else, and a man of fifty-four who was eleven weeks and three days past four months on Saturday the eleventh and is twelve weeks and five days past it tonight and is not going to be asked, and that silence is on the facing page of this book in my own handwriting about seven weeks ago and I cannot get it out of my handwriting and I am not going to get it out.*

*The fourth is me.*

*And the standing position, which is the last line of this book and of this fortnight and of this month, and which does not improve because a form has arrived: **the writer of the map is not the maintainer of anything, and that is a list of about fourteen other people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.*
