# Chapter 170: The Morning Of The Thirtieth, And A Word That Does Not Make It Rain And Does Not Stop It Raining

**Wednesday the thirtieth of September. ## A word, on a Wednesday, in a district that has not heard it

He had a word and it was in a book in a coat and it was on a piece of paper of his own in a coat and it was in his head, and it had been there since about eleven o'clock the previous afternoon, and it was **Rainway**, and it was the name of a water, and he had written it down twice, and neither of the two was on anything that goes into a building.

**He went out into the front room at about eleven o'clock and stood at the window for about four minutes and looked at the weather, and the weather was doing what the weather does.**

That is the whole of what a word in a book does, and I wrote it down at about half past eleven on the thirtieth of September in one line and the line is the truest line in this entry and it is not a resolution and it is not going to be improved.

*A word in a book does not make it rain and it does not stop it raining. The water is about two miles long and it has a name because somebody in about 1911 had to write one down, and that is the whole of the word, and I have got it, and it is in this book, and it is mine, and it does nothing. **I have been waiting since the sixth of April for a thing that would do something, and I have got a name for a water, and I am not going to build an instrument out of a word in a coat, and there is no form for a word in a coat and there is not going to be one, and a person who builds one is the person the conductor of thirty-eight was talking about in April.***

**And he did not tell anybody. Not a woman of thirty-one with a return to reconcile on a Thursday, not a woman of thirty-four in a village with a schoolroom in it, not a woman of thirty-seven on a step at a landing, not a man of sixty-one on a top track who told him not to come back, not a man of forty-four on a bank, not a boy of seventeen, not a man of about fifty in a room with four machines, not a man of about forty-one on no list at all who has been in a corridor four times a day since the twenty-eighth of December, and not a foreman of fifty-four who is on a firm's books and not on a district's.**

**And nobody was thanked, and he is not going to thank anybody, and a person who has thanked somebody is a person who has asked, and there is no column anywhere for the number of times he has wanted to say a word out loud and has not.**
