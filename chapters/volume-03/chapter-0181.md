# Chapter 181: Eight In The Morning, Two Prices, And A Bus That Is Not A Day Off

**Saturday the thirty-first of October and Sunday the first of November and Monday the second of November and Tuesday the third of November. There is no vehicle on a Saturday or a Sunday and there has not been one in this district since about 1961, and the round goes out on Mondays and Thursdays at ten past eight and comes in at about four, and the flatbed comes in from the city on a Friday morning at about a quarter to seven and is in Mireward at about half past ten and goes back on the Friday afternoon, and a lorry comes to a yard at the north end on a Thursday morning, and there are two motor vehicles a week in a district of a hundred and forty people and there is one tractor. A bus goes past the end of this street every twenty minutes on a weekday and two of them a day go as far as a village eleven miles the other way. He has been on one of them twice and he had not been on one since the second of January until this morning.**

The page home is the three hundred and forty-second day and it is the Saturday. The token is cold on the three hundred and twenty-eighth. The plate in the grass at the low end of Bridge Street is two hundred and eight days old and has been told to nobody. The condition column is two hundred and sixty-one days and is not there. **A foreman of fifty-four is twenty-seven weeks and three days past four months and nobody is going to ask him.**

---

## Two days with nothing in them, and he did not put them anywhere

**Saturday and Sunday he was in eleven streets and there was nothing to go to and nothing to come from and he wrote two Field Book entries about that and they are the only two he has written in eleven months that are about a day with nothing in it, and he has not read either of them back, and he is not going to.**

What he did with the Saturday was walk to the end of the street and back, and with the Sunday he cut nothing and mended nothing and sat in a front room with a cup of tea that went cold twice, and **a man with nothing to do on a Saturday in a district where the works stopped on the thirty-first of August is a man who is available for a decision, and he wrote that down on the Sunday night in about four seconds and it is the truest thing in the entry, and it is also the reason he had been in this position since the seventh of September and it is not going to be a rule and it is not going to be a resolution.**

*There is no motor vehicle in this district on a Saturday or a Sunday and I have known that since the second of January and I have used it, twice, for eight months, as the reason I have not decided anything. A thing you cannot get to is a thing you cannot decide about. **That is not true and I have known it was not true since the twenty-ninth of September and this is the third weekend I have spent it, and the third weekend is the one after the schedule took effect, and I got on a bus this morning at eight, and I did not get on it because I had made up my mind, and I got on it because I had not made it up, and I do not know which of those two it was and I wrote that down and I am not going to improve it.***

---

## Monday the second, the round, and the third state

The round went out at about ten past eight on Monday the second of November and came in at about four with the bag empty, and it is a Monday and a Thursday round and it has been since the second of January and it is one man short, and the bag has been empty since the twenty-eighth of August, and it is not a walk and nobody has called it one.

**Monday the second of November was a Monday and there was no gang on it and the rack was not a rack day and the barriers were still out and the road was still open, and both of those last two are correct.**

The page home is the three hundred and forty-fourth day. The token is cold on the three hundred and thirtieth. The plate is two hundred and ten days old. The condition column is two hundred and sixty-three days and is not there. **A foreman of fifty-four is twenty-seven weeks and five days past four months and nobody is going to ask him.**

He did the round's business, which is going about a district that has nothing to collect, and at about eleven he was on the Row and the man of about forty was on it with a brush and did not stop and has still not been asked for his twenty-eight undated words and is not going to be, and the fishmonger of about forty-five had her trays out by nine and was not thanked and was not asked and said nothing, and a man of about sixty with a shed eleven minutes away said good morning and does not know that a piece of paper exists and is not going to be told.

**And the machinery is still printing, and it printed nothing about this district, and that is correct, and the reason it printed nothing is that the schedule had its row on the thirtieth of October and a row that is done is a row that is done, and the season's work went out on the first with a form in it that wants a length of frontage in yards, and there is no correspondence address and there is no post and the form is not coming back and the office will complete it on a Thursday in about a minute and a half out of a contract that expired on the thirty-first of August, and the figure it will put in is two thousand three hundred and forty yards, and the figure is correct, and there is nothing in this district that can put a different figure anywhere.**

And the chair is still not there. There is no chair and no date and no chair has been asked for, and the matter stands over and is not adjourned and is on no list and was not on the business of anything this month and is not going to be on the business of anything.

---

## And the four seconds, on the Monday, at about half past two, on the Row

**It was four seconds, on an ordinary Monday, on a pavement, with the brush going somewhere behind him.**

The sound went — no sound at all, and under it, in the same four seconds, the taste he has never put a name to and has never put a place to. It came and it went in the same four seconds and it was not the same as the silence and it was not in it.

**He did not write the time down. That is three times in eight months and he wrote on the Monday night that he had not written it down, and a man who writes down that he did not do a thing has still done the thing, and there is no form for that and he is not going to build one.**

**It acquired nothing.** No face, no place, no person, no name. **It did not become the brush, the trays, the Row, the barriers, the rack, the man of about sixty, the bus timetable, the schedule, the four hundred and ten yards, the eleven yards he had walked on a bridge in September, or a village. It did not become Neris, and it did not become a key, and it did not become the name of a water, and it did not become anything at all.** A lost sound and a bell that rings are two different things and I am not going to write them in the same column.

---

## And on the Monday night he wrote down the two prices, in his own hand, and then he could not put them in a list

He stood in the front room at about ten at night and did the thing he does, which is to write the whole of it in the order it is going to happen, and it took him about four minutes and it took four minutes because he kept starting it as a paragraph and it was not a paragraph.

*The schedule took effect on Friday the thirtieth of October at about a minute past eight in the morning and it says not maintained and the eleven streets and a village eleven miles the other way are both on it and neither of them has been asked and one of them cannot be and the other one has not.*

*It is a Tuesday. I have got a Tuesday.*

*ONE. The bus at the end of the street. Eight in the morning, and the same bus back in the evening, and the village is eleven miles and it is there in about an hour and an hour and I will be in a schoolroom from about eleven until about four.*

*TWO. A building in Crown Heights, about a mile from where that schedule was drafted. Thirty-four steps up. Nine desks on the left. A hand index with a card in it for one named thing, and the named things in that room are rateable things with a street on them, and about nine hundred drawers of street numbers from 1911, and a bus past the end of this street every twenty minutes on a weekday.*

**And then he wrote the prices, and he wrote them in the two columns of nothing, one under each place, in about eleven seconds:**

*Going to the village is the only way I will find out what is in the drawer, because the only person who would tell me without my asking is a woman of fifty-four at the ninth desk on the left who told me on the twenty-ninth of September that a second question would get the same answer, and I have not asked her a second question and I am not going to.*

*Going to the drawer is the only way the village gets a number, and a number is the only thing in this city an objection can be filed against, and I cannot file an objection against a place that is not addressable, and a village with no correspondence address is not addressable, and the sentence at the top of the instrument is the office's own words and it says the district is not going to be maintained, and nobody has to do anything for that to be true.*

**He stood there and looked at the two of them and then he wrote one more line and the line is the reason he got on a bus at eight the next morning, and he is not going to improve it, and it is the fourth time in eight months he has written down the real reason instead of the reason he would have said:**

*I cannot know which one of those I need without going to one of them, and going is the choice, and I am going to the village, and I know what that costs, and I am not going to find out in four years whether going to the drawer would have saved her, and that is not a thing I can put off. **There is no instrument in this city for finding out whether the right errand was the one you did not do.***

---

## The wrong-shaped thing, which is the vehicle, and it is the smallest one in the book

**For eight months the only vehicle that has carried him out of this district on a Tuesday has been a white panel van with a rack on the back for mowers and a company name on the side in a colour that has gone the colour of the weather, and it belongs to a firm, and it has two stops and then a yard, and the man who drives it has said twice without being asked that it is not a day off.**

**A van with a rack for mowers is the wrong shape for a village.** It is the wrong shape in three separate ways and it took him about four minutes to work them out standing at the kitchen table and he wrote none of them down because they are the same shape as four other things this month and he is not making a row.

One. It goes where a mower goes and stops where a mower stops, and a mower does not care what is at the end of the lane.

Two. It belongs to a firm, and everything in it belongs to the firm, and he has been a passenger in it eight months and has never once been a passenger in a thing that was going somewhere for him.

Three. **It comes back.** It has two stops and then a yard and it does not wait either way, and he has said about it, in his own book, that it is not a day off, and a thing that comes back is not a day off and it is not a way of being in a place for four hours, and **this morning the thing that came back was a bus, and the bus does not come back on the same morning, and the bus goes eleven miles, and that is the whole of the difference and it took eight months and a Tuesday to find it.**

---

## Tuesday the third of November, twenty to eight in the morning

He was at the end of the street at about twenty to eight. There is about four hundred yards of it from his door and he walked it and it was dark and there was a man of about forty-five setting out her trays eleven minutes early and she did not look up and he did not say good morning, and **the fishmonger is not thanked and is not asked and was not on a list this morning and is not going to be on one.**

The bus came at about five past eight. **It is a city bus and it is not a day off and its timetable is a document he does not have and has not asked for and is not going to ask for, and two of the ones that go past the end of this street on a weekday go as far as that village, and this is one of them, and he knew which one it was because he has been on one of them twice and he did not look it up.**

He got on. He paid the ordinary fare and did not look for a cheaper way and did not consider one.

The page home is the three hundred and forty-fifth day. The token is cold on the three hundred and thirty-first. The plate is two hundred and eleven days old. The condition column is two hundred and sixty-four days and is not there. **A foreman of fifty-four is twenty-eight weeks exactly past four months and nobody is going to ask him.**

**The list on the facing page reads twenty-nine and it has not moved in one hundred and ten days, and an interval of one day is not a pair, and a pair is a fact about where I was and not about what happened, and if the count of pairs in this month is small and not large that is correct.**

---

## And what he was not, on that bus, and the reason it is not a rescue and it is not glad

**He was not pleased. He has written that down and he is not going to improve it.**

*Going to the village is not a good deed. It is the errand that a man does when he has decided, and I decided on Saturday night, and the deciding took about four seconds and there was nobody in the room, and the four seconds are the same four seconds as the yard and the top track and the errand about the village that I gave up on the twenty-ninth of October, and the difference is which way they went and not how long they took.*

*And I am not going to be told what I would do. If I go there and somebody in that schoolroom tells me what is best for a hundred and forty people, then I will have come eleven miles to be told what I already decided, and that is the thing the office did to eleven streets with a contract, and I wrote that down on the twenty-ninth and I have not improved it.*

*So I am not going to be given anything today. I am going to a room with about twenty people in it, and it is the only day of the week I have, and I am spending it there, and I know what the other one costs, and I am not going to find out in four years whether the drawer had anything in it about the fen country, and there is nobody in this city I am allowed to ask.*

**And the whole of the reason, which is the fourth time in eight months and the first time it has not been about a figure, in his own hand:**

*The sentence at the top of the instrument is the office's own words and this one came out not, and the eleven streets is in the third state, which is a road that is not maintained and was not maintained, and the difference between that and a maintained road is a line in a column in a building in Crown Heights. And in about nine weeks somebody is going to find out what it means to be a district that is not going to be maintained, and everybody in this city has been assuming that somebody means an office. **It will not be an office. It will be a place where about a hundred and forty people live and a woman of thirty-four teaches, and it will be found out by them and not by anybody in Crown Heights, and that is the only reason on this earth I am on that bus.***

*And I cannot ask a hundred and forty people and then leave. I have done that four times this year with a man, a boy and a man on a top track, and I did not do it to be cruel, and every one of them is on a list now, and one of them is sixty-one and cannot be asked and I cannot find out whether it was all right. **I am not going to be a man who sits in a schoolroom for four hours and comes away with the answer and leaves them the question.***

He got off at about ten past ten in a village that has no address and stood in the road with a bag he did not need to carry, because the fare was the fare and the fare was not about carrying.

**And a bell on a bracket over a door went while he was standing there. He did not write it down and he did not ask about it and he did not count it, and he has four bells he has never written down either, and the fifth thing is somebody writing down the four, and that is not going to be him and not going to be today.**

---
