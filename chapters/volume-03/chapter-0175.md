# Chapter 175: The Fourth Landing, And A Day That Is Worked From The End Of It

**The flatbed came in from the city on Friday the sixteenth of October at about a quarter to seven and was in Mireward at about half past ten, and he got off where the driver was going, and the driver had four sacks on the bed and did not ask him anything, and it goes back on the Friday afternoon and is in the yard there at about half past four and it does not wait either way.**

The page home is the three hundred and twenty-seventh day. The token is cold on the three hundred and thirteenth. The plate in the grass at the low end of Bridge Street is a hundred and ninety-three days old and has been told to nobody. The condition column is two hundred and forty-six days and is not there. **A foreman of fifty-four is twenty-five weeks and two days past four months and nobody is going to ask him.**

---

## She was doing the second one when he got there and she told him what she was going to do

On the second of October she had been on the third when he arrived, and he had held a painter for fourteen minutes.

**Today she was on the second, and he came up the cut, and she did not turn round and said:**

"**I am going to do the third, and then the first, and then the fourth, and if you are going to stand about you can stand about at the fourth because there is nowhere for you to be useful at the fourth.**"

He stood about at the second for about an hour and then walked up the cut with her to the fourth and stood there.

**And that is the whole of the change. She had done the same four landings in the same order on the second of October and she has done them in that order for twenty-six years, and today she is not doing them in that order, and he worked out in about four minutes why, and it is the reason he wrote the entry he wrote at eleven o'clock that night, and it is not hers and it is not his and nobody said a word about it on the bank.**

*A day is not worked from the front. A day is worked from the back, and you find out what it is going to cost you by working out where you are going to finish.*

**She is the first person Milo Rook has ever met who writes a route backwards, from where you are going to be. She did that in the spring, in about nine minutes, on the deck of a boat, and walked him in and out of the tenth crossing, and was not asked, and he has never said her name to her in a sentence of thanks and is not going to.**

What was new was that she was doing the day that way and not just the crossing.

---

## The fourth landing, and the four seconds, and the time he did not write down

The fourth landing is four miles up and nobody uses it. There is no path for the last half mile; there is a fence and a gate that is not a gate any more, and she went through the gap in it the way you go through a gap in something that used to be a gate.

He stood on the bank from about twenty past twelve.

The water was a foot lower than it had been on the second of October and about nine feet off the wall it is a different colour. There is a post in the mud on the far side with about four inches of green weed on the tide line, and she looked at it for about two seconds and then did not look at it again, and he did not ask her what the weed line meant, because the tide line is a tidemark and a tidemark is a tidemark and nobody has ever been able to get a figure out of one that is worth anything in about four years.

**At about ten past one the sound went.**

It went the way it has gone twice in eight months. There was no sound at all — the water against the hull, a curlew about two hundred yards up the bank, the wind in nine yards of wire, the flatbed's engine still turning a mile and a half behind him where the driver had dropped him and gone on his round — and none of it. And under it, in the same four seconds, there was the taste he has never put a name to and has never put a place to, and it came and it went in the same four seconds, and it was not the same as the silence and it was not in it.

Then everything came back and it was all exactly where it had been.

**He did not write the time down.** That is twice in eight months and he wrote that down on the Wednesday in September and has not improved it, and this time he did not write it down and wrote on the Friday night that he had not written it down, which is a man who writes down that he did not do a thing having still done the thing, and there is no form for that and he is not going to build one.

**It acquired nothing. It had no face on it and no place and no person and no name, and it did not acquire one. It did not become the boat and it did not become the weed on the post and it did not become the four landings and it did not become a woman of thirty-seven standing eight feet away from him with a painter, and it did not become the name of a water, and it did not become anything at all.**

A lost sound and a bell that rings are two different things and I am not going to write them in the same column.

**And he did not tell her.** He has a word for a water about two miles above a ford that has been dry since about 1919 and it is in this book and it is in his own hand, and there was a woman eight feet away from him who could have taken him to a place he has never been, and **if it ever becomes a thing I will write the name of that thing in this book at the time, and until then it is four seconds and a taste and nothing, and I am not going to be the man who gives a four-second thing a name, because a name is the only thing in this story that anybody can be asked for.**

---

## What he wanted to do on the bank, and did not do, and the reason

**He wanted to write her day down.**

That is the whole of the temptation and it is a small one and it is on the page because it is the same shape as every other one in this book and it is worth about as much. Four landings, in an order, on a Friday, in October. It would have taken him about eleven seconds and it would have gone in the book and it would never have gone anywhere else, and in about four years he would have had four places and a woman and an order, and a woman and an order is a party.

*A ferry route is a ferry route. It is not a map and it is not a schedule and it is not a return and it is not a document, and the twenty-six years she has got is not a record and the jar on the table at the top of a track is not a wage. **I have spent eight months learning that the only protection any route in this city has ever had is that it cannot be produced, and I have just spent two hours on a bank with the only person in this district who has a route I cannot draw, and I could have drawn it, and it would have taken eleven seconds, and I did not, and she did not ask me why I was not writing, and she is not going to.** A route that cannot be produced cannot be asked after, and every conclusion this district has produced in eight months has been that something was going to be found out about a person, and this one is that a thing is safe because there is nothing to find.*

---

## The recovery, which is not hers and is not a word

**The flatbed was on its way back at ten to four and he was on it and it did not wait, and he got into eleven streets at about half past five, and the bag of the round is not the round and the round is a Monday and a Thursday.**

He did the thing on the bus. Not a column. A thing, once, in about four minutes, with a pencil, on the back of a sheet of his own paper, and it took four minutes and it is the only method he has got that he did not make himself.

*Start from the end.*

Not from the front, not from the register, not from the corridor with the board on it and not from the machine with four machines in it. **From the end. From the place you are going to be finished, and work out what it costs to be finished there.**

He did it in about four minutes on the bus and it worked and it was not his.

*If I finish in eleven streets, then Mireward is out, because the flatbed does not wait and there is no bus and there has not been one since about 1961, and the round is a Monday and a Thursday and it goes out at ten past eight and comes in at about four, and a person without an address cannot afford the day off. If I finish in Mireward, then the round does not go, and the round is one man short, and it has had nothing in it since the twenty-eighth of August, and nobody would notice for about three weeks and then somebody would.*

**And then he wrote the two sentences underneath it that he is not going to improve, and they are these, and they are the shape of everything that is going to happen in the next three weeks and he knows it and has written it down and is not going to act on it tonight:**

*A day has an end before it has a beginning. I have been starting every day I have had since the second of January from the front, and a man of thirty-seven has been starting them from the back for twenty-six years, and she did not teach me that and she does not know she did it, and she is not going to come to eleven streets, and I am not going to ask her anything about it, and nobody is going to thank her.*

---

*Field Book, Friday the sixteenth of October, 23.40, a front room in eleven streets, one day off the fifteenth.*

*One. **The changed method is not mine and it is not a word and she does not know about it.** Four landings, twenty-six years, and she works the day from the back, and the first, second, third and fourth are the same four and the order is not the order she used on the second of October, and the reason is that a day is not worked from the front. **I have been starting every day I have had since the second of January from the front, and a man who starts from the front does not know what the day costs until it is over, and I have known what every day cost since April and none of it has been in yards.** I got the method off a bank in about four minutes and it is the only thing I have taken off anybody in ten days and it is not a sentence and it is not a rule and there is no facing page for it.*

*Two. **Four seconds, at about ten past one, at the fourth landing, and I did not write the time down, and that is the second time.** A sound gone and a taste come and no face and no place and no person and no name, and it acquired nothing, and it did not become the boat, the weed on the post, the four landings, a woman standing eight feet off, or the name of a water. **A lost sound and a bell that rings are two different things and I am not going to write them in the same column.** And I did not write the time down, and I have written down that I did not write it down, and a man who writes down that he did not do a thing has still done the thing, and there is no form for that.*

*Three. **I wanted to write her day down and I did not, and there is a reason and the reason is the finding and the finding is now old and I am not going to improve it.** A ferry route is a ferry route and twenty-six years is not a record and a jar on a table is not a wage, and a route that cannot be produced cannot be asked after, and I could have drawn four places and a woman and an order in about eleven seconds and I did not and she did not ask me why I was not writing and she is not going to. **I have now got three people in this district who have told me in under a hundred words that the only protection any of them has is that they cannot be produced, and every one of them is right, and I have taken all three and used none of it on them, and that is the whole of what a field book is for.**

*Four. **Nothing has moved.** Eleven remembered routes, ten crossings, the tally ten lines, Hettie Brann's paper in Dr Sen's pocket, not looked at since the thirteenth of January. The page home the three hundred and twenty-seventh day, blank, four marks in four hands, none of them mine. The token cold, the three hundred and thirteenth day, and it has done nothing since the seventh of December. **The list on the facing page reads twenty-nine and it has not moved in ninety-two days, and an interval of one day is not a pair, and a pair is a fact about where I was and not about what happened, and if the count of pairs in this block is small and not large that is correct.** The condition column is two hundred and forty-six days and is not there and the reason column is not there and no new figure is to be printed for either. The second tin is at thirty-seven and there is no thirty-eight. The plate is a hundred and ninety-three days in the grass and has been told to nobody. A foreman of fifty-four is twenty-five weeks and two days past four months and nobody is going to ask him. **The four unshowable things in five months are four and there has been no fifth and I am not counting and there is no total and there is not going to be one.**

*And the standing position: **the writer of the map is not the maintainer of anything, and that is a list of about fourteen other people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.**
