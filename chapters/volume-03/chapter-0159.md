# Chapter 159: The Last Morning Of A Contract, And A Week That Was Never Printed

**The gang was on the back length at half past seven on Monday the thirty-first of August, eleven men and a foreman with a clipboard, and it was the forty-third walk since the first of April, and there was no sheet on the clipboard, and there is no form in this city for a walk that is not on a sheet.**

The plate in the grass at the low end of Bridge Street is a hundred and forty-seven days old. The page home is the two hundred and eighty-first day. The token is cold on the two hundred and sixty-seventh day. The condition column is two hundred days empty. **The second tin went to thirty-seven in a building eleven miles away on Friday morning and it is not going anywhere after that and the tin does not know that and neither does the repair schedule it is filed under.** A foreman of fifty-four is eighteen weeks and five days past four months today, and he is on a road at half past seven and is not going to be asked, and that silence is on the facing page of this book in my own handwriting and I put it there and I cannot get it out of it.

**The barriers were already out when he got there, and the road was open, and both of those are correct, and a site is not cleared by a date.**

---

## The sheet

A weekly sheet is a printed form, and printed forms come off a machine, and the machine is in a room about a mile away with four machines in it and a man of about fifty in it who has been in that room nineteen years and has never once been asked to set a machine for one district.

**A sheet covers Monday to Sunday. It is a week. A week is a thing a schedule of works has to contain, and the schedule of works has run out, and so the week beginning Monday the thirty-first of August is not a week anybody has printed, and a machine cannot print a form for a period that is not in a contract.**

Roy Pryce had asked. That was the whole of what he said about it, at about ten past eight, to nobody in particular, with his thumb on the clipboard.

"**I asked on Friday. I was told there is no week after the contract. That is not the office being difficult, that is a machine, and the machine is right, and I have got a gang standing on a road at half past seven on a Monday because a contract says to the thirty-first and the contract does not know what day of the week the thirty-first is.**"

Nobody said anything to that. He had said it the way he says the four words in the condition column, and then he had gone and walked the length.

**And that is the thing in this month that was built for one purpose and is the wrong shape for another, and it is not the worst one, and the worst one is on a printed side in column six. A sheet is a record of a length of road maintained between two Mondays. It is the right shape for what happened on this road for nine months. It is the wrong shape for a morning, and this morning is a morning, and it happened, and eleven men were on it, and there is nowhere to put it.** A length is still a third kind of place and it is not a remnant and it is not a day, and I have not got a word for what it is on a morning like this and I am not going to have one by the end of the week.

---

## The column that is not there

**The condition column is not empty today. The condition column is not there.**

That is the sentence he wrote in this book in July and could not improve, and he has now been in a position to find out whether it was true, and it was, and it is worse, and the difference is now available to him in a way it was not available on the twentieth of July and he is not going to spend it.

A column that is empty is a column somebody could have written in. A column that is not there is a column that does not exist on any form in any building, and the two July entries are on a sheet in a rack four rooms above a fitter's shop and in a tin and in a second tin on a bottom shelf in a plan room in another building, and a foreman wrote in it twice, four words and a figure, on the thirteenth and the twentieth, and **I am not going to say one word about why the days in between were blank, and nobody else is going to be allowed to either, and the two entries are four words and a figure and they are not a fault and they are not going to be improved.**

He did not tell the gang that the column was gone. **He told the gang there was no sheet, which was a true sentence, and the true sentence covered about four of the things that were missing, and a true sentence that covers four things is a sentence that is being used, and he has written that down before.**

---

## The man of about twenty-six

The man of about twenty-six in a high-visibility jacket was at the north end at about eight with his hands in his pockets and did not ask for a sheet. Nobody asked him for anything. He stayed about half an hour and went off up the length the way he goes.

**I have not asked him what he does here and I am not going to and he is not a fifth of anything and he is not going into this book as a thing that means something.**

---

## The four hours

**The round went out at about ten past eight and comes in at about four, and it is one man short, and a man on the round walks the district in about six hours and comes back with nothing, and there was nothing to collect today because there was no sheet to collect.**

So he was on the back length from about ten until about three, in the ordinary way, and he was on it with the only other person in eleven streets whose name is on a printed side, and he had four hours.

**The seventy-eight words are these and he wrote them out on the Sunday night in his own book and has not said them to anybody:**

*I did not put your name on it and I have known since Friday morning that it was going to be your name, and I have had four days to say something and I have not said anything, and there is no form in this city for saying something about a box on a printed side in an office about a mile away, and there is no form because nothing in this district is a party to anything.*

**And on Monday the thirty-first of August, on a road, in daylight, with four hours, I did not say them, and I could not have said them, and I have been trying since about eleven o'clock to write down why in a way that is true, and there are four answers and every one of them is half an answer, and I am going to write all four down because a field book is not for the good one.**

One. **There is no form, and a sentence said out loud on a road to a foreman is not a form, and a sentence said out loud on a road to a foreman in front of eleven men is a thing that about nine people heard, and nine people is not a file and there is no column for nine.** But nine people is not nothing either, and the answer does not say so.

Two. **Anything I said would be about me.** Not about the district and not about a length of road. There is a typed name in column six of a printed side and the only sentence anybody could put beside it is one written by the man who has been in this district since the sixth of January and who has never once been asked for anything by anybody here, and about four years from now there is a man who can ask this room for the sentence and the room will produce it and the sentence will be mine.

Three. **A document is a door, and a door is what Barnaby Pike told me about on a top track on Friday the twenty-fourth of July in about four seconds, and he has not been thanked for it, and a woman of thirty-four told me on a Friday in July that a date in a child's head is not a fact about anybody until somebody writes it down, and a woman of thirty-seven told me on a Friday in August that she would rather it was a bell than a person, and a woman of thirty-one told me in a corridor on Thursday that a room can be asked for a card, and not one of the four has been thanked for any of it and two of them were never thanked for anything at all and have never asked to be.** And a file is a party and a party is a person and a foreman who is a party is a man who can be written to, and the only thing a department does with a man who can be written to is write to him.

**And three is the best of the four and it is still only half an answer, because I have no way of showing that a door is a door, and the man with the spade could not show it either and he has been not writing it down since about 1975.**

Four, and it is the one I did not expect, and it is the one that is on the page. **On Monday the twenty-fourth of August, in a yard, with about nine people in it, Roy Pryce said the whole of the argument out loud, and it was correct, and it was the best thing said in this district in eight months, and it is not in any file, and it cannot be put in one, and he has said that he is not going to say it again.**

**So the sentence exists. It is in a yard, spoken, remembered by about nine people, and that is the only place it is and the only place it can be, and there is nothing for a man with a book to add to it, and the adding would be the only part of it that was mine.**

---

## What it cost, and to whom, and nobody asked me to

**It cost Roy Pryce and he will never be told and he did not ask and he is not going to.**

He is fifty-four. He has been in charge of that gang for thirty-one years. His name is in a box on a printed side that says he signed a thing he did not sign, and it is the only name of any person that has been on a printed side about this district since the fourteenth of April, and it got there on a Thursday in about a minute and a half by an office that could not write to us and went to the document it could reach.

**On the last morning of a thirty-one-year contract, the whole of the record of Roy Pryce's last day in charge of that gang is a figure in a tin in a plan room in another building, and a figure in a tin is not a record of a man, and there is a man on this road this morning and a plate in the grass a hundred and forty-seven days old that nobody has reported, and not one of those is a sentence and not one of those can be put beside a name in a box.**

And the sentence that could have been put beside it was said in a yard to nine people, and there has never been a yard in this district that anyone kept and there is not going to be one now, and a yard is the only instrument in this district that has never once turned into a document, and I knew that in June and I have not used it and I am not going to be able to use it again this year.

**And that is the sixth thing of the other shape and the other shape has six in it, and the five that I have been calling my five have five in them, and those are two different lists and I am writing that down now because in about four years somebody is going to merge them and add them up and get a number that means nothing.**

---

## The plate, and the day

**I did not go to the low end of Bridge Street today, and there is no version of that in which anybody is going to be surprised about it, and I have not written down why, and that is now the fifth time I have not gone and looked at a thing I could have looked at.**

The plate went down on the sixth of April. It is a hundred and forty-seven days in the grass at the low end of Bridge Street. Nobody has reported it and nobody has been asked about it and that is a decision and it was made on the first of June and it has been told to nobody, and there are four people in this city who know it is in that grass and have said nothing about it, and one of them is me.

**This morning was the last morning there would be eleven men and a foreman and a road on this side of the bridge in this district, and I was on the road, and the low end is about a mile and a half the other way, and there is no longer any ordinary reason for me to be on that road at all after today, and I have written that sentence down and I have not written down what I did about it, which is that I did not go, and I am not going to, and the not-going is now about a place rather than about a person, and I do not know what the difference is worth and I have decided to stop finding out what it is worth.**

---

**And at about ten past three on Monday the thirty-first of August the round came in, one man short as it has been since the twenty-eighth of December, and the bag was empty, and there was nothing in it because there was no sheet, and the eleven men went off the back length at half past three and did not shake hands and it is not a thing that anybody does.**

*Field Book, Monday the thirty-first of August, 23.55, a front room in eleven streets, one day off the thirtieth.*

*One. **The promise. On the twenty-fifth of June, in this book, in my own handwriting, I wrote that I was not going to explain why the ninth crossing did not cost me a remembered route before the end of July. The end of July was the thirty-first of July. I did not do it on the thirty-first of July and I have not done it since, and I am writing now, on the thirty-first of August, that I am not going to do it, and that is the second time I have said so and the second time has cost a day, and I have not explained it and I am not going to explain it and nothing on this page is going to be a device. A promise kept in a book and broken in a book is not a promise and is not a break and is a third thing, and I have not got a word for the third thing and I have not had one since January, and I am going to stop needing a word for it.** The remembered-route list is eleven, the crossings are ten, the tally is ten lines, Hettie Brann's paper and Dr Sen's pocket, not looked at since the thirteenth of January and not going to be looked at in September.*

*Two. **The rule on the facing page. NO SEVENTH COLUMN BEFORE THE FIRST OF SEPTEMBER, in my own hand, written on the first of July. Tomorrow is the first of September. I am not going to draw a seventh column tomorrow either, and I have known that since about the middle of August, and the reason is not that a rule is a stop, because it is not a stop, and I said that out loud in a village in July to nobody. The reason is that I did not want the column and the twenty-eighth of August was when I found out that I did not want it, and it has taken me three days to admit that the reason I was not going to wait for the first of September was that I was hoping a month would make it correct. **A rule that runs out is not a release and it never was a stop, and the whole of what this month has cost me in one line of my own handwriting is that the most honest sentence in this book is on a piece of paper with a date on it and the date is tomorrow and the sentence is about a column I am not going to draw.** I am not going to be improved by anybody in a yard.*

*Three. **Nothing moved. The pairs are twenty-nine, and they have not moved since the night of the sixteenth of July, and it is the last day of a month and I have written an entry on most nights in it, and an interval of one day is not a pair whether or not either of the two days has anything on it, and that is the reason and it is the correct reason and it is the seventh time I have written it. Two sevens in about a fortnight was a coincidence in July and it is still a coincidence on the thirty-first of August and I have written nothing about it. The page home the two hundred and eighty-first day, blank, four marks in four hands, none of them mine. The token cold, the two hundred and sixty-seventh day, and it has done nothing since the seventh of December and it has been in a coat in a fenland and in a pocket in a machine room and it has not done anything anywhere. The condition column is two hundred days and it is not there. The reason column is not there. The plate is a hundred and forty-seven days. The second tin is at thirty-seven and the last walk of the season is on no sheet. A foreman of fifty-four is eighteen weeks and five days past four months and he walked a road this morning and nobody asked him and he is not going to be asked.***

*Four. **The four bells are four and I have not written them down and I have not told anybody, and there are four people who have not been asked whether it is four, and one of them is a man of thirty-seven on a step who said it once in twenty-six years because I said the nine, and one of them is a man of sixty-one on a top track who said he would not be there on the next Friday, and I went on the twenty-eighth and I did not go, and I have written that down and I have not written down what about.** A practice is not a place and it is not a day and four people doing a thing is four people doing a thing, and the last thing that has to happen in this district is somebody writing down the four, and I have not, and it is the whole of what I have to say about it and I am not going to improve on that sentence in a book at midnight on the last day of a month.*

*And the standing position: **the writer of the map is not the maintainer of anything, and that is a list of about fourteen other people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.**
