# Chapter 131: A Sentence Where The Machine Prints A Dash

It came out of the same machine as the other eight hundred and something, on the Wednesday morning, and Milo Rook was in the room when it did, and the man of about fifty was in the room as well, because the man of about fifty is in that room at about nine o'clock on a Wednesday and has been for nineteen years.

**A district returns card.** Single side, about nine inches by five, printed on the top half and ruled on the bottom. Four columns across the top: *district. rateable units. returns received. reason.* The bottom half is for the district's own use and has been for about forty years and almost nobody fills it in.

The card that came off the machine on Wednesday the seventeenth of June was for **MIREWARD**.

**Rateable units: nil. Returns received: nil. Reason: —**

And then, in the reason column, where the machine prints a dash, there were six words in a hand.

> *Asked. Nothing came.*

---

The man of about fifty read it. He has no name on anything and he has been in that room nineteen years and he transfers five figures off an allocation sheet with a pen every week, and he looked at the reason column of a card for about four seconds and then he put it down on the pile face up, which is not what he does, because what he does is put it down face down.

"You have not put that one over," said Milo Rook.

"No."

"That is the first one you have not put over in nineteen years."

"**It is the first one in nineteen years with anything in that column, and I am not putting it over, and if you want to argue with me about it do it at half past six and not in this room.**" He did not raise his voice. He has never raised his voice in that room. "**And take it out of this room and do not tell me whose it is, because I have got about nine hundred of these a month and I have never once been told whose hand is on one and I am not starting in June.**"

---

**And that is the whole of what the room did, and it is the whole of what the room is, and nobody was added to it.**

The machines in that building are still printing. Four of them, toward documents that are not going to be printed, and none of the four has been told to stop, because being told to stop is a document, and this is the district that cannot produce documents. About nine hundred returns cards a month come off that machine, and every one of them is correct, and the reason column on a card where nothing is wrong prints a dash, because there is no reason.

**There has never been a sentence in that column in nineteen years, and this is the first one anybody in this city has ever seen, and it is six words, and it is the only sentence anybody has ever written in that column, and it is not a reason for a nil. It is the reason a person is still asking.**

---

At about half past six that evening there were about nine people in a yard and there was no coffee, and it has been established that there is no coffee.

He said it out loud because a thing that has come off a machine in a room with a witness is not a thing you keep for a week and then say in a yard, and he said it in about eleven seconds and then stood there and did not improve it.

"**A card came off the machine on Wednesday morning for Mireward, and Mireward has been nil on every card since before that room existed, and the reason column on a nil prints a dash, and on Wednesday it had six words in a hand on it, and the six words are *asked, nothing came*, and nobody in this city can tell me whose hand it is, and I am not going to find out by asking a machine.**"

"Somebody has to go."

"Somebody has to go."

"Well."

"**And nobody in this district is going, because nobody in this district has been told to, and being told to is a document.**"

"Then it is not going to happen," said somebody, and it was the correct thing to say and it was said in the wrong voice, and Milo Rook did not improve the wrong voice either.

---

**And at about ten past four the same afternoon, at a gate in a high wall, a man of forty-one on no list at all was told the thing by a man who had come in on foot from Crown Heights, and declined it.**

"You have to go yourself, and you know it."

That is nine words. It is the ninth time a man of forty-one has said nine words at that gate since the twenty-eighth of December, and there is no column anywhere for the number, and the number is not going in a book.

"Somebody could send you," said Milo Rook.

"**I have been in this gate four times a day since the twenty-eighth of December and I have said nine words in it nine times and I have not once been sent, and you are not going to start, and if you do start then this gate becomes a place somebody is sent from and I am not going to be the reason that happens before I am forty.**" He shifted the handcart out of the way for nobody. "**You have to go yourself, and you know it, and you are not going to hear it from me twice, and I am not going to be the fourth person in this district to tell you a thing about your own handwriting.**"

Nobody wrote that down. **And the three things under the brick are three, and they have been three since the twenty-first of January, and the line on the front of the run-number card has not been taken off, and the third of them is a boy's blank card that has been blank since the nineteenth of January, and nobody has taken it and nobody is going to.**

---

**And the sheet is right about today, and it is the eleventh time, and for the first time the reason it is right is not only a shed at Cardew Docks.**

The second side of the sheet of who does what and on which day runs Monday to Sunday and travels in the satchel, and the round goes on a Monday and a Thursday and it is one man short and has been one man short since the twenty-eighth of December.

**On Wednesday the seventeenth of June it had a blank on it, and that blank is the eleventh one on that side since the eighth of April, and the sheet is right about it.**

The sheet has been right about every one of them because the only person who stood in the weather in this district on each of those eleven days was a man of about sixty at a shed at Cardew Docks, eleven minutes away, who has been there since before the war and who does not know a piece of paper exists and is not going to be told. That is still true today and it is not going to stop being true.

**And today there is a second reason the sheet is right, and it is a different kind of reason and it is the reason there is a second reason at all.** Six words came off a machine this morning from a district with no rateable unit, and the district has a hundred and some people in it in high water and nineteen children in a schoolroom and at least four boats, and somebody in it asked for something this spring, and nothing came.

**A blank on that side is a day when nobody stood in the weather, and the sheet cannot see eleven streets, and it has never been able to see a district entered as nil, and a sheet that is right about a day it cannot see is not a fault and is not a finding and is not the fifth thing.**

The four things in five months are four and they are still four. A rectangle of standing water on the twenty-ninth of January. A hundred and ten yards of frontage in the rain on the nineteenth of February. A plate in a field on the sixth of April, which is seventy-two days in the grass this evening. About nine people in eleven streets standing in the weather for nothing at all on the fifteenth of April. **It is two months since the fourth of them and there has not been a fifth, and a place that has no chair and no date does not get a fifth thing, because a fifth thing needs somebody to be surprised.**

---

**He did not go on the Wednesday and he did not sleep much, and on the Thursday morning at half past seven the gang was on the back length, eleven men and a foreman with a clipboard, and the reason column was empty and the condition column was empty, and the condition column has been empty since the twelfth of February and a works clerk of forty-six worked out in about four seconds on the eighth of May why a column that wants to know why is the amendment, and said it once.**

And a man of about fifty-eight came into the yard at about ten past eight with a rod and a chain in a bag and a pad under his arm, and he had been going out into Mireward on the first dry week of every summer since 1974, and the first dry week was that week, and he said so before he was asked.

---

**The Field Book, Wednesday the seventeenth of June, 23.20, a front room, one day off the sixteenth, and the run of nights is fifty-three and I noticed that about four days ago and I have still not written down why I noticed it, and the list on the facing page reads twenty-seven and it read twenty-seven on the sixteenth and it read twenty-seven on the twenty-fifth of April and an interval of one day is not a pair whether or not either of the two days has anything on it.**

*Seventeenth, 23.20. One day off the sixteenth.*

*One. **Six words.** A card came off the machine at about nine this morning for a district that has been a typed zero on every printed side in this city since before the room existed, and the reason column on a nil prints a dash, and on Wednesday it had *asked, nothing came* on it in a hand, and the man of about fifty put it down face up, which he has not done in nineteen years, and said he was not going to argue about it in the room. **A sentence is a thing a person can be sent to look at. I said that in a yard in April about a form in another building and I did not improve it then and I am not going to improve it now.** And the six words are not a reason for a nil. Every other reason in that column in this city is a reason that nothing is wrong. That one is a reason that somebody asked and nothing came, and it is the only sentence anybody has ever put in that column and there are about nine hundred of the cards a month and I have seen the column empty since I came into this building.

*Two. **The question, and it has three parts and none of them is about this district and the first one just got a sentence in it.** Why a region the Registry enters as stable is on a forecast of weather-driven seam events at all, on the stated ground that there is no maintained seam, no recorded loss, no resident witness and no schedule. And there is no resident witness, and there has not been one, and I have now got a piece of paper that is the first thing anybody has written about that district with an intention behind it in nineteen years, and the intention behind it is that somebody got nothing. **A place is not stable because nothing has happened in it. A place is stable because there is nobody writing down what happens in it. A man of about fifty has written that on a blackboard in a drainage section in about eleven words in February and I have written it here in my own hand and I am not going to improve it and I did not write the blackboard one.** The other two parts are where they were. There is no second outlook, a man of about fifty-one has been in a room nineteen years and has never known anybody who has seen one, and I am not going to ask him this month and I wrote that down in May. And there is an instrument a person cannot produce and I have a punch roll and a flag and a field book and that is half of the third part and half of an answer is not an answer.

*Three. **The blank on the second side, and it is the eleventh, and I have written down today that there are two reasons and not one, and the second reason is a district.** The man of about sixty at a shed at Cardew Docks is the only person who stood in the weather in eleven streets and that is still true and it is not going to stop being true. And there is a place about four miles and a bus away where somebody stood in the weather today and where somebody has stood in the weather a lot this year, and this district's sheet has been right about eleven Wednesdays for two months and half of the reason is eleven minutes away at a shed and half of it is that a card has never been able to see past a printed list of districts that is a hundred and forty years old. **A blank that is right about a day it cannot see is not a finding and I have written that down in May and in June and I am writing it down again in June because it is still true and not because I need it to be, and there are four things in five months and there are still four and the fourth is the fifteenth of April and a fifth would need somebody to be surprised and nobody in eleven streets is going to be surprised about Mireward because Mireward is a word on a card.**

*Four. **And what has not moved, and I put it here every night and I am not going to stop putting it here.** Ten remembered routes, ten, the original eight intact, none removed, all ten marked by somebody who is not me, and the ninth crossing's failure to cost one is still unexplained and nothing in this book is going to explain it. I have not crossed since the twenty-second of December. The tally has nine lines in it, the paper is Hettie Brann's and the pocket is Dr Sen's, and I have not looked at it since the thirteenth of January and I am not going to look at it in June and I have said that seven times now and improved it none of the seven. **The order is suspended and is not discharged and is not varied and is not withdrawn, and the public name is not on the street name index, and the classification of the eleventh is withdrawn and stays withdrawn, and the removed sentence cannot be restored and a desk has been asked twice and has not answered twice, and the matter stands over and is not adjourned and has no date and it is not going to be on the business of the twenty-fourth of June either, and there is a date in this district for the first time since the fourteenth of April and it is a date about a car and it is going to pass on a Wednesday like everything else in this district.** The levy is unchanged and not curable. A round is one man short and will be one man short in July. The plate is seventy-two days in the grass and nobody has reported it and nobody is going to and that is a decision and not a refusal and I made it on the first of June and I have told nobody. **I have not told Mara Quill about the season for ten weeks now and she said in a corridor in June that she is not going to ask, and a date is a column, and I have not asked her about the seventh of January since the fifth of May and I am not going to ask her in July either, and a woman of sixty-eight on nothing is not named in this entry and the count stands at eight and I am adding nothing to it in here.**

*Five. **And the man of fifty-eight, and I am putting him at the top of tomorrow's page.** He came into the yard at ten past eight with a rod and a chain in a bag and a pad under his arm, and he has been going out into Mireward on the first dry week of every summer since 1974, and he said so before I asked him anything, and I had not asked him anything. **A man who goes out in a summer and writes four lines about a drain is not an office and does not become one, and there has never been a district office for Mireward and there is not one now, and the district is on this city's returns because a return list is printed from a list of districts that is a hundred and forty years old and has never been printed from a list of offices.** He has a pad. The pad is in a drawer in Crown Heights from this year back to about 1969. Nobody has ever asked him for one of them. I have known about him since February and I have not asked him for one and I wrote that down in February and I have not asked him in four months, and I am not going to ask him on the Thursday for a thing I could have asked him for in February, and if I ask him on the Thursday I am going to have to write down why I did not ask him in February, and I do not have an answer to that, and I have not got one now, and I am going to go and get a cup of something and go out to a fenland with a man I have met once.

*And the standing position, which is the last thing in this book and which is not going to improve in a volume or in a year: **the writer of the map is not the maintainer of anything, and that is a list of about fourteen other people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.**
