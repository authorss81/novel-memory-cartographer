# Chapter 189: Nine People And A Shape, And Five Doors, And Nothing To Find Out With

**The round went out at about ten past eight on Thursday the twenty-sixth of November and came in at about four with the bag empty, and it is a Monday and a Thursday round and it has been since the second of January and it is one man short, and the bag has been empty since the twenty-eighth of August. **And this is the last Thursday of a month, and the return went to a woman of thirty-one in a room on a second floor about a mile from here at about ten past nine this morning, and November has four Fridays in it — the sixth, the thirteenth, the twentieth and the twenty-seventh — so the return she reconciled today has four cells, and December has four as well, and the fifth-cell rule is a rule and not a reason, and he was not in that room and did not go and is not going to.**"

The page home is the three hundred and sixty-eighth day. The token is cold on the three hundred and fifty-fourth. The plate in the grass at the low end of Bridge Street is two hundred and thirty-four days old and has been told to nobody. The condition column is two hundred and eighty-seven days and is not there. **A foreman of fifty-four is thirty-one weeks and two days past four months and nobody is going to ask him.**

---

## He went out onto the far side at about half past ten and the ground was not the ground

The barriers were still out on the back length and the road was open, and there was about two inches of grey silt on the crown of the low end of Bridge Street, and the silt had a hard edge about four feet in from each kerb where the water had stopped moving, and it is not going to be moved by anybody and nobody has asked them to.

**The path on the far side of the bridge is about three hundred yards and it is raised about nine inches and it was under for about three hours on Monday afternoon, and on Thursday morning it was dry and it was in a different place.**

That is the whole of the finding and he got it in about four minutes and then he spent two hours failing to do anything with it.

**It was not a distance. He walked it twice and he could find where the path had been, because the grass on the old line is dead and the grass either side of it is not, and dead grass in November in this city is a line you could put in a plan. It was not a distance because a distance is the wrong shape for what has happened. The top of the path is now in a different shape, and the ditch that takes the water off it is not the ditch that it was, because the ditch has cut about nine yards further in and joined a hollow that was two yards south of where the hollow was, and the two yards and the nine yards are figures I can put in a book and they are not the thing.**

**A length is a third kind of place and is not a remnant, and a length of frontage in a schedule of quantities is a figure about a set of works, and what has happened on that path is not a length. It is a shape, and there is no form in this city in which a shape can be entered, and the two yardages and the nine yards are the wrong size of information and I have got them anyway because I cannot stop myself getting a figure.**

---

## What the district had about that ground, in three pieces, and none of them is anywhere

**One. About nine people.** Nine people who live in eleven streets and can get from the low end of Bridge Street to the old ford and back without going round, and each of them can do it, and each of them does it a little differently, and none of them has ever written it down and none of them has ever been asked.

**Two. Two mornings.** A man of about fifty with a mower walked out about nine feet onto the far half of that bridge on Tuesday the sixth of October at about eleven in the morning, stopped, looked at the grass, and came back in under a minute, and nobody has ever asked him why and he has said twice that he is not going to say it again. And a man of about sixty with a shed eleven minutes away was at the end of a lane on Monday the ninth of November doing something to a length of chain that had nothing to do with a length of chain, and Milo Rook stood four feet off him for about ten minutes and did not ask him a single question, and wrote in his own book that he had wanted to ask him about the far side of the bridge and had not.

**Three. A field book.** Eight months of nights, a face on every page with a rule on the facing page that ran out in September, and on the pages for those two mornings there is a time and a place and the words *I was here* and nothing whatever else, because there is nothing else to put, and he has read both of them about four times each since Monday.

**And the three of those things were the whole of the district's own remembered-route material for that bank, and there was never any paper in it, and there is now about a foot of grey silt in the frontages and a path in a new shape and a ditch nine yards in, and the nine people each still have their own half and the halves no longer join.**

---

## Why the halves no longer join, and it is not because anybody forgot anything

He worked that out on the far side at about half past twelve and it took him about nine minutes and it is the truest thing in this entry and he is not going to improve it.

*They were never written down. **They were in agreement because they were all on the same ground.** Nine people and a hundred and forty feet of a bank and a ditch and a path and a fence, and every one of those was the document, and the nine of them had never compared anything, because there was nothing to compare — you did not check with the man next to you whether the ground was the same, you walked on it.*

*The ground is not the same. And the nine of them will each still be able to get across, because a person who has walked a place for nineteen years can walk a changed place, and two of them will get across easily, and four of them will get across, and one of them will not get across the way he used to and will find a different way and will not think of it as a different way.*

*And there is no way to find out whether they still agree without asking all nine of them, and the only reason they agreed in the first place is that nobody asked, and so the one thing that would tell me is the thing that would destroy it, and there is no instrument in this city that can do it any other way, and none is going to be built, and I am not going to be the man who builds it.*

**And the two that cannot be asked are these, and it is not sentiment, it is structure:**

**The man of about sixty with the shed eleven minutes away does not know that a piece of paper exists. Nobody in this district is going to tell him. He is not on a list and he is not going to be put on one and there is no instrument that could put him on one without somebody writing to him, and writing to him would tell a man of about sixty who has had four inches above that lane for forty years that a building about a mile away has a record of where he stands. He would want to know what it is for. He would be right to want to know. And there is no answer to give him that is not a sentence about a person.**

**And a man of about fifty with a mower has said twice, in September and on the sixth of October, that he is not going to say it again to anybody, and on Tuesday the tenth he said nothing at all when he was given nine words of a question, and he was right then, and he will be right on Friday, and the asking is the thing that would break a thing that is holding.**

---

## Five doors, and he knocked on none of them, and that is not a rule

He stood in the lane on the Thursday afternoon and counted them in about four seconds and did not go to any of them, and he wrote the five down that night and he is not going to write them again.

**One. A woman of thirty-four in a schoolroom eleven miles the other way.** He asked her one question on Tuesday the third and it was two questions wearing one coat and she said so and answered it anyway. **He may not ask her a second thing and there is no instrument in this city for converting a decision back into a question and he has not gone looking for one and he is not going to build one.**

**Two. A woman of fifty-four at the ninth desk on the left in a building in Crown Heights.** She said on the twenty-ninth of September that a second question would get the same answer, and he went up the thirty-four steps on Friday the twentieth and did not ask it, and the not asking is eight weeks old and it is hers and it is not a word, and she is not going to come to eleven streets.

**Three. A man of about sixty with a shed eleven minutes away.** He does not know that a piece of paper exists. See above.

**Four. A man of about fifty with a mower.** He is not a party to it and he has said so twice and been silent the third time, and he was on the far half about nine feet out on Tuesday the sixth of October and he knows the line on that deck better than any human being and he is not going to be asked about it by a man who has a book.

**Five. A woman of thirty-one on a second floor about a mile from here.** **He did not go and he did not telephone and he did not put it in a message, and on Tuesday he told her one thing and one thing only, and one thing was about water and the other was about a number he stopped giving out in August, and he did not tell her what is gone, because he has not got a way to say it that is not a price.**

**Nobody is to be told that it was the price of anything. Not the man of sixty. Not the mower man. Not the woman of thirty-four. Not the woman of fifty-four. Not the woman of thirty-one. Not a woman of sixty-one at a counter, not a woman of forty-six above a fitter's shop, not a foreman of fifty-four who is thirty-one weeks and two days past four months and is not going to be asked about any of it. And not himself, in a book that goes into a coat.**

**He wrote that down on the Thursday night in about four lines, and the four lines are: *I am not going to write the word because about any of this. There is a difference between a thing that happened and a thing that was paid for, and nobody in this street has been charged anything, and if I write because then I have turned a Tuesday into a bill and I have made that village into the paying party, and she is not, and I am not, and a Tuesday is the only day of the week I have and I spent it and it cost what it cost and the cost is not going to be on a schedule.***

---

## And what he gave up, on the far side, at about ten past one, standing up, in about four seconds

**He gave up the survey.** Not a rule against one. Not a promise. A whole season's errand, which was to walk that bank properly, in November, with a wheel and a chain and a pad, and get the shape of it and get the shape of it into a book so that a person in about nine years would not have to ask any of the nine.

He had built it on the Sunday in about eleven seconds and it had eleven items in it and he has not written the eleven down.

**What stopped it, in about four seconds, standing up on the far side of a bridge at ten past one on a Thursday afternoon in November, was this: a survey of a bank is a set of figures about a place, and the moment I have figures about that place the place is a place I have described, and a place I have described is a place that can be looked at, and a place that can be looked at is a place that can be entered in a column, and I have been watching that happen to eleven streets four times in eight months out of a contract and a schedule of quantities, and I am not going to do it to a bank with a sickle and a ditch in it.**

**He did not tear anything up. There was nothing to tear up. He wrote one line on the back of a sheet of his own paper and put it in the coat, beside another sheet, and the two of them are in an inside pocket together and a coat cannot be asked after and that is the only reason either of them is safe.**

*Nine people had a bank. I have a sheet of paper with a man in it and a sheet of paper with nothing in it, and I gave the survey up in about four seconds standing up, and that is the fifth time in eight months and I am not going to make a column of the four seconds and I am not putting it on either of the two lists and the two lists are two lists and the sum of them is not a number.*

---

## And the answer, which is that there is nothing to find out with

*There is nothing in this city that holds a route.*

*There are about nine hundred drawers of street numbers from 1911 and the named things in them are rateable things with a street on them, and there is no version of any of them that holds a bank. There is a schedule of districts that is a list of places costed by a length, and a schedule wants a quantity, and a quantity is a length, and a length is a third kind of place and is not a remnant, and what is gone is not a length. There is a punch roll about nine inches long that knows how many and not who. There is a pad in a drawer in Crown Heights with four lines in it about about nine feet of drain that a man of fifty-eight wrote once a year for about forty years and nobody has ever asked him for one and he is not an office. There is a roll in a woman's head of about twenty children on a Tuesday. There is a jar on a table with thirty-one years of wages in it. There is a handbell on a nail and a rope on a bracket and a bar and a bell on a post at a ford, and the fifth thing is somebody writing down the four, and that is not going to be me.*

**And the finding is this, and it is not going to be improved, and it is eight months old and it is still the best thing anybody in this district has produced: a route that cannot be produced cannot be asked after, and that is the only protection any route in this city has ever had. I have spent eight months working out that everything anybody here protects is protected by being unproducible, and on Monday afternoon a river came through a drain and moved about two yards and nine yards of a bank and the protection went, and it did not go because anybody was careless.**

**The water will be green over that path by the spring. The nine will all still be able to get across. What is gone is that nine of them were in agreement about a shape without ever having checked, and the only way to check is to ask, and the asking is the thing that would have made it a record, and a record can be asked for. I have got about a hundred and forty people in a flood and about nine people in a shape and the flood will be talked about on Friday and the shape will not be talked about at all, and neither of those is anybody's doing, and I am not going to be the man who makes either of them into a thing I am proud of or a thing I am sorry about, and I have written that down and I am not going to write it again.**

---

*Field Book, Thursday the twenty-sixth of November, 23.55, a front room in eleven streets, one day off the twenty-fifth. Two days in this month have no entry and I am not going to give them one, and I am writing down both reasons here so that a later block cannot turn either of them into a practice.*

*One. **The third of November has no entry because a day I spent in a schoolroom is not a day I had**, and there are four benches and about twenty children in that room and a lath with a line burnt on it and a roll in a woman's head that is not a sheet, and I asked her a question I had the answer to and I knew while I was asking it and she said it was two questions wearing one coat.

*Two. **The twenty-fourth of November has no entry because I said a thing out loud in a room for the first time in eight months and I am not going to write it down.** A thing you have said to a person is not yours, and a date is a column, and I have told her I stopped counting the weeks in August and I have not told her how many it was and I am not going to, and there is no instrument in this city for asking a person how many weeks it has been since a thing they did not tell you about. **She has not told me that she noticed in September and she was not going to and I am not going to ask her whether she is going to. There is no column for it and there is not going to be one.** And a page in a drawer with a coat in it is now in its twelfth block and neither of us said one word about it in about fifty minutes and it is hers and it is not going to be produced.

*Three. **WHAT IS GONE, AND IT IS NOT A FIGURE AND IT IS NOT A LIST AND IT IS NOT ANYTHING I CAN WRITE DOWN.** The district's own remembered-route material for the south bank: about nine people, two mornings, and a field book with two pages on it that say the time and the place and the words *I was here*. It was never on paper. **The ground moved about two yards and about nine yards and a ditch joined a hollow that was two yards south of where the hollow was, and it is not a distance, it is a shape, and I have the two figures anyway because I cannot stop myself getting a figure.** The path will be green by the spring and all nine of them will still get across and the agreement between them is gone, and the only way to find out whether it is gone is to ask all nine, and the reason they agreed is that nobody asked.

*Four. **Five doors and I knocked on none of them and that is not a rule and I am not going to make it one.** A woman of thirty-four and no second question. A woman of fifty-four and no second question and a second time of not asking on the twentieth. A man of about sixty who does not know that a piece of paper exists. A man of about fifty with a mower who has said it twice and been silent the third time. A woman of thirty-one on a second floor, and on Tuesday I told her about water and about a number I stopped giving out in August and I did not tell her this. **Two of the nine cannot be asked at all and the reason is structural and not sentiment: one of them does not know that a piece of paper exists and there is no answer to give him that is not a sentence about a person, and the other one has said twice he is not going to say it again and was right and the asking is the thing that would break what is holding.**

*Five. **The survey, given up on the far side at about ten past one on Thursday, standing up, in about four seconds.** Eleven items, not written down. A survey of a bank is a set of figures about a place, and the moment I have figures about a place the place is a place I have described, and a place I have described can be entered in a column, and I have watched that happen to eleven streets four times in eight months out of a contract. **It went on neither of the two lists. The six of his own are six and the six of the other shape are six and the two lists have not been merged and neither has a seventh and I am not putting this on either of them and I am not making a third list.**

*Six. **And nobody is to be told it was the price of anything, and I am not going to write the word because.** A Tuesday is the only day of the week I have and I spent it and it cost what it cost, and the cost is not going to be on a schedule, and nobody in this street has been charged anything, and I am not the paying party and that village is not the paying party, and **a correct decision has a cost and the cost does not arrive addressed to anybody and a man who puts a name on it has made a bill out of the only thing he had.**

*Seven. **Nothing has moved and nothing is cured.** Eleven remembered routes, ten crossings, the tally ten lines, not looked at since the thirteenth of January. The page home the three hundred and sixty-eighth day, blank, four marks in four hands, none of them mine. The token cold, the three hundred and fifty-fourth day, and it has done nothing since the seventh of December. **The list on the facing page reads twenty-nine and it has not moved in one hundred and thirty-three days, and an interval of one day is not a pair, and a pair is a fact about where I was and not about what happened, and if the count of pairs in this month is small and not large that is correct.** The condition column is two hundred and eighty-seven days and is not there and the reason column is not there and no new figure is to be printed for either. The second tin is at thirty-seven and there is no thirty-eight. The plate is two hundred and thirty-four days in the grass and has been told to nobody. A foreman of fifty-four is thirty-one weeks and two days past four months and nobody is going to ask him. **The last Thursday of this month was today and there were four Fridays in November and there are four in December and the fifth-cell rule is a rule and not a reason, and he was not in the room and did not go and did not ask and is not going to write down what is in a line she has never described to anybody in about two years.** The four unshowable things in five months are four and I am not counting them and there is no total and there is not going to be one, and nobody has stood in the weather on a Wednesday in November.

*And the standing position: **the writer of the map is not the maintainer of anything, and that is a list of about fourteen other people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.**
