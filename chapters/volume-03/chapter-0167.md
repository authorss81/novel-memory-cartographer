# Chapter 167: The Last Thursday Of A Month, And A District That Has An Address It Did Not Ask For

**Mara Quill reconciles the return on the last Thursday of every month and has done for about two years, and the last Thursday of this month is the twenty-fourth, and the return goes to a board on the first Thursday of a month, and the first Thursday of next month is the first of October, and Milo Rook has not been in that room on a last Thursday in about two years and was not going to be and was not.**

The round went out at about ten past eight on Thursday the twenty-fourth of September and came in at about four with the bag empty, and it is a Monday and a Thursday round and it is one man short, and the bag has been empty since the twenty-eighth of August. **He did the round's business, which is walking about a district that has nothing to collect, and he got back at about four and went and washed his hands and did not go to a records building and was not going to.**

The page home is the three hundred and fifth day. The token is cold on the two hundred and ninety-first day. The plate in the grass at the low end of Bridge Street is a hundred and seventy-one days old. The condition column is two hundred and twenty-four days and is not there. A foreman of fifty-four is twenty-two weeks and one day past four months and nobody is going to ask him.

---

## What a return is, and what happened in it, and who did it

**A return is a grid. It has a row for every district that reports to it and a cell for every week of the month. A cell is completed with a figure where the district returned a figure, and a reconciled return is one in which every cell carries one or the other, and there is no third thing and no blank, because a blank in a cell is not an answer, it is a query, and a query goes down.**

The eleventh street district is on the fourth page from the bottom and it has been on the fourth page from the bottom for as long as she has been doing this, because that is where it was entered and entries are not moved.

**Every cell on that row is a dash again this month, and there are four of them, and they were put in with a pen, and the pen was the wrong instrument and she knew it was the wrong instrument when she picked it up, and she has used a pen on this return once in about two years and the once was last month.**

And a dash is a departmental statement that nothing was received, and it is a statement about the post, and there has been no post since the twenty-eighth of August.

**She did not describe the line. Nobody asked her to describe it, and he did not ask, and he was not in the room, and a cell and a line are two different things in a return and paper is not a reason, and that has been the arrangement for two years and it is not going to change on a Thursday in the last week of a month.**

---

## The corridor, at ten past four

She came out of the room with her coat already on and the sheet was under her arm and she did not turn it round, and he had the round's bag over his shoulder, and the bag was empty, and the bag is not the district and she said so in August.

"**You have got a district with a correspondence address.**"

He did not say anything for about four seconds.

"**Say that again.**"

"**A district that can be written to is a district whose returns are held somewhere. A district whose returns are held somewhere is a district somebody can ask a card from. And on the Thursday before last a district returns card went out of a records office about a mile from here in a bundle addressed to a contractor.**" She was not raising her voice and she was not slowing down. "**I found it on Friday because a card that has been going round in a run since the middle of January has not come through my counter, and the only two places it comes from are a room with four machines and a satchel that went out with the works' papers, and the works' papers stopped on the thirty-first of August.**"

"I did that on Monday."

"**You did that on Monday morning. And I have been sitting with it for three days and I have not put a pen to anything, and I have worked out that I am not angry, and I would like to know what to do with the not being angry, because there is no box for it either.**"

---

## Three versions of a register, and a fourth one that is worse

"**You know what I did on the twenty-seventh of August.**"

"You opened a register of correspondents and entered nothing."

"**I went through it standing up in about nine minutes. Three versions and all three of them are unsafe. Enter it and in about four years somebody audits a register, because registers get audited, and the audit finds a district with an address and no replies, and the audit asks the district for a reply. Enter it for the fen country and the entry is for a district that has no such thing. Enter nothing and the district stays on one printed side of a district schedule as a row that is not going to be maintained, and that is the only line it has ever had and it is a line about stopping.**"

"**And you have now given it a fourth one, and it is the worst of the four, and it is not in a book, and it is a Thursday bundle going to a firm's yard.**"

"Yes."

"**Nobody audits a contractor's yard.**"

"**No.**"

---

"**Say the rest of it.**"

"**The rest of it is that a records counter is somewhere a department can be asked a question and a contractor's yard is not, and if the card comes back from that yard then a man in that yard has looked at it, and he will hand it to the only name in the file, because that is what the file is for, and that name is R. PRYCE, and Roy Pryce will carry it out to the district on his own time on a Monday, because he is a foreman of fifty-four and there are two hundred and fifty-two people in a building with no number and he has been in charge of that gang for thirty-one years, and he will know exactly what it is and he will not be frightened by it, and he will not put his name on it, and it will go back.**"

"**And then nothing arrives.**"

"**And then nothing arrives, and you will put another dash in a cell, and a dash is a statement about the post, and the post has stopped, and that has been true since the end of August and you and I both know it.**" She stopped. "**And that is the first Monday since the twenty-eighth of August on which the correct thing and the useless thing have been the same thing, and I have been in this room for two years finding the places where a thing that exists can be asked about, and there are now four of them, and you made the fourth one in nine seconds on a Monday morning, and you did not ask me, and I am not going to pretend that you asking me would have stopped it.**"

---

## The disagreement, the fourth time, and it is the same two sentences and neither of them is going to win

He said: "It is the only place in this city where an objection is a thing a man with a box can ask for in about four years."

She said: "**It is the only place in this city where an objection is a thing that exists.**"

"Yes," she said, before he had got to the second half, "**and I have said the second half for you, because you have said it four times and I have said the first half four times, and neither of us has a better one, and I am not going to have it a fifth time in a corridor with a bag over your shoulder.**"

---

"And there is a card in a run of about forty thousand," she said, "**with your name in the two inches at the bottom and a line through a paragraph, and it has been re-run every Friday at about four since the nineteenth of July, and tomorrow is the tenth Friday.**"

"I know what tomorrow is."

"**Then you know that a card with a thing struck through it is a card with a mark on it, and the mark is on a printed side, and there is nothing in this city that can get a machine to give a hand back, and the only way a hand comes off a card is if the card is not printed, and the card is not printed because a district with no rateable unit is not printed, and you have spent eight months refusing to make a rateable unit out of anything.**"

"And on Monday morning I made a correspondence address out of a bundle going to a firm, which is not a rateable unit and is not a register and is not a card, and which is the fourth door in this city that anybody can knock on, and I did it because I could not stand a week in which the only thing this district had was an absence, and because a man of about fifty in a room had nothing to do and I wanted to give him something." He did not raise his voice at any point and he was aware of that. "**And it is wrong, and it cost a boy of seventeen four seconds in a yard in front of nine people, and it is going to cost a man of about fifty in a room his findability in about four years, and I did both of those and neither of them was a map.**"

"**You did not do it for the boy.**"

"No."

"**You did not do it for the man in the room.**"

"No."

"**Then you did it because you are a man who writes things down and there was a week with nothing in it and you had nowhere to put it.**"

"Yes," said Milo Rook. "**And I have written that down at about eleven o'clock tonight and it is the truest thing in this entry and it is not a resolution and it is not going to be improved.**"

---

Nothing else was said about it that day.

**She did not describe the line. He did not ask about the drawer, and the page in the drawer with a coat in it is the tenth week in a row that he has not asked and has not been allowed to, and it is hers, and it has not been produced and has not been named and has not been confessed to, and neither of them mentioned it in one word.** He has not told her about the season and he is not going to and she has not asked and she has not asked since July. She found out he had stopped counting by noticing that the number had stopped going up, and she has not told him she noticed, and there is no column for that, and there is not going to be one.

**And neither of them was warm, and neither of them won it, and nobody in that corridor said so out loud, and a person who has thanked somebody is a person who has asked, and neither of them thanked anybody.**

---

*Field Book, Thursday the twenty-fourth of September, 23.45, a front room in eleven streets, one day off the twenty-third. Nothing has moved and the day-counts have gone on without me. The page home the three hundred and fifth day, blank, four marks in four hands, none of them mine. The token cold, the two hundred and ninety-first day, and it has done nothing since the seventh of December. The pairs are twenty-nine and have not moved in seventy days and an interval of one day is not a pair and a pair is a fact about where I was and not about what happened. Eleven remembered routes, ten crossings, the tally is ten lines, Hettie Brann's paper in Dr Sen's pocket, not looked at since the thirteenth of January. The condition column is two hundred and twenty-four days and is not there and the reason column is not there and no new figure is to be printed for either. The plate is a hundred and seventy-one days in the grass and has been told to nobody. The second tin is at thirty-seven and there is no thirty-eight. A foreman of fifty-four is twenty-two weeks and one day past four months and is not going to be asked.*

*And the standing question, in three parts, and the first of them has changed shape twice in ten days and is not a form any more. **There is no form in this city in which a district can say that something happened to it** — a man of sixty-one, a man of forty-four, a woman of thirty-four, and a department, and all four right, and no answer. **And this week I made a fourth door, out of a bundle, on a Monday morning, in nine seconds, and it cost a boy of seventeen and a man of about fifty and a woman of thirty-one who is not going to be told by me, and the reason is that I am a man who writes things down and there was a week with nothing in it.** Part two has not moved and there is still no second outlook and nobody has asked for one in writing since May. Part three is half an answer and has been since January and there is a fourth part under it I have not been down to. **I did not go down it this week. I have written that sentence more times than I have improved it, and I have improved it none of them, and there is a woman of fifty-four on the ninth desk on the left in a building in Crown Heights who has checked twice on her own time that there is no card in a hand index in this city for a funeral, and nobody knows she did it but the two of us, and she is not thanked and I am not going to thank her, and I have not asked her a second question and I am not going to.*

*And the standing position: **the writer of the map is not the maintainer of anything, and that is a list of about fourteen other people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.**
