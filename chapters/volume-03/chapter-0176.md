# Chapter 176: A Village's Name On A Printed Side, And It Is A Name And Not A Face

**It was completed on Thursday the twenty-second of October, which was a Thursday, and they complete them on the Thursdays, and it has been a Thursday thing for about eleven years and it is not a punishment and it is not a meeting. It came back into a building in Crown Heights in the afternoon and Cass Ardery had it on the table in a room with about nine people in it by about half past three.**

The page home is the three hundred and thirty-third day. The token is cold on the three hundred and nineteenth. The plate in the grass at the low end of Bridge Street is a hundred and ninety-nine days old and has been told to nobody. The condition column is two hundred and fifty-two days and is not there. **A foreman of fifty-four is twenty-six weeks and one day past four months and nobody is going to ask him.**

---

## What came back

A single side, printed on one, headed with the words that were on the other one and then two more:

> **SCHEDULE OF DISTRICTS — SEASON 4 (PROPOSED) — AMENDMENT 1**
> *Additional entries. Column 4: districts proposed to be withdrawn from maintenance. To be completed by the office. A district with no correspondence address will be completed by this office.*

And in the grid, in a typeface, in about the same minute and a half:

> *COBB LOCK — District 214. No correspondence address. No maintained works of record. Ground (d). Length of frontage 410 yd. **PROPOSED FOR WITHDRAWAL.***
> *Person responsible for maintenance up to the date of withdrawal: **none of record***

**Nobody lied. Nobody in that room did anything wrong. It is the same mechanism for the fourth time in eight months and it has now been the same mechanism on two printed sides and a board and an amendment, and every single one of them is correct.**

A district with a name in a list a hundred and forty years old can be entered in a column. A district with no correspondence address cannot be sent the amendment and cannot be asked about it, and Cobb Lock has had no correspondence address for as long as anybody can account for, and the office did the only thing available to it, which was to enter it.

---

## And here is the difference between the two entries, and it is the whole of what this block is about

He had the first of the two in his head since Thursday. He read the amendment twice and then he read the first of October's board in his head, twice, side by side, standing up, in a room with about nine people in it, and it took him about nine seconds and nobody in the room noticed him doing it.

**The eleven streets is on that schedule as a set of works, with a man's name in it.** A district number in the second column that is not a district number. A contract. A supervisor. **R. PRYCE**, in a remark in a darker typeface, on a board on the first of October, and in a box in column six on the twentieth of August, and in a box on a maintenance responsibility return on the seventeenth of September. Three times, and he signed none of them.

**Cobb Lock is on that schedule as a place, with nobody in it.** District 214. Four hundred and ten yards of frontage. A box for a person, and in the box, in the same typeface as the rest of the line: **none of record**.

He stood there and worked out what that difference was, and it is this, and it took him about four minutes and he did not say it out loud:

*One of these entries is a place that got in because a piece of paper existed. The paper is a contract. The contract named a man. The contract has expired. **So the entry has a way out of it that has nothing to do with anybody: the day that contract ran out, the thing that put eleven streets on that board stopped being true, and a schedule is a thing that is either maintained or not maintained, and if nobody starts another contract then eleven streets is not maintained by operation of the sentence and not by anybody's decision, and the eleven streets would come back the way a rack comes back, by somebody putting a sheet in it.** The other entry is a district. A district is a district for ever. There is no contract to end. Nobody has to do anything to keep it out, and nobody has to do anything to bring it back either, and the only thing that could bring it back is a contract, and there is nobody in this city to whom a contract could be given about a village with no address.*

**A thing that is withdrawn because a document ran out can come back. A thing that is withdrawn because it is a place cannot come back at all, and the difference between the two entries is one box, and the box on one of them says a man's name and the box on the other one says none of record, and the one with the man's name in it is the easier one, and I did not know that on Thursday and I know it now.**

---

## The room, and the thing Cass Ardery said, and the thing he did not say

"Nobody has done anything wrong," said Cass Ardery, in about nine seconds, at the speed he read forms out loud.

Nobody said anything.

"**A district with a name in a hundred-and-forty-year-old list can be entered in a column and Cobb Lock is in the list and Cobb Lock has got a number, District 214, and a number is not an address, and a name in a list is not a post, and those two facts have been true side by side since about 1911 and nobody has ever found it inconvenient.** The office could not send it and could not ask it and did not try to either. And there is no maintained works of record there, and ground (d) on the key is *no maintained works, nothing to insure against*, and the key is correct and I have read it and he has read it and there is nothing in this room to argue with it."

"And the person responsible."

"**There is no person responsible, and the form says so, and *none of record* is a true statement, and it is the truest thing on that sheet.**" He squared it. "**Compare it with the other one. That one names a foreman of fifty-four because a contract names a supervisor, and the contract has expired, and nobody asked that man anything. This one names nobody because there is nobody. In about four years one of those two entries is going to be the whole of what either place is on the record as, and I could not tell you from this table which.**"

---

**And the thing he did not say, and did not ask, and that nobody in the room asked anybody about either.**

At the foot of the amendment, in the position where the issuing line goes, there is a box with a rule under it, and the rule is for a name, and there is nothing on the rule.

**The office of the First Surveyor. A printed box with a rule and nothing on the rule.** It did not request anything and it did not receive anything and it did not answer anything, and nobody in that room asked who is behind it, and Milo Rook has never asked, and it is a box and it is a rule and it is a place on a printed side where a name goes, and it is not a person.

He looked at it for about four seconds and then he wrote on the back of his own hand, in pencil, *it is a box*, and then he did not rub it out, because there was no corridor after that and because a thing on the back of your hand is at least in your own hand.

---

## What is not in that room, and is not going to be, and is a name and not a face

**There is no face in it. There is no person from that village in that room and there is no person from that village on the amendment and there is nothing in the amendment but a place, a number, a yardage and four words in a box.**

*And the naming of a village is not the naming of a person, and I want that written down in my own hand tonight, because in about four years a man in a room is going to ask the room where the thing came from, and the answer is going to be a name on a board, and the name is a place, and a place is not a party.*

**Nobody in this room is frightened of anything.** There is a foreman of fifty-four's name in two boxes on two other printed sides and he is the least frightened man in this story about a piece of paper, and a second-in-command with a thing he classified in his own hand four years ago, and a man of about fifty in another building with four machines in it who does not know a piece of paper exists. **And a woman of thirty-four in a schoolroom eleven miles the other way is going to hear about this, or is not going to hear about this, and if she hears about it she is not going to be frightened either, and that is not a comfort to anybody, because a person who is not frightened is a person who has not been told.**

Nobody in that room is going to tell her. There is nowhere to tell her. A village with no correspondence address cannot be written to, and that is how the amendment got made, and it is going to be how the amendment stays quiet, and **nobody has done anything wrong and the wrong thing is going to happen anyway and it is going to happen correctly.**

And nobody thanked anybody.

---

*Field Book, Thursday the twenty-second of October, 23.20, a front room in eleven streets, one day off the twenty-first.*

*One. **THE PROPOSAL HARDENED TWICE IN THREE WEEKS AND THE SECOND TIME IT NAMED A VILLAGE, AND IT NAMED A PLACE AND NOT A PERSON.** Cobb Lock. District 214. Four hundred and ten yards. Ground (d). PROPOSED FOR WITHDRAWAL. Person responsible: none of record. **Nobody lied. A name in a list a hundred and forty years old can go in a column and a name in a list is not a post and both of those facts have been true side by side since about 1911.** It is the fourth time the same mechanism has run in eight months and it has now been on two printed sides, a board and an amendment, and every one of the four is correct, and there is nobody to be angry at and there has not been since the third of July.*

*Two. **THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE TWO ENTRIES, AND IT IS ONE BOX.** The eleven streets is on that schedule as a set of works with a man's name in it, and it got in because a contract exists, and a contract has a date and the date has gone, and so it has a way out of it that nobody has to do anything about. Cobb Lock is on that schedule as a place with nobody in it, and it is a district, and a district is a district for ever, and there is no contract to end and nobody in this city to whom one could be given. **A thing that is withdrawn because a document ran out can come back. A thing that is withdrawn because it is a place cannot come back at all. And the one with the man's name in the box is the easier one and I did not know that on Thursday and I know it tonight.**

*Three. **The reason for a thing is printed on a different form, in a different building, three days apart, and nobody reads both.** The key with the four grounds is on the second side of a district schedule in the middle of May. The schedule is on a board in a corridor. The amendment is a return about districts in which works have been discontinued, and Cobb Lock is not a district in which works have been discontinued, Cobb Lock is a district in which nothing has ever been maintained, and one instrument cannot tell the difference and the other instrument is in a drawer. **A schedule about places, costed by a length, entered from a contract, with the reason on a form three days earlier in another building. Five wrong shapes in one sentence and I have improved it none of them.**

*Four. **Nothing has moved.** Eleven remembered routes, ten crossings, the tally ten lines, not looked at since the thirteenth of January. The page home the three hundred and thirty-third day, blank, four marks in four hands, none of them mine. The token cold, the three hundred and nineteenth day, and it has done nothing since the seventh of December. **The list on the facing page reads twenty-nine and it has not moved in ninety-eight days, and an interval of one day is not a pair, and a pair is a fact about where I was and not about what happened, and if the count of pairs in this block is small and not large that is correct.** The condition column is two hundred and fifty-two days and is not there and the reason column is not there and no new figure is to be printed for either. The second tin is at thirty-seven and there is no thirty-eight. The plate is a hundred and ninety-nine days in the grass and has been told to nobody. A foreman of fifty-four is twenty-six weeks and one day past four months and nobody is going to ask him. **The office of the First Surveyor is a box with a rule and nothing on the rule and it did nothing at all today and nobody asked who is behind it and I wrote it on the back of my own hand and did not rub it out.** The seasonal outlook is the last one and there is no second outlook and nobody has asked for one in writing since May and the absence is the point.

*And the standing position: **the writer of the map is not the maintainer of anything, and that is a list of about fourteen other people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.**
