# Chapter 180: A Schedule Is Not A Work, And Monday The Second Of November Is A Monday

**The flatbed came in from the city on Friday the thirtieth of October at about a quarter to seven, four sacks on the bed, and it went back on the afternoon and was in the yard at the city end at about half past four, and it did not wait either way, and he was not on it, and he was in eleven streets all day, and being in a place is not a day off and a person without an address cannot afford one.**

The page home is the three hundred and forty-first day. The token is cold on the three hundred and twenty-seventh. The plate in the grass at the low end of Bridge Street is two hundred and seven days old and has been told to nobody. The condition column is two hundred and sixty days and is not there. **A foreman of fifty-four is twenty-seven weeks and two days past four months and nobody is going to ask him.**

## Four printed sides, and the four are the whole of the month

He did the count on the Friday morning at the kitchen table, standing up, in about four minutes, and then he did it again on the bus and got the same four, and **a count done twice is not a check, it is a habit, and a habit is a column, and he wrote that down and left it.**

*One. **Thursday the twentieth of August.** Column six of a district schedule, a box that says he signed it and he did not, and NOT TO BE MAINTAINED AFTER 31 AUGUST.*

*Two. **Thursday the seventeenth of September.** A maintenance responsibility return for Season 3, a second box with the same man's name in it, and beneath it the office at which returns for the district are held. A description of a place is how a person gets found.*

*Three. **Thursday the first of October.** A board in a corridor. A schedule. Column four, ground (d), in a darker typeface, in a line that does not begin in the name column because the eleven streets is not a district, with a contract and a supervisor's name and an expiry and two thousand three hundred and forty yards of frontage.*

*Four. **Thursday the twenty-second of October.** Amendment 1. **Cobb LOCK. District 214. Four hundred and ten yards. Ground (d). PROPOSED FOR WITHDRAWAL. Person responsible: none of record.** A village, a yardage, and a box with nobody in it.*

**Three of them name this district and one of them names a village eleven miles the other way. All four were completed by an office, on a Thursday, in about a minute and a half, and nobody lied in any of them, and none of them has been discharged or varied or withdrawn, and nobody has put a counter-statement next to any of them, and there is not going to be one.**

**The eleven streets has been on a printed side four times in seventy-one days, twice on a wall or a board and twice in a building, and it has been on a printed side as a thing that is not going to be maintained four times, and the first time since the fourteenth of April was the seventeenth of September, and the third and the fourth have both been inside nineteen days of each other, and it has never once been on a printed side as a place.** And every one of the four has had a figure in it, because a schedule wants a quantity, and a quantity is a length, and a length has to be priced before a season can be costed, and a district with no rateable unit has no rate and did not need one, because the rate was on the contract and the contract has gone. **The eleventh streets is now a sum of money as well as a row and a box and a name, and it is the first thing in eight months that has been about money, and it took a hundred and forty years of street lists to get here and nobody chose it.**

---

## And this morning the schedule took effect, and here is what happened

**Nothing happened.**

He wrote that at the top of a page and then crossed it out and wrote it again lower down, because the first one looked like a heading and he does not want a heading.

*A schedule of districts, season four, proposed, took effect on Friday the thirtieth of October. **A schedule is a thing that is either maintained or not maintained. This one says not.** And at about ten past eight on the Friday morning the barriers were still out on the back length and the road was open, and at about half past seven, which is when eleven men and a foreman used to be on that road, there was nobody on it, and there has not been anybody on it since Monday the thirty-first of August.*

***And there was nothing to withdraw.***

**That is the whole of it and it took him about four minutes at the kitchen table and it is the finding of the month and he is not going to improve it.**

*There is no works contract in eleven streets, so there is no gang, no sheet, no rack, no tin going anywhere and no satchel, and there is no apparatus on that road that anybody maintains, and there has not been one since the spring. **A district with nothing to maintain cannot be withdrawn from maintaining it.** The office entered it off a contract that expired on the thirty-first of August, and the contract has gone, and the thing the contract was maintaining is not there any more, and so the withdrawal has nothing to act on and it is correct and it is not curable and it is not a fault.*

**And it is not the same as being maintained. Those are two things, and this district is now in the third, and there is no column in this city for the third.**

*A schedule is either maintained or not maintained. **In practice a schedule has three states and it is a printed instrument with two of them on it, and the third is a road that is not maintained and was not maintained, and the difference between that road and a maintained road is a line in a column in a building in Crown Heights and nothing whatever on the ground, and that difference is now the whole of what this district is, and it is a line, and I cannot produce a line.** A document that says a thing is going to happen is not the thing happening, and this city has been printing them for a hundred and forty years, and a dash is not a zero, and an absence is not a form, and **a withdrawal of nothing is not a maintenance** — and there is no form anywhere in this city for the difference between a road that is looked after and a road that has been written off, and I have looked, and it is not the same form as the other four wrong-shaped things in this book because the other four were wrong about a person and this one is wrong about a place, and I do not know which of those is worse and I am not going to find out in October.*

## Monday the second of November is a Monday

**The gang was on Mondays and Thursdays at half past seven. The weekly sheet covered Monday to Sunday. The sheet went into the rack on the Friday, the duplicate into a tin, and the duplicate into a second tin on the bottom shelf of the plan room of the Civic Atlas Office under a repair schedule. The last walk of the season was Monday the thirty-first of August, the forty-third since the first of April, and it was on no sheet at all. The last rack day was Friday the twenty-eighth. The second tin is at thirty-seven and there is no thirty-eight.**

**Monday the second of November is a Monday and the round goes out at ten past eight and it is one man short and it will have an empty bag, and there will be nobody at half past seven on the back length, and the rack is not a rack day, and the tin is not a tin that is going anywhere, and the repair schedule is a repair schedule and nobody has asked when it ends and I am not going to ask.**

And the barriers will still be out and the road will still be open, and both of those are correct.

## The standing question, in three parts, on the last night of the month

*Field Book, Friday the thirtieth of October, 23.20, a front room in eleven streets, one day off the twenty-ninth.*

**Why does weather memory threaten a region the Registry claims is stable.**

**(a) It is not a form and it is not a practice and it is not a place and it is not a day. It is a kept day, in about 1911, that somebody printed a side about, and a column, and forty people wrote their names in it in one hand, and the sheet became the account, and the account wanted one account, and the part of the mourning that would not go into one account went out of the record, and out of the record is the weather, over a fen, on a water called Rainway.**

*A place is not stable because nothing has happened in it. **A place is stable because there is nobody writing down what happens in it.** And when a department finally does write down a mourning the mourning does not stop, it changes weather — and the department cannot write a mourning down again, because there is no form for one, and a district with no form for a loss is a district with nothing to report, and a district with nothing to report is entered as stable, and a district entered as stable is a district that cannot be frightened, and a district that cannot be frightened is a district where there is nothing to insure against, and that is ground (d) on a key printed on the second side of a printed schedule in the middle of May, **and in October that ground has been used to write down a village of a hundred and forty people, and the key is correct, and I have got nothing against the key, and there is nobody to be angry at, and there has not been since the third of July, and it has got worse every week since, because every week the machinery does something correct.***

---

**(b) There is no second outlook. Unmoved.**

**The seasonal outlook is the last one. A man of about fifty has been in a room nineteen years and has never known anybody who has seen a second one. Nobody has asked for one in writing since May. I am not going to put a figure on how long it has been, because a figure is a column, and this is the only part of anything in this book that has not moved at all, and I have stopped finding that satisfying, and I have not stopped noticing it, and I am not going to go and ask a man about it, because I have used that man once already this month and he does not know it.**

---

**(c) An instrument a person cannot produce. Half an answer, and the eighth of the eight is a word.**

*A punch roll about nine inches long that knows how many and not who. A yard man's flag and a book of times. A field book. A pad with four lines in it in a drawer in Crown Heights that nobody has rung up about since 1969. A roll of nineteen children in a woman's head. Three dates in three heads, none of them given. The smell of a fen in a dry week, which is the strongest of them and the one thing a person produces and cannot produce on demand. **And a name on the outside of a wrapper, in a hand that is not a hand in that room, in about 1911, on a water about two miles above a ford that has been dry since about 1919, and nobody in this district has ever used the word in a conversation and a woman of fifty-four has been in a room for four years and has never heard it said.***

**And what I have added to the eight in nineteen days is not a ninth instrument. It is four sentences about two figures.**

*Two thousand three hundred and forty yards for a district, and four hundred and ten for a village, and both of them are lengths of somebody's works or lengths out of a list from 1911, and **a length is a third kind of place and is not a remnant, and a length of frontage in a schedule of quantities is a figure about a set of works, and no one on any board in this city can tell the difference, and neither can I, and I have not asked, and I am not going to ask.** A schedule wants a quantity. A quantity is a length. That is not the design working against anybody. **That is a quantity.** And the same fact, on a different morning, seen by a woman of thirty-one at a records counter about a mile from where that schedule was drafted, who put five dashes in five cells on the fourth page from the bottom with a pen and initialled a cross-reference in four seconds, and who is right, and who could not do one single thing about it, and who has not described a line to anybody in about two years and is not going to.*

**And the finding, unimproved, for the ninth time, and I am not going to improve it this time either: a route that cannot be produced cannot be asked after, and that is the only protection any route in this city has ever had, and every conclusion this district has produced in eight months has been that something was going to be found out about a person, and this one is that a thing is safe because there is nothing to find. And there is no form for a word in a coat and he is not going to build one, and a name in a coat does not make it rain and does not stop it raining, and I have had it since about eleven o'clock on Tuesday the twenty-ninth of September and it has been in the same place as a schedule since Thursday the first of October and I have not put them next to each other, and the reason they were in the same place is that I was in the same place.**
