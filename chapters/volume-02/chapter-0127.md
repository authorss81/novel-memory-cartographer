# Chapter 127: Thirty-Nine Minutes

On Wednesday the tenth of June, the second side of the sheet of who does what and on which day had a blank on it for the tenth time since the eighth of April, and the sheet was right about it, and there is no column on that paper for a man who is somewhere else. **The round goes on Mondays and Thursdays and it does not go on a Wednesday, and everything below this happened on the Thursday, which was a walk day as well, and eleven men had been down six hundred yards at a quarter past seven.**

**Nobody in eleven streets stood in the weather.** On that Wednesday, and on the twenty-seventh of May, and on the third of June, the only person who stood in the weather in this district was a man of about sixty at a shed at Cardew Docks, and the grass on the far side of a fence at the low end is being cut by somebody who is not the works and not the council, and about nine people in eleven streets had nothing to do in the rain and did not invent something to do, and there is no form in this city for a day on which nothing was done in a street.

---

**And the round was thirty-nine minutes late on the Thursday, and here is the whole of it, and it took about eleven minutes to establish and nobody asked for it.**

Seven stops in eleven streets on a Monday and a Thursday, and a handcart through the quay section gate, and a boy of seventeen, and at about one o'clock on the Thursday the gate was against a lorry with a chain across it and a man in a yellow jacket on the other end of the chain who did not know the district and could not have known the district.

"You are not on the sheet," said Milo Rook, at about two that afternoon, at a counter.

"The round is not late on the sheet." Hettie Brann did not look up. "**The sheet is who does what and on which day. It is not a clock. There is no column on that piece of paper, or on any piece of paper in this city, for a round that was late, and I am not going to invent one, and the second side is going to have a blank on it for Wednesday at the bottom of this week and the sheet will be right about that blank, because what is not on the sheet is not a fault of the sheet.**"

"He was thirty-nine minutes."

"He was thirty-nine minutes because a lorry came across a gate that is the only gate a cart can get out of on a working day, and there is nobody to send with him, and there has not been since the twenty-eighth of December, and there is not going to be in June and there is not going to be in the fourth year." She put a return down. "**And I am not going to be the one who fixes it, and I want that written down as a refusal and not as a complaint, because I have thought about it for about a minute and a half and the minute and a half is the whole of what I have for it.**"

---

**And then she refused the thing he had actually come in with, and she refused it in about nine seconds and she was right, and it was the second refusal in two days and neither of them was him asking.**

He put the second return on the counter, the one that came back in the envelope on Monday morning, the one with the twenty-fourth of June on it.

"I am not taking that behind this counter," she said.

"It is only paper. It is the same paper the other one is on."

"**It is a document and a document behind this counter makes this counter a repository, and a repository is a place, and I have three pieces of paper face down under here that a woman of sixty-one counted out loud in a yard in April in front of about nine people and said *it is three*, and if a tram authority's return lives under this counter then the three are in a place with a return in it, and a thing in a place can be chased, and the second that anybody in this city chases a return in this district somebody is going to sweep.**"

"You are protecting three pieces of paper by refusing a form."

"I am protecting three pieces of paper by refusing a form, and I have thought about it for a minute and a half, and there is a man of thirty-one in this city who has put a form about a round on the top of a table in his own office where anybody can walk in and pick it up, and he did that on Friday morning on purpose, and I have read about it because you told me in a corridor and not in a yard, and I want to say one thing about that and then I am going back to four returns." She did not put the return down and she did not push it away either. "**You left yours where it can be taken. I am not going to bring mine here where it cannot. Those are not the same decision and one of us is going to be wrong about it in about four years and I do not know which and I am not going to find out in a minute and a half.**"

He took it back. Nobody thanked her, and she would have told him not to.

---

**And at about ten past four, in a yard, a man of fifty-four with a clipboard said the thing nobody in this district has said out loud in two months and had been going to say since December.**

The gang had been on the length on Thursday and eleven men had gone down six hundred yards and the condition column on the weekly sheet was empty and there is no column on that sheet that wants to know why a column is empty.

"Two months," said Roy Pryce.

"Past four months."

"**Two months past four months.**" He put the clipboard on the trestle table. "In December a man of thirty-four said that in about four months a fella in an office is going to ask me whether I saw thirty-one persons, and four months off the twenty-second is the twenty-second of April, and it is the tenth of June, and nobody has asked me, and I want to say the part of it that I have not said to anybody and I am going to say it once."

Nobody had a pen out.

"Nobody has asked me and nobody in this district is going to ask me and that has been done on purpose and it is not my doing and it is not theirs, it is this yard, and I know the difference between the two and I am not going to be the one to say it out loud a second time." He picked the clipboard up. "**A thing that has never been asked about is not protected. It is just not yet asked about. And in about four years a man with a box is going to ask me, and I am going to have to say yes, and the yes is going to be about a length and not about a morning, and I have had two months to think about which of those two I am going to answer and I have not decided and I am not going to be asked about it either.**"

Milo Rook did not count the words. He noticed that he did not count the words, and he wrote that down on the following night and not on the afternoon, and that is the smallest thing that has happened to him in seven months and it is the only one he has not told anybody about, and telling nobody about it is going to be the eighth of his own habits.

---

**And the cost of it, the whole cost, said once, out loud, at about half past four, by a man of forty-one on no list at all, and it is the seventh time he has said a version of it and it is the first time he has said all of it.**

"A boy of seventeen does seven stops in eleven streets on a Monday and a Thursday and takes a handcart through a gate in a high wall, and there is nobody to send with him, and there has not been since the twenty-eighth of December." He did not look at the gate. "**And a round is carried by a postman, and a postman is on a form, and the form wants a depot, and a depot is an address, and this district has no rateable unit and no address and has not had one since the eleventh of October six years ago when a street name came off an index. So the district cannot have a second man on its round, and that is not an injustice, and nobody has done anything wrong, and it is a design, and every word of the standing order is a reason a boy of seventeen is doing two rounds on a cart.** It will be one man short in July and in August and it will be one man short in the fourth year, and it cannot be cured from inside this district, and it is not going to be paid by a woman of about thirty-four and it is not going to be paid by a woman of sixty on a second landing, and it is not going to be paid by anybody in this yard, and it is being paid by a boy of seventeen at about ten minutes to five on a Thursday with a handcart, and there is no column for it, and the sheet is right."

And nobody wrote it down, and the number of times that has happened stopped being written down in this book on the twentieth of May.

---

**The Field Book, Thursday the eleventh of June, 23.10, a front room, one day off the tenth, and I am writing the tenth at the top of this entry because the eleventh is a working day and there is nothing in the eleventh that I have not already got, and the first of the two is the whole of these three days and the second is a walk.**

*Eleventh, 23.10. One day off the tenth.*

*Tenth, 22.20. One day off the ninth.*

*One. **A round that is one man short cannot show a shortage on a form, and the sheet is not late, and the sheet is right, and the lorry is not the finding and the boy is not the finding and the gate is not the finding.** A woman of sixty-one worked the whole of it out in about a minute and a half standing at a counter with four returns in front of her: there is no column on the sheet, or on any piece of paper in this city, for a round that was late, and a thing that is not on the sheet is not a fault of the sheet. **And the cost, said out loud in a yard at about half past four by a man of forty-one on no list at all, for the seventh time and the first time in full: a round is carried by a postman and a postman is on a form and the form wants a depot and a depot is an address, and this district has no rateable unit and no address and has not had one since the eleventh of October six years ago.** So it is not an injustice, nobody has done anything wrong, it is a design, it is not curable from inside here, and it is being paid at about ten minutes to five on a Wednesday by a boy of seventeen with a handcart, and there is no column for it. **And I have not joined the two reasons, and there are two, and they are not joined, and I wrote that down in a book in a coat in May and I have not joined them in June either and I am not going to before the end of this.**

*Two. **And the refusal, and it was hers, and it was in about nine seconds and she was right, and it was the second refusal in two days and neither of them was me asking for anything.** I took a return for the quarter ending on the thirtieth of June to a counter and she would not have it behind the counter, and the reason is that a document behind that counter makes the counter a repository, and a repository is a place, and there are three pieces of paper face down under it that a woman counted out loud in a yard in April and said *it is three*, and a thing in a place can be chased, and the second anybody in this city chases a return in this district somebody is going to sweep. **And then she said the half of it that is the finding of the whole three days, and she said it in front of four returns: you left yours where it can be taken, and I am not going to bring mine where it cannot, and those are not the same decision, and one of us is going to be wrong about it in about four years and I do not know which and I am not going to find out in a minute and a half.** I have not thanked her and she would have told me not to and she has told me not to, in a different way, three times. That is the eleventh time somebody in this district has refused me in seven months and been right and I have kept no list of the eleven and I have written down tonight that I have kept no list, and a list of them is a column.

*Three. **Two months past four months.** A man of fifty-four said in December that in about four months a fella in an office was going to ask him whether he witnessed thirty-one persons, and four months off the twenty-second of December is about the twenty-second of April, and it is the eleventh of June, and nobody has asked him, and nobody in this district is going to ask him, and it has been done on purpose and it is the yard's doing and not his and not mine. **A thing that has never been asked about is not protected, it is just not yet asked about** — and a works clerk of forty-six has said that about a different box in a different building and neither of them has the other one's. In about four years a man with a box is going to ask him, and he is going to have to say yes, and the yes is going to be about a length and not about a morning, and he has had two months to decide which of the two he is going to answer and he has not decided and he is not going to be asked about it either. **And I did not count the words.** He has said nine words to me on two occasions and both of them were in a yard in May, and I have counted both of them, and I counted one on the second of May and I counted one on the seventh, and this afternoon there were a great many more than nine and I stood there and did not count them, and I noticed at about ten to five that I was not counting and I have not written down what that is and it is not a discipline and it is not a rule and it is the smallest thing that has happened to me in seven months and nobody in this district knows it happened and I am not going to tell anybody, and the not-telling is now the eighth of my own habits and I have not counted the other seven and I am not going to, and none of the eight is a resolution.

*Four. **The fourth thing in five months, and the tenth blank.** The second side had its tenth blank on Wednesday and the sheet is right about it and about all ten, and nobody stood in the weather in eleven streets on any of them, and the man of about sixty at the shed does not know a piece of paper exists and is not going to be told, and the coffee man has still not been told that he is not on a Wednesday, and the man of about forty sweeps at ten past six and has not asked the man he sweeps for. **There are four things in five months in this district that cannot be shown to anybody and this is not a fifth, and the four are a rectangle of standing water in January, a hundred and ten yards of frontage in the rain in February, a plate in a field in April, and about nine people standing in the weather for nothing on the fifteenth of April, and I have written that sentence in three books in a row and I am not going to write it a fourth time in a yard and there is nothing in this entry about any of the four and there is going to be nothing about them in the next one either.**

*Five. And the rest, carried and not moved and not improved. **Ten remembered routes, ten. Nine lines in the tally and I have not looked at it since the thirteenth of January and I am not going to look at it in June. The token cold. The page home the hundred and ninety-ninth day, blank, four marks in four hands and none of them mine.** The run number has not moved since the twenty-fifth of April. Three things under the brick. Tern is not found and the twenty-two days are unasked and unsaid. **The order is suspended and not discharged and the name is not on the street name index and the classification stays withdrawn and the removed sentence cannot be restored and a desk has been asked twice and has not answered twice, and the matter stands over and is not adjourned and has no date and is on nothing, and this district now has a date about a car and not a chair, and the twenty-fourth of June is eight days off.** The second tin held twenty-five on Friday the fifth and will hold twenty-six on Friday the twelfth. I have added nothing in these three days about a woman of sixty-eight on nothing and the count stands at eight and her name is not written in this entry.

*And the standing position, which does not improve: **the writer of the map is not the maintainer of anything, and that is a list of about fourteen other people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.**
