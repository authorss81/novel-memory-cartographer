# Chapter 126: A Quarterly Thing Arrives Twice

On Monday the eighth of June, at about half past eleven in the morning, the envelope came back.

It is the same envelope. It is a tram authority's district office envelope and it went out on Wednesday the twentieth of May into the Friday post with the flap down and nothing written on the outside of it, and the letter inside it had asked for a number by the twenty-first of May, and he had not written a number and had not written a refusal, and there is no form anywhere in this city for putting a letter back a day early.

**It came back with a date stamp across the front and nothing else on the outside of it.** There is no word on it. It is not stamped *returned* and it is not stamped *not stated* and there is no letter in it and no note and no initials and no enclosure letterhead. The flap has been sealed again by somebody with a wafer, and a wafer is a thing you cannot see and cannot argue with, and the district was not told that the waiting had stopped. **It is nineteen days from the Friday post to the wafer, and nobody in this district expected it in nineteen days, and a man of fifty-eight with a spade has been wrong about a date before and has not been told he was wrong this time either.**

Inside the envelope, on the authority's own paper, was a second printed sheet, and it was the same form.

> **CAR 214 — LINE THREE. RETURN OF SERVICE PARTICULARS.**
> *Quarter ending the thirtieth of June.*
> *1. This office is closing the return for the quarter ending. Item 6, the number of persons carried, is required for the working of 07:52 off Crown Heights on **Tuesday the ninth day of June**.
> *2. The roll for that working is accounted for and is not in dispute.*
> *3. **The district is asked to state in writing the number of persons carried on that working.**
> *4. A reply is requested by **the twenty-fourth day of June**. If no reply is received the item will be returned as not stated and the quarter will close with the item standing open.*

---

**And what he had in his hands at half past twelve that afternoon was two pieces of paper, and the finding is about four seconds long and it took him until the following morning to be able to write it down.**

Both of them are the same form. Same six boxes, same six item numbers, same heading, same office, same car number, same time off the same place. **Item 6 on the first is the number of persons carried on a working in December. Item 6 on the second is the number of persons carried on a working in June. Neither of them mentions the other one. There is no column on that form for *the same section was not completed last quarter*, and there is no reference, and there is no box, and the second one does not know the first one happened.**

"They are in the same file," he said, on Tuesday morning, to nobody, in a front room. "**That is the part I did not expect and it is the part that matters. They are not two pieces of paper that have been sent to a district and lost. They are two pieces of paper in one file in one drawer in one room, eleven weeks apart, and a person who opens that file in about four years gets both of them and both of them are correct.**"

And then the wrong shape, which is the tenth time in seven months that a thing somebody built for one purpose has come out the wrong shape for another, and it is the oldest thing in this district and it is the plainest:

**A quarterly thing arrives twice. The return is not a record of a quarter. The return is a form that is issued at the front of one and closed at the back of it, and the closing of the first one produced a stamp and no correspondence and no letter, and the issuing of the second one did not require anybody to have answered the first one, because the form has no memory.** A form that had memory would be a file, and a file is a place a person can be asked about, and this form was built in about 1961 to be filled in off a roll by a conductor at the end of a run, and it has never once had to remember a quarter, and a thing that is not asked to remember does not.

"So it is nothing," said Cyril Sale, at about eleven o'clock on the Tuesday, in four rooms above a fitter's shop, and he had read both sheets and he put them down and said nothing else for about four seconds, which is how he says most things.

"It is not nothing. It is worse than nothing and it is the same thing as nothing, and they are on the same piece of paper, and that is what is new."

"It is a return." He squared them. "**Two returns in a file is what a file is for. In about four years somebody pulls it for something else entirely and there are two item sixes and one of them is a December morning and one of them is a June morning, and the numbers are not the same number and neither of them is the number this district has been asked for twice.**"

"You could put a figure in either of them in about four minutes."

"I could not put a figure in either of them in about four minutes and you know exactly why, and I have not got the time and you have not got the number." He looked at him for a moment longer than he usually does. "And you have had four chances to ask me for a number I keep and I have never asked you for one and I am not going to, and I would like that noticed by somebody other than me."

---

**And the thing underneath it, which he got to at about four in the afternoon on the eighth and which is the reason there is going to be a date in this district before there is a chair, is this.**

**The number is available.** That is the whole of it and it took seven months to arrive at. A conductor of thirty-eight says the next call out loud about nine hundred times a day, and a hole goes through a piece of paper about nine inches long for every person that gets on, and the paper comes off a machine at the end of a run and goes in a tray, and nobody has ever asked her what is on it because they already have it, and the roll is right and it has never once been wrong and it does not know who any of them are and that is not a defect and she said so before he did and she was right.

The number for the working of 07:52 off Crown Heights on the ninth of June is in a tray in a shed. **The district could answer the second return. It is the easiest thing this district has ever been asked for. It is easier than a name, easier than a figure of people in rooms, easier than a plate, easier than a sentence, and it is in a wooden case in a room that is not locked, and the instrument is not lost and it has never been lost and nobody has to find anything.**

He wrote all of that down and then wrote the next three lines and then stopped for the day.

*And the reason the district is not going to answer the second one is not the line of the twelfth of January, and it is not the shield and it is not the gag, and a woman of thirty-one said in a corridor in May that she did not know which and I have not found out since. **It is this: a number that has been put on a return once is a number somebody will ask for twice, and the second time it is not for the return.** A punch roll is not a return and a return is not a roll, and there is no column on nine inches of paper and there never was and it has never wanted one, and a machine counts it and a person does not. **If this district puts one figure on one line, then in about four years there is a woman of forty-five being asked to produce a roll for a working in a quarter, in a room, by a man with a box, and she will be asked in front of somebody, and that is the day the nine hundred becomes one.** And the standing question is a third one and it has been a third one since January and I have got a punch roll and a man of sixty-three with a flag and now I have got this, and it is still not an answer, and I am not going to write it in a yard, and the next thing I do not know and there is a fourth of the question underneath the third and I have not got to it and I am not going to in June.*

**And the second return has the twenty-fourth of June on it. The twenty-fourth of June is a Wednesday, and it is thirty-four days after the date the first one asked for, and there is nothing on any calendar in this city for the twenty-fourth of June, and the district has a date, and a district that has a date is a district with a chair, and this district has not had a chair since the fourteenth of April.**

He did not tell Mara Quill, because she is a party to a proceeding in a building about four hundred yards from that office and there is no column on a return for a party, and he did not tell Hettie Brann, because a counter is not a repository, and he did not put it in his own book with a date on it, because it has a date on it, printed, on the sheet, in the wrong column.

He told the man of forty-one on no list at all, on Tuesday, at a gate, in nine words, because the man of forty-one is on no list and a man on no list is the only kind of person a thing with no form can be said to.

---

**And eleven men walked six hundred yards at half past seven on the Monday morning, and the condition column was empty and the reason column was empty, and a boy of seventeen went out at ten past eight and came in at about four, and on Wednesday the tenth of June the second side will have its tenth blank on it and the sheet will be right about it, and the grass on the far side of a fence is being cut, and nobody in eleven streets is standing in the weather.**

---

**The Field Book, Tuesday the ninth of June, 22.55, a front room, one day off the eighth, and the second entry is the ninth of June and the first is the eighth and I am writing them in order for once.**

*Eighth, 23.40. One day off the seventh.*

*Ninth, 22.55. One day off the eighth.*

*One. **A quarterly thing arrives twice, and the two of them are in one file in one drawer in one room, and the second one does not know the first one happened, and there is no column on that form for *the same section was not completed last quarter*.** Item six on the December return is the working of the sixteenth of December and item six on the June return is the working of the ninth of June, and the two are the same six boxes and the same four paragraphs and the same item number, and the first is now returned as not stated and the quarter is closed and **a closed return is a thing that is not waiting for anybody, and nobody in this district has been told that the waiting has stopped, and I found that out from a wafer.** The form was built in about 1961 for a conductor to fill in off a roll at the end of a run and it has never had to remember a quarter, and a thing that is not asked to remember does not. **A form that had memory would be a file, and a file is a place a person can be asked about, and this form is not a file and it is not going to become one, and that is the oldest thing in this district and I have now met it ten times in seven months and I have never once been able to make it into a complaint.** A works clerk of forty-six read both sheets at about eleven on the Tuesday and put them down and said *it is a return* and then said the half of it that is true, and he has had four chances to give me a number and I have not asked for one on any of the four and I am not going to and he noticed and I am glad he noticed.

*Two. And the thing underneath it, which is the real escalation and it is not a form at all. **The number is available.** The number for the working of 07:52 on the ninth of June is in a tray in a shed, on nine inches of paper with a hole in it for every person that gets on, and it is right, and it is not lost, and it has never been lost, and nobody has to find anything and nobody has to look for anything, and the woman who puts the holes in it does it about nine hundred times a day and has been asked nothing. **And this district is not going to put it on a line, and the reason is not the twelfth of January and it is not the shield and it is not the gag, and Mara Quill said in a corridor in May that she did not know which and I have not found out since and I am not going to ask her in June.** The reason is: a number that has been put on a return once is a number somebody will ask for twice, and the second time it is not for the return. A roll is not a return and there is no column on nine inches of paper and a machine counts it and a person does not, and if this district answers one item then in about four years there is a woman of forty-five being asked to produce a roll in a room, in front of somebody, by a man with a box. **And the standing question is three parts and the third of them is what is it about an instrument a person cannot produce which makes the thing it cannot produce the most important thing in a district, and I have a punch roll and a man of sixty-three with a flag and now I have this, and I have not got an answer and I have got three things that are not one, and I have not joined any two of them and I am not going to.** There is a fourth part under the third and I have never been down to it and I am not going to in June and I am not going to describe it here.

*Three. And the date. **The twenty-fourth of June is a Wednesday and it is thirty-four days after the date the first one asked for, and there is nothing on any calendar in this city for the twenty-fourth of June, and this district has not had a date since the fourteenth of April.** A district that has a date is a district with a chair, and a chair is a thing a department puts on a list, and the list for the fifth of May was six items long and this district was not one of them. **So the district now has a date about a car and not a chair, and a date about a car is a thing a district can fail, and I have been living in a district that could not be failed at anything for six months and I did not know that was a position and now I do.** I have not told an advocate of thirty-one, because she is a party to a proceeding and there is no column on a return for a party. I have not told a woman of sixty-one, because a counter is not a repository. I have not put it in this book with a date on it, because it has a date on it, printed, on the sheet, in the wrong column. **I have said it in nine words at a gate to a man of forty-one on no list at all, because a man on no list is the only kind of person a thing with no form can be said to, and he did not repeat it and he did not ask me a question about it and he went up the Row.**

*Four. And what has not moved, and I am not going to say all of it, because I have said most of it in five books in a row and the whole of it is in the entry of the twenty-first of May and it has not moved since. **Ten remembered routes, ten, and the ninth crossing's failure to cost one is still unexplained, and I have not crossed since the twenty-second of December, and the tally has nine lines and I have not looked at it since the thirteenth of January and I am not going to look at it in June.** Three things under the brick. The run number has not moved since the twenty-fifth of April and I have not asked anybody about a working timetable since the twenty-fourth of April and nobody has been sent anywhere and the division has not said its sentence twice in the same building because it has not said it once since April. Tern is not found. The three nils about Mireward stand at three and I have not made a fourth and nobody has gone there. The order is suspended and is not discharged and the public name is not on the street name index and the classification stays withdrawn and the removed sentence cannot be restored and a desk has been asked twice and has not answered twice and the matter stands over and is not adjourned and has no date and is on nothing. **The levy is two and four a head a week, in advance, week by week, with no term, for two hundred and fifty-two people in a building with no number, and it is not curable, and the woman of sixty on the second landing is the ninth of twenty-six names and is not named and was not in the room on the fourteenth and is not going to be told anything by anybody in this district.** I have added nothing this fortnight about a woman of sixty-eight on nothing and the count stands at eight and her name is not written in this entry.

*The page home the hundred and ninety-seventh day, blank, four marks in four hands and none of them mine. The token cold, a hundred and eighty-third day, and it is in the inside pocket of this coat and it has not been out of it since the seventh of December and it has done nothing every day of one hundred and eighty-three of them. **And there is no seventh column on a roll about nine inches long and it has never wanted one, and there is no column on a return for the day a figure is of, and there is no column on either of those two things for the person who made it, and I have spent seven months trying to build a column and I have finally found a piece of paper in this city that is complete without one, and it is not a document, and it is not a record, and nobody has ever asked for it, and it will still be complete in four years when the returns are not.*

*And the standing position, which does not improve: **the writer of the map is not the maintainer of anything, and that is a list of about fourteen other people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.**
