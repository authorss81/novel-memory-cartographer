# Chapter 116: A Question Put In A Box

It came on Friday the first of May with the rest of the Friday post and he read it at about ten past eleven, and then he put it face down on his own desk, which is a thing he has watched about nine people in this district do, and he went out to a fence at the low end at ten the next morning and stood about eleven feet from it for an hour and a half.

The paper is the authority's own and it is four paragraphs and there is a heading on it.

> **CAR 214 — LINE THREE. RETURN OF SERVICE PARTICULARS.**
> *From the district office, Crown Heights. Friday the first day of May.*
> *1. This office is closing the return for the quarter ending and the return for the working of 07:52 off Crown Heights on Tuesday the sixteenth day of December last is not complete at item 6, which is the number of persons carried.*
> *2. The roll for that working is accounted for and is not in dispute. The roll carries the count. The roll does not carry the count in figures on the working return, and the working return calls for figures.*
> *3. **The district is asked to state in writing the number of persons carried on the working of 07:52 off Crown Heights on Tuesday the sixteenth day of December last.** The district is the only party to which this office has been able to address correspondence in respect of that working.*
> *4. A reply is requested by the twenty-first day of May. If no reply is received the item will be returned as not stated and the quarter will close with the item standing open.*

**Item six. Four lines deep. It is the sixth box on a form built in about 1961 for a conductor to fill in off a roll, and the roll is right, and the roll is a piece of paper about nine inches long in a wooden case, and on Tuesday a woman of thirty-eight told a man of thirty-one in a cabin that it is the only thing in this city that knows how many people were on that car and does not know who one of them is, and she said that before he could, and she was right, and the letter has just told him the same thing in about a hundred words in the language of a return.**

---

**And at about half past ten on the morning of Monday the fourth of May, in a corridor in Crown Heights, a woman of thirty-one said nine words and then did not say any more about it for six minutes.**

"Nobody has to answer that."

"No."

"And nobody has to not answer it either, and that is the new part, and I want you to hear the new part, because I have been in this job eight years and it has not come up before." She had a folder under her arm and she did not open it. "In January the protection was ours. We asked for it, we knew what it was for, and it was the right size for the thing it was made for, and it cost a woman of sixty from her own name and we paid that on the fourteenth in front of thirty people. **A letter about a car does not know we exist. It is not asking the department. It is asking the district, and the district is the only address there is, and the answer to it is the answer of the fourth of December, and you have just used the line of the twelfth of January as a shield against a tram authority's return, and I am not saying that is wrong, I am saying that this is the first time anybody has used it and I do not know yet whether it is the shield or the gag.**"

"You think it is both."

"I think it has always been both and that January was the first time it cost anything, and I have never said that to you before and I am saying it now, and I would like you to notice that I am saying it in a corridor on a Monday morning about a letter and not in a room about a paragraph, and that is the whole difference between the two and it has taken me eight years and a hearing to be able to say it."

He said nothing for about four seconds.

"I have not told you about the season."

"I know you have not."

"I did not tell you in a corridor at ten past ten on the fourteenth of April and I did not tell you in a room at eleven and I did not tell you at half past one when a line with a condition in it was read out that is yours, and I have not told you in three weeks, and I wrote at the top of an entry on the twenty-first of April that it is not a rule, and it is not a rule, and I have been carrying four pages from an office that signed no name past you for four months and I was right about it four times and on the fourteenth it stopped being right and I have not told you that either, and I am telling you now that I have not told you, which is not telling you, and I would like it recorded that I know the difference between the two sentences and have said both of them."

Mara Quill looked at him for about four seconds.

"I am not going to ask you about it," she said. "**I have a page in a drawer with a coat in it and I have not produced it for four consecutive months and you have not asked, and I have noticed the count from my side and I have not mentioned it, and we are two people keeping a number about each other and the number is the same and neither of us has said it out loud, and that is the most intimate thing that has happened between us and it is a document neither of us has mentioned.** Do not improve on that in a yard."

---

**And at about ten past one on the same Monday, in a yard, a man of fifty-eight with a spade across his knees said nine words to a man of thirty-one about a letter, and he did not ask for the letter and he was not shown it and nobody sent him anything.**

"You have got a date on it."

"I have got a date on it."

"The twenty-first of May." He did not look up. "**Then you have got eleven working days and you have not written anything and you are going to write something on about the nineteenth, and I am telling you now that you are going to write something on about the nineteenth, and I have told you a thing about yourself and I would like you to notice that I got it from nine words on a piece of paper I have not read.**"

"How."

"Because a man who cannot answer does not leave it. A man who cannot answer rings the office and says it cannot be answered and writes *cannot be answered* and it is a figure and the figure is not a lie, because the return asks for a number of persons carried and a number of persons carried is not a sentence and *not stated* is not the same as *no response* and there is a difference between them and it is about four words and I have had about eleven years to learn it and I have never had a use for it." He moved the spade. "I am telling you that you will not use it, because you have got a rule in your head that says do not put a word in a column that wants to know why, and this is not that column, and you are going to let a rule about one column stop you answering a different one, and that is not a rule of mine and it is not a rule of anybody's, it is just what happens to a man who has been careful for six months."

---

**The Field Book, Sunday the third of May, 23.40, a front room, one day off the second, and there is a letter face down on a desk in this building that has been face down since Friday.**

*Third, 23.40. One day off the second.*

*One. **A letter, four paragraphs, from a tram authority's district office, and the third paragraph asks a district to state in writing the number of persons carried on the working of 07:52 off Crown Heights on the morning of Tuesday the sixteenth of December.** The district is the only address there is, which is a sentence about this city's records and not about a car, and the letter is dated the first of May and came on the first of May and wants an answer by the twenty-first, and the twenty-first is in eighteen days, and the fourth paragraph says that if there is no reply the item will be returned as not stated and the quarter will close with the item standing open. **An item standing open. Not a fault, not a nil, not a zero, not a defect, not a query, not a query on line six, and not a form about a person. An open item on a return for a quarter that closed in March, and there is no column in this city for a thing that is open because it cannot be said.***

*Two. And what I have, and it is nearly nothing. **The roll is accounted for and is not in dispute. The roll carries the count. The roll does not carry the count in figures and the return calls for figures.** A conductor fills item six off a roll about nine inches long, and on Tuesday a woman of thirty-eight told me that roll is the only thing in this city that knows how many people were on that car and does not know who one of them is, and she said that before I could and she was right. **So the truth is that this city already knows the number, has had it since the sixteenth of December, and has it on a piece of paper in a wooden case in a tray in a shed, and what is missing is not a fact. What is missing is a man with a pen putting a fact that already exists onto a line that somebody will read in about four years.** And that is the whole of the difference between a roll and a return and I have been in this district seven months and it is the first time I have been able to say it in one sentence and the sentence is not mine, it is a form, and the form is wrong-shaped, and the thing it is wrong for is a number and not a person and that is the first time in seven months I have found a form that is the wrong shape in the direction of a figure instead of the wrong shape in the direction of a why.*

*Three. And the line, and the shield, and the gag. **The answer of the fourth of December is a refusal to supply a schedule of persons and the line of the twelfth of January says that no schedule of persons resident at the withdrawn alignment is to be obtained from the district by that department in that proceeding or in any proceeding while that answer stands, and it stands, and its condition is not the department's, and the department said so in a room on the fourteenth of April in front of about thirty people.** And there is a letter on a tram authority's paper that has never heard of any of it and never will, and the answer to that letter is the answer of the fourth of December, and if I send the answer the answer is a refusal, and if I do not send it the item stands open, and there is no third thing, and I did not choose either of the two and the first of the two was chosen in December by about nine people in a room and the second is going to be chosen by a machine in another building in about eighteen days and it is not going to know what it is choosing. **I have used the department's protection for a tram authority's return in the first time in five months that anybody has used it, and an advocate of thirty-one has told me in a corridor this morning that this is the first time and that she does not know yet whether it is the shield or the gag, and she said she has known since January that it was both and has never said it to me before, and she is right, and I am not going to write the word gag next to it in a book either, and that is going to be a gap in this entry and I am leaving it as a gap.***

*Four. And what I did not say. I did not tell her about the season, for the third week, and I told her that I had not, and she said she was not going to ask, and she has a page in a drawer with a coat in it and has had it for four months and has not produced it and neither of us has asked and both of us are counting and neither of us has said the count out loud, and she said that is the most intimate thing that has happened between us and it is a document neither of us has mentioned. **I am not going to improve on that in a yard. I have written it here and I am not going to use it and I know what I am doing and I have been told by about four people in seven months what I am doing and I have gone on doing it, and the going on is the thing, and I have a diagnosis in this book about a seventh column and this is not a seventh column and the diagnosis does not reach it and I have not got a better one and I am not going to get one this month.** I did not ask her about the seventh of January. That is seven times. I did not ask her about the number on the outside of an envelope. I did not ask her who the fourth holder is.*

*Five. And the item. **The twenty-first of May is in eighteen days and I am going to be asked every working day between now and then and a man of fifty-eight with a spade has told me in a yard that I am going to write something on about the nineteenth and he got that from nine words on a paper he has not read, and he is right, and the reason he is right is that a man who cannot answer rings up and says it cannot be answered and writes three words and the three words are a figure and not a lie.** And I have got a rule in my head that says do not put a word in a column that wants to know why, and this is not that column, and I am going to let a rule about one column stop me answering a different one. I have written that down on purpose, in my own hand, in my own book, on a Sunday night, and it is the first thing I have written in seven months that is a reason for a future act, and I have no idea what I am going to do with it and I want it written down that I wrote it down before the nineteenth and not after.*

*The page home the hundred and sixty-first day, blank, and it is there. Ten routes, ten. The token cold, a hundred and forty-seventh day.*
