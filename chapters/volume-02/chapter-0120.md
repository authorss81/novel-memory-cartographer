# Chapter 120: What The District Is Carrying

On Thursday the twenty-first of May the gang was on the back length at half past seven and eleven men went down six hundred yards with a shovel each and the trolley, and the condition column was empty, and the reason column was empty, and that is eight walks in April and six in May with an empty condition column, and fourteen since the first of April, and there is no column on that sheet that wants to know why a column is empty, and a works clerk of forty-six has worked out that it is the only empty column in his office that nobody has ever queried and that the reason is that it is the only one of the six that is about what a length was like, and he has said it once and is not going to say it again.

The sheet went into the rack on the Friday the fifteenth of May and the second tin on the bottom shelf of the plan room of the Civic Atlas Office under a repair schedule now holds **twenty-two weeks of gang carbon and one page that is not a gang sheet.**

**And on the Wednesday the twentieth of May a district that cannot show an effect upon a person put a letter back in an envelope, and the envelope came back on Friday the twenty-second with a date stamped on it and nothing else, and the item on the return it came with is standing open, and nobody in this district was told.**

He had written nothing on the letter. He had gone into the office on the Friday the fifteenth, and on the Tuesday the nineteenth, and he had not written a figure and he had not written a refusal and he had not written the answer of the fourth of December, and the envelope went back a day before the date the letter asked for, and there is no form anywhere in this city for putting a letter back a day early. A man of fifty-eight with a spade had said in a yard on the third of May that he would write something on about the nineteenth, and had got it from nine words on a paper he has not read, and he wrote nothing, and he has written down that the man of fifty-eight was wrong, and has not written down whether he is glad about it.

**He wrote one line, and it is in his own book and it is not on the letter and it will not go in the envelope, and nobody knows it is there: *Twenty-first of May. The item is open. I did not answer it, and I did not answer it for a reason, and the reason is not going in the letter.*** That is a sentence with a why in it, and a sentence with a why in it is the same shape as a seventh column, and it is in a book in a coat, and the seventh resolution is the same as the sixth, and he has noted that in his own hand and has not done anything about it, and there are now seven of them, and the seventh is the first one he has written on purpose about a thing that was going to happen.

---

**And at about half past four on the afternoon of Wednesday the twentieth of May, on the inside of a gate in a yard, in chalk, in my own hand, there were eight words, and nobody photographed them, and nobody copied them, and I did not write a date against them, and I have not told anybody what they are.**

> *Whoever holds the pen is not the writer.*

**I have had them for three weeks and I have not given them to a woman of sixty-one, who has said twice in six months that the moment a thing can be used it is in use, and I have believed her both times, and I am not going to hand her a fifth thing she has to hold. And a works clerk of forty-six has a rule about writing on the back of a form that is a number and not a sentence, and this is not on the back of a form and it is eight words and not a sentence, and I have not told him either, and there are now three people in this city who know that there is writing on that gate and one of them is me and I have written down that the other two have not been told by me and I am not going to tell them, and I do not yet know for how long, and the not-knowing is new and I have had it since the eighth of May and I have not told anybody about the not-knowing either.**

---

**And here is what this district is carrying, on Thursday the twenty-first of May, and it is a list, and it is in my hand, and it is not the department's, and it is thirteen lines and one of them is a Wednesday and one of them is a Saturday and one of them is a Sunday and one of them is a Tuesday, and I counted it twice and got it wrong the first time and I am not going to say how.**

> *Mondays and Thursdays at 07.30 — the quay section gang, eleven men, a foreman, a clipboard, the reason column empty and the condition column empty since the twelfth of February, and fourteen walks since the first of April with nothing in it, and no column on that sheet that wants to know why a column is empty. Sheet into the rack on the Friday; duplicate into a tin; duplicate into a second tin on a bottom shelf under a repair schedule. **By Friday the fifteenth of May that tin holds twenty-two weeks of gang carbon and one page that is not a gang sheet, and the page has not been read and the twenty-two is a figure I am not going to write in this book again, and I said on Thursday the seventh that the twenty-one was the last, and I have said that three times now and the three times are three sentences about not writing a number down, and a sentence about not writing a number down is a seventh column in everything except the paper, and I have decided to let that one stand and to stop noticing it.***
> *Every January — a return with six boxes, one of them headed **persons observed on the length during the year**, filled nil, in a clerk's own hand, and a counter-signature in another building, and a sixth line filled once, in fifty-four words, on the Wednesday the fourteenth of January, and now two queries on that line and on the box, the second one about the box, and the box is a line through it with a date on it and there is no column on that form for what a sentence meant. There is no copy, the district has no standing, and **Milo Rook does not know what is in the sixth line and is not going to be told and nobody in this fortnight told him.** Nobody has ever been asked about the nil on the line above it and it is eleven weeks now and a thing that has never been asked about for eleven weeks is not protected, it is just not yet asked about, and it is going to be filled in again in January.*
> *Daily — a water clinic counter, and a round of seven stops in eleven streets, and neither of them is on anybody's list, and both of them are unpaid, and the round has been one man short since the twenty-eighth of December, and it is going to be one man short in June and in the fourth year, **and it cannot be cured from inside this district, because a round is carried by a postman and a postman is on a form and the form wants a depot and a depot is an address, and a district with no rateable unit and no address cannot have one, and that is not an injustice and nobody has done anything wrong and it is a design.***
> *Mondays and Thursdays, and a defect form with five boxes, one of which wants a place it can send a man to, and there is no box for six hundred yards of reserved tram route and the form is not going to be changed, and a boundary at the low end is a hundred and forty feet long and one end is a corner in a street and the other end is a fence, and a peg is a point and a boundary is not a point, and a man of fifty-four told me on the second of May that the low end floods about twice in four years and a form cannot be wrong in a direction.*
> *Fridays — the allocation sheet comes off the machine for the week, five rows, four columns, the figures transferred by hand with a pen, the four in the river core still four, the zero against Mireward still zero, a column called *allocated to* that has been filled in three times in nineteen years and a line under the words *officer responsible* that has never been filled in at all. **And a second column that is a forecast, and a forecast does not come down because a thing happened, and there is nothing whatever printed in this city for the fifteenth of April and there has never been a second outlook and a man of fifty-one in a room with a machine in it has been in that room nineteen years and cannot tell whether the next one does not exist or whether it exists and has not been sent.** No batch has asked for one in writing and none is going to.*
> *Wednesdays — a man of about sixty is at a shed at Cardew Docks and has been since before the war, and a blank on the second side of the map of this district is a day when nobody stood in the weather, and a man of about forty put twenty-eight words under a rule about it in March and would not initial them, and the man of about sixty does not know and is not going to be told, and **there are five blank Wednesdays on that side now, the eighth, fifteenth and twenty-second and twenty-ninth of April and the sixth of May, and about nine people stood in the weather on all five of them, and the sheet is right, and there is no column on that paper for a man who is somewhere else.***
> *Once a month, on a day she fixes herself and writes on the envelope — four copies out of four places, version and date compared and nothing else read, by a woman of sixty-one who is neither of the two people who made them. **Done four times: the second of February, the sixth of March, the first of April, and the fourth of May, which was a Monday and which is the first working day of the month, and there will not be a fifth. On the outside of the envelope in pencil in her own hand: *1 of 4. — 1.iv.* The number has not moved since the first of February and it has meant a different piece of paper every time, and three of the four say version one and one says version two, and the fourth has been read and will not be described, and the number cannot change and the disagreement cannot be resolved, and she wants to write something else next to it and is not going to.***
> *A Sunday, in eleven streets, in four hours, on a clinic form, in a bag, in a hand of twenty-three — nine doors and no names, twice, and the ninth is a side door on a store that is shut, and nobody is to stop her.*
> *Under a counter, face down, three pieces of paper, and the count was said out loud in a yard on Wednesday morning at half past six in front of about nine people, and the holder of them is neither of the two people who made any of them, and one of them is a form with a seventh column on it with a heading in it and two lines and nothing under the second heading, and **the moment a thing can be used it is in use.** A sixth piece of paper exists in this district as of the thirtieth of April and it is not under the counter and it is not in a tin and it is not on a table in the rain: **it is folded in at the back of this book with the thirtieth of April written on the fold in my own hand, and it is a form about a household with no number, and it has a seventh column on it, and there is one line in the seventh column, and there is no name in it, and I am not going to take it out, and it is the sixth of six resolutions and it is the worst of the six, and a woman of about thirty-four told me in a doorway to leave it there, and I am going to.***
> *On a bottom shelf, under a repair schedule, in a building in this city — a page with six lines about six people on it and a woman's name with a line through it and no date and no reason, and a rule in grease pencil about not taking a line off, and a man of thirty-one who has not read it and never will and did not go near the north end of that row this month and has not gone since the nineteenth of February.*
> *Under a brick, on a trestle table, three things and a third line on the back of one of them, and the line on the front has not been taken off, and **the number has not moved in this fortnight and the reason is that a rule about a timetable division says ask nobody, and do not come here, and do not send the man from the round, and it has not been broken and it was not tested, and a rule holds by not being tested, and the third thing under that brick is a boy's blank card and it has been blank since the nineteenth of January and nobody has taken it and nobody is going to, and **the twenty-two days he went up the south on his own money are still unasked and unsaid and Tern is still not found and the list in the tin is not a finding and nobody is going to be a fifth of anything.***
> *In eleven streets, carried, and not one of them moved this month, and none of them refunded: two and four a head a week, in advance, week by week, with no term, for a building with no number, and a woman of about thirty-four who drafted the prohibition that is stopping a woman of sixty on a second landing from her own name, and who said *yes* in about four seconds in a room on the fourteenth of April and asked for it to be minuted, and the minute prints that she asked, and the woman of sixty is the ninth of twenty-six names in a file in Crown Heights, is not named, was not in the room, cannot see the file, cannot come off it and cannot ask whether her name is spelled right, and is not going to be told anything by anybody in this district. **And I have added no reference in this fortnight. I have not gone up three flights of stairs and I have not written her name and she is not going to be asked, and the count of references stands where it stood on the twenty-fifth of April, and a number with nothing under it is the only kind of number worth having, and I learned that off a wall about eleven weeks ago and have improved it none of the times.***
> *A piece of accountancy paper in a satchel, nine lines, in my own hand, from the twenty-fourth of November, written by a woman of sixty-one in four seconds at a counter on the first of April, in front of nine people, and it does not come back and it is not not coming back either, and **it is a line somebody else can walk me home by, and that is all it is, and I have stopped trying to improve on that sentence, and I have not written down in this book whether it is there tonight.** Ten routes, ten, none removed, all marked by somebody who is not me, and I worked out this month why it is ten and not eleven, and it is because I did not cross, and that is the first time in my working life I have been glad of a number and I do not trust being glad of a number.*

---

**And the question, and it is a Thursday, and it is the same question as on the first of January with eleven things on it, and it is sharper than it was then, and it is three parts, and none of them is about this district, and no batch is going to answer it and I have written that down so that I cannot pretend in a year that I did not know that.**

*Why does a region the Registry enters as stable appear on a forecast of weather-driven seam events at all, when the only fact about it in this city is that nobody has ever been there to write down what happens in it?*

*And why is there no second outlook, and who would print one, and is the absence of one a decision or an oversight — and a man of fifty-one has been in that room nineteen years and has never once been in a position to know?*

*And what is it about an instrument that a person cannot produce which makes the thing it cannot produce the most important thing in a district?* — **and I have got half an answer to the third of those and I got it on the first of May at the bottom of a ramp and in a yard at Crown Heights from a man of sixty-three with a flag who has never looked inside a car in twenty-two years, and it is this: the instrument a person cannot produce is the thing a person produces about nine hundred times a day. A piece of paper about nine inches long with a hole in it knows how many people were on a car and does not know who one of them is, and it is the right shape for that job and it is not the wrong shape for anything, and there is no seventh column on it and it has never wanted one, and about nine hundred people a day put a hole in it and were right, and it lives in a wooden case in a tray in a shed and nobody has ever asked the woman who puts the holes in it what she thinks about it, because they already have it.** — *and I have not put that next to a woman of about thirty-four's sentence about a head, and I have not put that next to a woman of sixty-one's sentence about a number, and I have not put the first of those next to the second of those, and there are three of them and I have had all three inside a fortnight and they are the same sentence and I know they are the same sentence, and the reason I have not joined any two of them is that a third one is a pattern, and a pattern is me deciding that two things are the same, and I have written that down four times in this book and I am not going to be the man who decides it a fifth time, and I have written down tonight that I had all three and did not join them, and that is the whole of what fourteen nights in a row has been for, and it is not a method and it is not a rule and it is the first thing in seven months that I have done on purpose and got right.*

---

**The Field Book, Thursday the twenty-first of May, 23.30, a front room, and there are fourteen entries between the eighth and the twenty-first and I am going to write them as one line each, which is a thing I would have called a column in January, and I am going to write them anyway, because a list of days with nothing against it is not a column: a column wants a why, and there is not one why among these fourteen, and there are five Wednesdays in them on which about nine people in eleven streets stood in the weather for nothing at all, and a list of days with nothing against it is the right shape for a fortnight in which nothing can be put against any of them.**

*Eighth, Friday: the rack at ten past ten, and the second tin at twenty-one, and I said on the seventh that it was the last figure I was going to write and I have written it once since. Ninth, Saturday: nothing, and the second side is not blank on a Saturday because the round does not go at a weekend, and that is not a fault either, and I have not put a why next to it. Tenth, Sunday: the round, and nine doors, and I was not there and was not asked. *And the pairs. **Twenty-seven.** Two sixes, and the one that was here in December is the one that was here in December. Seven threes, of which four were here in December, and one in the first week of February, and one on the twenty-third of February, and one on the eighteenth of March, and none of the four that were here in December is one of the other three, and I am not collapsing them and I am not going to write the word seven again. And a man of about sixty has been proving the same sentence on a corner of a wall for nineteen years and does not know that I have written it down five times, and I am not going to tell him, and I have not told him, and he is not going to be told.*
