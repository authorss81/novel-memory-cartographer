# Chapter 122: Two Lists And No Mark Between Them

The water clinic is at the water steps and it has a dispensary counter, three consulting rooms and a back stair, and on Tuesday the second of June Milo Rook was in the third consulting room from about nine in the morning until about one, and he did not have an appointment and was not asked for one, and in nine weeks that has happened four times and nobody has made a rule of it.

She is a seam physician and she has been his doctor for longer than either of them has wanted to put a date on. **She reads across.** That is the thing about her that no one in this district has ever said out loud and that everybody in the building has watched her do: she will take a thing out of one department's hand and hold it next to the same thing out of another department's hand and look at the two of them, and she is the one who notices when they do not agree, and she has never once in about nineteen years in post written a word about it in either department's paper.

---

**The two documents were on the desk in the third consulting room on Tuesday morning, and they were both printed lists, and both of them were correct.**

The first was the water authority's *Premises at which treatment may be obtained*, a single folded side, printed in February, and it ran to about nine lines of premises in this borough, and it listed the water clinic on seven days and did not list it at all on a Sunday.

The second was the hospital's own *Sunday and holiday medical cover*, a single side, printed in January, revised in April, and it listed the water clinic on a Sunday morning from ten to one.

"I have had both of these on this desk since February and the February one and the January one have been saying opposite things about the same hour for four months," said Iona Sen, "and I want you to notice something about them before I say it."

"They are both right."

"They are both right," she said. "**That is the part. If one of them were wrong I would have written to somebody in February.** The water authority prints that sheet from a list of premises. The hospital prints its sheet from a list of the places where a doctor is actually on. Two lists, two printers, both correct, and they are about the same hour and they do not agree, and there is no form in this city on which a doctor puts *these two printed lists say different things and I have checked both*. There is a box for a disagreement with a colleague and there is a box for a fault in an appliance and there is nothing at all for a fault in a printing office, and the two of those printing offices are about a mile apart and have never heard of each other."

"Who prints the authority's."

"Off a list of premises that was compiled in about 1839 and has been added to ever since. **And a list of premises is not a list of what is open. It is a list of what exists.** They are not the same and there is no form on which the difference is written down, and everybody in this city knows the difference and nobody has ever had to write it down, because writing it down would mean saying that a place on the list might not be a place, and if a place might not be a place then the whole of the list is a question and the list is a hundred and forty years old and it is printed every year."

"And the Sunday."

"The Sunday is a service the hospital is not paying for, which is why it is not on the authority's sheet, and the authority's sheet is what anybody with a Sunday morning and a bad leg will pick up." She put her thumb on the fold. "**There have been seven Sundays since the middle of April when the round went and somebody on it needed a doctor at ten o'clock, and on two of those seven somebody's mother was at home and waiting, and she was seen, and she was seen because the nurse of twenty-three knew, and the nurse of twenty-three knows because I told her in February.**"

---

**And she said what she did about it, and what she did about it was nothing, and the nothing had a shape and she had worked the shape out in about nine seconds.**

"I wrote both of them in the day book. One line, this morning, the hour and the two sheets, and no mark between them, and that is how I have written everything in that book for nineteen years and I am not going to change it now." She turned the book round so he could see it, and there was one line on the page in her own hand, and there was no mark between the two halves of it, and there was no second line. "**If I go to either of those offices and say the two sheets disagree, then the clinic is a place somebody has asked about. And being asked about is the whole of what this district has been doing for six months, and I have watched what it costs, and I am not going to put a place on this counter that has to be defended.**

"You have two sheets that disagree. I have two sheets that disagree. There are about nine people in eleven streets who would take one of those sheets to a doctor."

"Yes."

"**And what happens in about four years is that somebody in a room produces the authority's sheet, because the authority's sheet is the one in the file, and the file does not contain the hospital's, and the file says there was no Sunday service, and there was, and I will be dead or I will be sixty-eight and the file will be right.** And that is not a tragedy. That is a Friday morning in about four years, and it is not going to hurt anybody, and I have decided that in advance and I have written the two sheets in a book in one line and I am not writing to anybody."

"That is the arrangement and not an answer."

"It is the arrangement and I have made it twice before in nineteen years and it is the arrangement and it is not an answer and I am not going to be one of the people in this district who improves a sentence." She squared the two sheets. "**I will tell you the one thing I have got out of it. A list is not wrong because it disagrees with another list. A list is only wrong when somebody has to act on it and the other one is in a different building. Everything else is two pieces of paper in a drawer, and two pieces of paper in a drawer is a thing that happens to everybody and is not a finding, and I have about nine of those a year and I have never once written one down.**"

---

**And at about eleven o'clock she asked him the thing she has not asked him since January, and it was three words, and he did not answer it, and she did not ask twice.**

"What was that."

"I did not hear the counter."

"The counter has not changed. It has made that noise every day since I came here and it made it at about a quarter to ten this morning when the nurse came through." She wrote something in the day book, and it was not about the counter. "**How long has it gone.**

"It came and it went. It was not there for about four seconds and then it was."

"All right." She did not ask him to find it. She did not ask him to describe it. She did not ask him whether it had a face on it or a place attached to it, and he has been asked all three of those in about six months by three different people and he has answered all three and been worse for it. "**I am not going to ask you to listen for it, and I want you to notice that I have decided that and not asked you, because a person who is asked to find a sound starts looking for it, and looking for it is a thing you do with your hands, and then you have got a routine, and a routine about a sound is the fourth thing.** Do the other one."

"I am not going to do the other one either."

"I know. Sit down, I have four things and one of them is your hand and it is not the hand you think it is."

---

**And at about ten past one, at the dispensary counter, a woman of sixty-one was doing four returns and did not stop.**

She has a satchel and a drawer and three pieces of paper face down under the counter where the count was said out loud in a yard in April, and she has not read them and will not, and the number on the outside of an envelope in a drawer is the same number it has been since the first of February, in pencil, in her own hand, and the envelope is a month old and says the first of April on a piece of paper that was last handled on the Monday.

"I am not going to do a fifth," she said, before he had got the counter out of the way.

"I was not going to ask you to."

"You were going to stand there." She put a return down. "**The arrangement was four and it is four and it is done, and the first working day of this month was Monday and I did not do it and I am not going to, and I have not told anybody I was not going to and you are not going to tell anybody, and it is not in a book and it is not a decision and it is the end of an arrangement between three people and two of them are not talking.**

"And the number."

"Has not moved and is not going to. **I have wanted to write something next to it four times and I am not going to and I have stopped saying that out loud, which is the fourth time I have stopped saying a thing out loud and the fourth one is the one I mean.**" She squared a pile. "Go and do whatever it is you came in for. You did not come in for the returns and you did not come in for the counter and you have been in this building twenty minutes and you have spoken to two women and neither of them has given you anything, and that is a Tuesday and it is not a reward."

---

**The Field Book, Tuesday the second of June, 22.40, a front room, one day off the first, and there is no entry in this book for anything that happened in the third consulting room this morning because nothing happened in it, and I have written that down as a fact and not as a complaint.**

*Second, 22.40. One day off the first.*

*One. **Two printed lists, both correct, both in the same borough, about the same hour, four months apart, printed by two offices about a mile apart, with nothing in this city on which to write that they disagree.** A list of what exists and a list of what is open, and the two are not the same, and the difference is not written down anywhere because writing it down would say that a place on a list might not be a place, and the list is a hundred and forty years old and it is printed every year. That is the shape of the thing and it is the fifth true sentence about a list that this district has been given since January, and the woman who gave it to me is a seam physician and a doctor and a woman who has read across two departments for nineteen years, and she has said that a list is only wrong when somebody has to act on it and the other one is in a different building. I did not put that in a yard and I am not going to and it is hers and not mine and I have improved it none of the times, which is once.*

*Two. And what she did about it, which was one line in a day book with no mark between the two halves, and the not-going-to is hers and not mine and she is right. **A thing I have been doing for seven months, and which is the thing I am good at, is to take a fact out of one building and hold it against a fact out of another building and go and look at the seam, and in seven months I have produced a seventh column out of it four times and a sentence with a why in it twice. She has taken the same two facts and written them on one line and gone back to four things and a hand, and there is no more on the page, and in about four years the file in the other building will be right and she will be sixty-eight.** And I asked her whether that was the arrangement and not an answer and she said it was both and refused to improve on that, and I have written it down and I am not going to say it again, and I notice that I have now written a woman's sentence down in this book four times and improved none of them, and a list of those is a column, and I have not made one.*

*Three. **A sound, and the not-telling of it, and a doctor who did not ask me to find it, and that is a change of method that is hers and not mine and I have taken it without being asked and it cost me nothing and I do not trust it.** I did not hear the counter at about a quarter to ten and it was not there for about four seconds and it was there again, and I have not written down what it was, and it did not come back. It is a sound and it is not a taste tonight and it is not a face and it is not a place and it is not a person and it is not a name, and I have decided nothing about whether the two things I have lost are one thing or two and I decided that in the first week of January and I have not improved on it in five months. **A seam physician in this city has decided that she is not going to ask me to listen for it, on the stated ground that a person who is asked to find a sound starts looking for it and looking for it is a thing you do with your hands and then you have got a routine, and a routine about a sound is the fourth thing.** I did not tell her and she did not ask and that is the first time in six months that a person with a pen in their hand has been in the room with one of these and has not asked, and I have not written down whether it was because she decided not to or because she did not notice and I am not going to, and I have had four days of wanting to ask her which and I did not.*

*Four. And the envelope. **Four comparisons and no fifth, and the first working day of this month was Monday the first of June and nobody did it, and the number on the outside has not moved and is not going to, and she has said in this building this morning for the first time that she has stopped saying out loud that she wants to write something next to it, and that the fourth time she has stopped is the one she means.** Three of the four say version one and one says version two and the fourth has been read and will not be described, and the disagreement cannot be resolved and is not going to be. **She is right and I knew she was right on the fourth of May and I have not asked her a fifth time and I am not going to ask her in June.** Her custody register page is in a drawer with a coat in it and has been for five months and it has not been produced and it has not been named and it has not been mentioned in this book tonight, and I have not asked, and I have now not asked for five months and I have stopped keeping the number of that and started dating it, which is worse, because a date is a column.

*Five. And what has not moved and what I have not done. **Ten remembered routes, ten. The tally has nine lines and I have not looked at it since the thirteenth of January and I am not going to look at it in June, and I said that on the second of June in this book and on the first and on the twenty-first of May and I have improved it none of the times.** The three things under a brick are three and the run number has not moved since the twenty-fifth of April and it moved then because a person who was not sent and not asked moved it. I have not asked a timetable clerk anything and I have not sent anybody and I have not been to Parcel Street since April and the rule holds and it holds by not being tested. The allocation sheet came off the machine on Friday and a man of about fifty transferred five figures out of it with a pen and the fourth column is empty and the line under *officer responsible* is empty and neither of those has been filled in since the twenty-sixth of January and I have not asked him for either and he has not offered.*

*The page home the hundred and ninety-first day, blank, and I am not writing that it is there. The token cold, a hundred and seventy-seventh day, and it was in the inside pocket of this coat on a clinic counter at about a quarter past ten this morning and a woman of thirty-eight was four feet away and did not know it was there and I did not think about it, and that is the third time in four weeks and I am going to stop writing that clause down and I have written it four times and I am stopping now.*

*And the standing position, which does not improve: **the writer of the map is not the maintainer of anything, and that is a list of about fourteen other people and not one of them has been asked to be on it.**
