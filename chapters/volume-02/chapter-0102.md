# Chapter 102: A Continuation Of An Adjourned Matter

The Hall of Wards has a register on a table by the door and it is a bound book and it is not a departmental document, and Milo Rook learned that from a man of about forty-five in a corridor in January and has not tested it since, and about two hundred feet of Crown Heights' worst floor is between the door of that room and the room he is standing in at ten past ten in the morning of Tuesday the fourteenth of April.

"Mr Rook," said Mara Quill, without turning round. "You have not said good morning and you have been standing there for about forty seconds."

"I was going to ask you something and I have decided not to, and I would like to say that first because in this district that is now a thing that gets said."

"You have not said good morning."

"Good morning."

"Now say the other thing."

Mara Quill put her bag down on a bench in a corridor in a public building and squared it, which is not a thing she does and which Milo Rook noticed and has written down in his own book as the first time he had ever seen her square anything.

"In January the district had nothing to say that a minute could hold," he said. "You told me that before I spoke to you about it and you were correct and I did not speak and about four people in this city have said since that I was right not to. **I have had three months to find a better reason and I have not found one and what I have found instead is that there is one thing in that room this morning that is not a document, and I am the only person in it who can say the shape of the thing that is not there.**"

"You are not the only person in it who can say the shape of a thing that is not there. I can say the shape of a thing that is not there. That is most of my job."

"You can say the shape of it as an advocate and I can say the shape of it as the man who has spent five months on both sides of two things in this city that are not on any of its records, and **a thing that is not on any of its records cannot be produced by anybody who has only ever read its records, and if nobody says it in a room then it gets said in about four years by somebody who has not been in this district.**"

"And what will you say."

"I do not know yet."

"Then do not say it yet." She picked the bag up. "And when you do, three things. **Do not put a number on it. Do not put a reference on it. And do not improve it afterwards in a yard, because I will be in that yard.**"

"You would not be able to."

"**I would be able to,**" said Mara Quill, "and that is not a threat, it is a statement about what an advocate is, and I have been in this corridor for eight years and I have watched three good sentences ruined by a man who could not leave them alone, and I am not going to be the fourth thing that happens to yours."

---

**And at eleven o'clock on Tuesday the fourteenth of April a reporting officer of fifty-one and twelve years read out of a book the words that made the room a room, and the words are eleven words long and they had been written on a printed side on the thirty-first of March by a clerk of a list and thrown at a party without a chair.**

> *"Item one. Hearing, district continuity order CO/6/BELL (suspended; adjourned from the twelfth of January past). To sit from eleven. Reporting officer: I. Sarratt, Survey Enforcement."*

"There," said Ivo Sarratt. "That is the sheet of the business of the day, and it is not a notice of hearing and it does not tell you what is being heard, and I am going to say what is being heard, because there is no document in this city that is going to tell you, and if I do not say it then this morning is a sheet of paper being read at a table."

Nobody said anything. About thirty people were in the room and the register was on a table by the door and it had a name in it in ink and a rule under the heading that said a hearing claimed as of right is listed as of right.

"**This is a continuation of an adjourned matter.** It is not a new matter and it is not an appeal and it is not a review and it is not a complaint, and anybody in this room who has come here expecting one of those four things is going to be disappointed and I would rather they were disappointed now than at half past three." He put the sheet down. "The matter is a district continuity order made six years ago in respect of a reach of frontage at the low end of Bridge Street and the service court off it. **The order is suspended and it is not discharged. It has not been varied. It has not been withdrawn. It is suspended on the authority of a letter of the eleventh of December past and it stays suspended and nothing in this room will change that today.**"

"And the three matters," said the chair.

"The three matters are listed for determination and they are not re-argued and I am going to say why in about eleven seconds, and then I am going to take them in order, and the first of them is going to take four minutes and the second is going to take nine and the third is going to take the rest of the morning."

---

**The first matter is the grounds of the making of the order and it took about four minutes and nobody argued it.**

"That matter is not in dispute between the parties and I have it in a file and I am going to read the file's own words back and then I am going to sit down." He read about two hundred words and the last of them were: *"the order was validly made on the then-current information and this department does not contend that it was improperly made, and does not contend that it was properly made either, and the department's position is that the matter is not one that can be determined on the papers before me and no party has asked me to determine it on the papers."*

"That is a strange thing to say in a hearing," said somebody at the back.

"It is a strange thing to say in a hearing and it is the correct thing to say in a hearing and I have had nineteen months of other hearings to find out the difference," said Ivo Sarratt, "and about four of them taught me."

---

**The second matter is the classification of the eleventh, and it is withdrawn, and it stays withdrawn, and nobody in the room asked for it back.**

"It was made correctly on the day it was made and it is not correct now, and the reporting officer confirms both halves of that and the department has not disputed either half." He turned a page over. "The classification is withdrawn and it is not to be relied on by any party. **It stays withdrawn. It is not restored today and it is not going to be restored by this department, and the reason it stays withdrawn is recorded in the department's own finding and I am going to read that finding out because it is the only sentence in this file that anybody in this district has ever been given, and it is four months old and about nine people have read it and one of them is a woman of thirty-four who has been carrying it since the twelfth of January.**"

> *"The classification of the site dated the eleventh of the current year is withdrawn and is not to be relied on by any party. The reason for the withdrawal is that there is now in operation in this city an instrument capable of placing a person upon a reach, in the same city, upon a sheet of paper with a rule at the foot of it, and that instrument did not exist on the eleventh."*

Nobody said anything for about eleven seconds.

"That finding stands," said Ivo Sarratt, "and I have not been asked to revisit it and I have not."

---

**The third matter is the removal of a sentence, and it is the oldest thing in the room and it is the one nobody in Alderquay can produce.**

> *"The removal from the original safety report of the sentence: **no evidence was found of any hazard to persons at the service court.**"*

"This matter has been before this department three times since the seventh of January," said Ivo Sarratt. "I am going to state the department's position in full and then I am going to stop and I am not going to be asked it again today and if I am asked it again today I will give the same answer in the same words."

"The withdrawal reference is not a district reference. It is held in the office of the First Surveyor. It was requested on the sixth of January and acknowledged on the seventh. It was requested a second time on the twentieth of February and acknowledged on the twenty-first. It has not been answered, then or now, and it will not be answered by this department, because **this department does not hold it and cannot account for the removal and has never been able to account for the removal and says so in terms.**"

He put both hands flat on the table.

"The sentence cannot be restored by anybody in this city. I have written that in a minute and I have said it out loud in two rooms and I am saying it out loud in a third. **There is no copy of the report. There is no copy of the sentence. There is no copy of the report with the sentence in it or the report without it. A desk in this city has been asked twice and has not answered twice, and I am the reporting officer and I cannot make it answer, and I am not going to pretend in a room that the department is going to try harder than it has.**"

Somebody at the back said, "Then what is the point of the third matter."

"That is a good question and I am going to answer it," said Ivo Sarratt, "and the answer is that the matter is on the list and the list does not print what is being heard, and that is the fault of a printed side and not of a room. And I have got a second answer, and it is the one I would rather have, and I am going to give it to you because I am fifty-one years old and I have been in this job twelve years and I have concluded that **a matter that is listed is a matter that somebody read.**"

Nobody wrote it down. About four people in that room noticed that nobody had.

---

**And then Milo Rook stood up, and it was the first time he had spoken in a room of this kind in his life, and it was about a hundred and forty seconds, and nobody has put a number on it since, including him.**

"I am not going to say what the department's position is," he said. "I am going to say one thing and it is not evidence and it is not a submission and I am not going to hand it in, and the advocate has told me not to put a number or a reference on it and I am not going to."

"Sir?"

"**This district cannot show your department an effect upon a person, and that is not because nothing has happened to it.**" He did not have a paper in his hand and about four people in the room looked for one. "It is because a name in this city's records is entered against a rateable thing, and a rateable thing is a building with a street, and there are three places inside this city that are inside your boundary and are not rateable things and cannot be given a street and have never been able to be given one.

"There is about six hundred yards of reserved tram route at Alder Quay that came off the running diagram in October and is correct to be off it, and it is kept by eleven men and a foreman with a clipboard twice a week, and it has no name, and it cannot be given one. There is a hundred and ten yards of frontage at Cardew Street that is not a street, and it is kept by about eleven habits and about four people, and it has no address and cannot be given one. And there is a hundred and forty feet of named frontage at the low end behind a fence with a settlement plate on it, and that one has a name and a date and a gang on a fortnightly inspection, and eleven days ago the plate came off its nails and is in the grass on the far side and there is no form in this city that can report a plate."

"You have told me about a plate."

"I have told you about a plate because it is the smallest thing I have got and it is the one I can prove is a plate." He looked at the reporting officer rather than at the chair, which about four people in the room noticed and Mara Quill did not correct. "**All three of them are held by people who are not in any of your records. Your department has correctly declined to enter them in your records. You cannot enter them. There is no box for them. And so this department is relying, and will go on relying, on those places being kept by people whom it cannot name, whom it cannot pay, whom it cannot write to, and whom it cannot ask a question without producing a document that in four years can be produced against a person.**

"That is the district's position. It is not a complaint about your department. It is the reason the district cannot be a useful party, and it is the reason the district is not going to get any better at being a useful party, and I would like it in whatever the department writes down, and I am not going to be asked whether I object to that."

"The matter does not stand with the department as stated," said the chair, mildly.

"**Then I have said it badly, and the advocate will do it again properly, and I have said it out loud in a room, which is what a thing that cannot be made into a document is for.**"

---

**And at about twenty past one the reporting officer read out the line, and it was his own, and he had written it in January with his own name on it and not a department's, and it is the only thing this district has won in six months.**

> *"No schedule of persons resident at the withdrawn alignment is to be obtained from the district by this department, in this proceeding or in any proceeding, while the answer of the fourth of December stands."*

"That line stands," said Ivo Sarratt. "I wrote it in January and I have not been asked to withdraw it and I am not going to be. **It stands while the answer of the fourth of December stands. I have to say that it is a conditional line and that the condition is not mine, and that is a thing a department should not have to say in a room, and I am saying it in a room.**"

Mara Quill said nothing at all.

And a woman of about thirty-four who was sitting with her hands flat on the table in front of her said the next thing, and it was four seconds long, and it is the whole of Chapter 103, and Milo Rook was in the room and did not speak and has written that down.

---

**And at about half past three the chair asked whether there was anything further, and Ivo Sarratt said there was not, and then he said the thing that is not a decision and is not a finding and is the reason a matter is not on a list for the fifth of May.**

"I have no further date to give you."

"Mr Sarratt, the matter was adjourned to this date in January. Are you adjourning it again?"

"**No.**" He said it clearly and he looked at the chair and not at the district. "I am not adjourning it. There is nothing to adjourn it to. I have three matters and two of them are determined in the sense that they stand as they are, and the third is one that this department cannot determine and has told the room it cannot determine. **A department does not set a date for a matter it cannot hear. That is not a decision, and I have not got the standing to make it a decision, and nobody in this room is going to read it as one, and I am recording in terms that the matter remains before this department and is undetermined and has no date, and that is a sentence I have had to learn how to say and I have had it in draft for about three weeks.**"

"Then the matter stands over."

"**The matter stands over. Which is not the same as being adjourned, and I would like it minuted that I have distinguished those two words, and the minute will not distinguish them, and I have asked for it anyway.**"

Cass Ardery said one thing to Milo Rook in the corridor at about ten past four, and it was nine words, and he had asked in January for the date to be minuted and it had been.

"**You asked for a date. There is not one. I am sorry.**"

"I did not ask for a date, Cass. I asked for the day to be a list day so that it would be printed, and it was, and it got us a chair, and the chair is worth about nine of the eleven days I said out loud in a yard on Saturday, and I would do it again."

"Would you."

"I would do the list again and I would not do the other eleven days again, and I have not decided which of the two I am going to write down."

---

**The Field Book, Tuesday the fourteenth of April, 23.05, a front room, and it is four days off the tenth and that is the fifth of the fours in this book, and four of them are in March and one of them is today, and I have written that down and I am not going to look at it again.**

*Fourteenth, 23.05. Four days.*

*One. I stood up in a room. I have written a hundred and forty of those seconds down as nobody has put a number on them including me, and I am going to write them down now as about two minutes, and the reason I am not going to improve on what I said is that a woman of thirty-one told me in a corridor at ten past ten that she would be able to stop me improving it and that I would not be able to stop her, and she was right about that and she has been right about everything since the seventh of January.*

*Two. And the thing I said, and it is the whole of what I have got after six months and it is not a discovery: **this district cannot show an effect upon a person because the department's own records are built out of rateable things and there are three places inside this city's boundary that are not rateable things, and the department is right that it cannot enter them, and it is therefore relying on people it cannot name.** That is not an accusation. A document cannot fix it: the day there is a box for a length, a length is a place with an address, and the day a length has an address it is a rateable thing, and the day it is a rateable thing somebody can be asked what it did to them. **I have got a box in my head that would fix eleven streets and it would cost me the three places that are the only reason this district is a place, and I have known that since the second of February and I have said it out loud for the first time this morning and it went out of my mouth in a room before I had finished the second sentence of it.** And the advocate says the answer is that I have said it badly and she will do it again properly, and I want that read back to me in April: I said a true thing in about a hundred seconds and it was not a submission and it is not a record and it is in nobody's file, and it is the only instrument this district has ever had and it is a mouth.*

*Three. **The reporting officer is a person and he is fifty-one and he has been in the job twelve years and he has told this district three things in one morning that he had to learn how to say, and I have written them down, and the third one is the finding: he has no further date to give and it is not a decision and a department does not set a date for a matter it cannot hear.** I asked for a list day in January, in about nine words, and what I got was a chair and no date, and I have swapped the chair for a date in my own head twice in six months and both times I was wrong, and about four people in this district knew I was wrong before I did.*

*Four. A conditional line. **It stands while the answer of the fourth of December stands, and the condition is not his, and he said in a room that it is not a department's business to have a condition in it, and I have not been able to stop thinking about it since about half past one.** I did not ask. I have not asked her about the seventh of January. I have now not asked her four times in a fortnight and I have written down that the not-asking has stopped being procedure and become something else, and I have still not decided what, and I have decided that I am going to keep not asking until I have decided what it is, and that is not a plan, it is a postponement with a date on it.*

*Five. And the sentence, which is the only thing I have got in this book that is not mine. **I have written that I did not tell her about the season, and that if I went on not telling her after the fourteenth then it was not a rule.** It is after the fourteenth. I have not told her. I did not tell her in a corridor at ten past ten this morning and I did not tell her in a room at about eleven and I did not tell her at half past one when a line was read out with a condition in it that is hers. **So it is not a rule, and I have written that down tonight, in this hand, at the top of this entry, and I am not going to take it back, and I have not decided what to do about it and I have not got a second hand to decide with.** And I have not told her because on the fourteenth of April a man was going to ask what season meant, and I was right, and I was going to be right again, and I have written down that being right is not the same as being a person somebody can plan a Tuesday with.*

*The page home the hundred and forty-second day, blank, and it is there, and I have written down in advance that it will be there on the fifteenth and I have written that down about four times this month and it is the only forward thing in this book. Ten routes, ten. The token cold, a hundred and twenty-eighth day, and it was in the coat on my own front door at about eleven o'clock tonight and the coat is the reason for one or two things that are not in this book and one of them got very close this afternoon and I have written down that it got close and I am not going to write down which.*
