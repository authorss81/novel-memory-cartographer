# Bible

The bootstrap canon is split into focused files:

- `premise.md` — premise, genre promise, boundaries, and starting position.
- `world.md` — Alderquay’s geography, history, factions, institutions, and physical rules.
- `characters.md` — protagonist, supporting cast, wants, flaws, relationships, and arcs.
- `power-system.md` — habit memory, remnant grammars, mapping rules, power stages, costs, and failure modes.
- `themes.md` — thematic questions and the intended emotional answer.
- `terminology.md` — canonical names, grammar categories, institutions, and usage rules.

The series destination is recorded in `outline/series.md` and `outline/ending.md`. No chapter prose has been written during bootstrap.
