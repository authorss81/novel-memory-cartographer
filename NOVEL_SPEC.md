# Novel Specification

Title: The Memory Cartographer
Repository slug: novel-memory-cartographer
Genre: urban mystery / magical places
Male lead: A cartographer who maps places erased from official memory
Length target: 700 chapters
Scope: 10 volumes of 60–80 chapters, set in and around the single city of Alderquay. This deviates from the catalog entry's 14 traveling volumes; see `state/canon-changes.md` CR-001.
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship, decided in the bible phase and now fixed: Milo Rook and Mara Quill. No harem structure.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A cartographer discovers that every forcibly erased street asks for something under rules of its own, drawn from a fixed vocabulary of seven memory grammars. Each case is a new combination, so no street is ever solved by knowing its category.

## Ending
He restores forgotten places without overwriting the lives built after the loss, and pays a permanent personal cost for the privilege of not being the only author.
