# Novel Specification

Title: The Memory Cartographer
Repository slug: novel-memory-cartographer
Genre: urban mystery / magical places
Male lead: A cartographer who maps places erased from official memory
Length target: 700 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A cartographer discovers that every forcibly erased street exists in a different kind of magical memory.

## Ending
He restores forgotten places without overwriting the lives built after the loss.

Bootstrap artifacts complete. The bible, series outline, and ending outline are defined. No novel prose has been generated yet.
